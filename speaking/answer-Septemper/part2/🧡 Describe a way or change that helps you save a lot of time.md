## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a way/change that helps you save a lot of time**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is
> - How you implement it
> - How difficult it is
> - And explain how you feel about the way/change

## 1. Better Version（最新最小改动版）

One change that has helped me save a lot of time is limiting how long I spend scrolling through social media and short videos. I used to do it in bed before going to sleep, especially when I was mentally drained after work. Scrolling felt like an easy way to relax. But I ended up spending one or two hours on it, and then I couldn't fall asleep naturally, especially after seeing those perfect lives online, which made me more anxious and upset. After a series of bad nights, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. So I set a **15-minute timer** and put my phone across the room before going to bed. Setting it up was simple, but sticking to it was quite difficult because once I started watching those short videos, the algorithm kept pushing more of the same. Short videos made it easy to say, “Just one more.” But when I checked the time, another hour had already passed. **Now that little timer really gives me a clear stop point.** It hasn't fixed everything, but my sleep has improved a bit, and I have more time for things I want to learn after work. I'm really glad that I made this change.

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| social medias | social media | 此处不加 s。 |
| some a simple way | an easy way | some 与 a 不叠用。 |
| relax myself | relax | 此处直接用 relax 更自然。 |
| expected it help | expected it to help | expect something to do。 |
| it make | it made / it makes | 过去经历用 made；现在习惯用 makes。 |
| scrolling my phone | scrolling on my phone | 在手机上滑动浏览用 on。 |
| my careers | my career | 此处指自己的职业发展。 |
| those rich life and luxury lives | those perfect lives online | 原表达数与搭配不自然；也可说 rich and luxurious lives。 |
| a series nights | a series of nights | series 后需要 of。 |
| 15 minutes timer | 15-minute timer | 复合修饰语用单数 minute，并加连字符。 |
| am / feels / can / keeps 与过去经历混用 | was / felt / ended up / kept | 过去经历保持过去时；Now 后的当前效果用现在时。 |
| hasn't fixed it everything | hasn't fixed everything | 删除多余的 it。 |
