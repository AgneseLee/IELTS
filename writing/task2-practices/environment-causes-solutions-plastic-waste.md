# The Growth of Plastic Waste

## 题目

Increasing amounts of plastic waste are being produced, polluting the world’s cities, countryside and oceans. What are the possible reasons for this problem, and what can be done to solve it?

## 共用逻辑提纲

- 总体判断：塑料垃圾增加源于公众层面的环保意识不足，以及制度层面的环境立法和监管不足；解决方案必须与这两个原因一一对应。
- 原因 1：缺少环保意识和教育 → 人们难以认识塑料消费与不当丢弃的后果 → 不负责任的消费和处置方式持续，污染城市、乡村和海洋。
- 原因 2：环境政策和立法不充分，同时人口增长推高资源消费 → 塑料生产与使用缺乏有效约束，公众参与也有限 → 塑料垃圾的数量与分布范围扩大。
- 方案 1（对应原因 1）：学校和媒体加强环境教育 → 改善公众对塑料消费、处置及水污染的认识 → 减少不必要的塑料使用和随意丢弃。
- 方案 2（对应原因 2）：政府加强并执行针对塑料生产和处置的环境政策，个人遵守相关规则 → 约束资源浪费并提升公众参与 → 减轻城乡和海洋生态系统的污染压力。
- 结论：教育改变个人行为，立法约束生产与处置；两者共同实施才能形成持久效果。

## 模板最小修改版（320 words，Target Band 7.0–7.5）

The debate surrounding **the increasing production of plastic waste and the resulting pollution of cities, the countryside and oceans** has engaged a broad audience recently. This problem can be attributed to multiple causes, and both governments and individuals can take feasible steps to alleviate it. This essay will examine the main causes of this issue and propose possible solutions.

One major reason behind **the growth of plastic waste** is the lack of **environmental awareness and education**. This shortage makes it difficult for **consumers to understand the consequences of unnecessary plastic use and careless disposal**. **These habits allow discarded plastic to accumulate on urban streets, in the countryside and in waterways leading to the sea**. Another underlying cause is **inadequate environmental legislation at a time when population growth is increasing resource consumption**. **Without effective controls or enforcement, plastic production and disposal remain poorly regulated, while limited public participation reinforces the same pattern.**

A practical approach to addressing this issue is to promote **environmental education through schools and the media**. This is effective because it directly improves **public understanding of responsible consumption and disposal**. **Better-informed consumers can reduce unnecessary plastic use and avoid discarding waste in places where it pollutes land or water.** Another important step is to strengthen **enforceable environmental policies governing plastic production and disposal**. The key reason is that **clear controls can reduce resource wastage while stronger enforcement increases public participation**. As a result, this strategy not only reduces pressure on **urban, rural and marine environments** but also ensures lasting and comprehensive outcomes.

In conclusion, the emergence of **increasing plastic waste** can be traced back to **insufficient environmental education** and **inadequate legislation amid rising resource consumption**. Nevertheless, by adopting effective measures at both governmental and individual levels, including **education through schools and the media** and **stronger, enforceable policies on plastic production and disposal**, its negative impact can be mitigated and the situation can be improved in the long run.
