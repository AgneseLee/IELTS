## Final Integrated Version (7.0-7.5)
**Word count: 294**

Some people argue that the study of ancient cultures is no longer a relevant subject in modern education. I completely disagree with this view, because ancient cultural studies still play a significant role in helping students understand modern society and develop important intellectual abilities.

First of all, studying ancient cultures undoubtedly brings substantial benefits to students. One key strength is that it allows them to understand how political systems, laws and social values have developed over time, thereby building a firm basis for their broader intellectual growth. For instance, studying ancient Chinese history, including the Tang and Han dynasties, can help students understand the complex reasons why a society prospers, declines or reforms itself. It can also show how bureaucratic systems and traditional values still influence contemporary society. Without this historical perspective, students may find it difficult to recognize how current social institutions operate. Therefore, ancient cultural studies contribute significantly to a deeper and more meaningful understanding of contemporary life.

Moreover, the aim of education should not be limited to professional skills. It should also help students develop abstract mental skills, such as critical thinking, analytical ability and cultural awareness. If ancient cultures were excluded from the curriculum, students would lose the opportunity to explore how societies evolved and why different civilizations made different choices. This could have a negative impact on their overall intellectual development and may gradually weaken their sense of national identity. As a result, excluding ancient cultural studies would make education narrower and less complete.

In conclusion, although modern education increasingly focuses on practical and technical skills, the study of ancient cultures remains essential. It helps students understand the roots of modern society and develop intellectual abilities. For these reasons, I strongly disagree with the view that ancient cultures are no longer relevant in modern education.
