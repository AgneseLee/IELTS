## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a home that you like to visit but do not want to live in**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - What it is like
> - Why you like to visit it
> - And explain why you would not like to live there

> Bank: B7 | Modules: high-rise-home / city-view / daily-comfort

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I compared the apartment's attractive view with its everyday drawbacks and identified what I actually need from a home.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella's apartment in a high-rise residential building in central Beijing.
- Background: The building is near the office skyscraper I visit for meetings, so its glass exterior and bright lobby feel familiar.
- Main event: We drink coffee by the window and enjoy the city view, but the packed lifts and poor ventilation make daily life less comfortable.
- Ability shown: I looked past the view and considered how the apartment would feel on an ordinary weekday.
- Reflection: The visit helped me understand that fresh air and easy access matter more to me than living high above the city.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

The home I enjoy visiting is Stella's apartment in<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;"> a high-rise residential building</mark> in central Beijing. It is near the office tower I visit for meetings, so the area feels familiar. The building has a glass exterior and a bright lobby. Her apartment is on an upper floor with a wide city view and plenty of natural light. I visit every few months. We usually make coffee, sit by the window, and talk. However, the lifts are often packed at rush hour. The ventilation isn’t very good, so the rooms sometimes feel a little <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">stuffy</mark>.

For a short visit, the apartment feels <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">bright and comfortable</mark>. But living there every day would be different. Waiting for crowded lifts would get tiring, and the stuffy rooms would bother me over time. I also feel more comfortable living <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">closer to the ground</mark>. That is why I enjoy visiting but would not move there.

The place helped me think more carefully about what I need from a home. A beautiful view is nice, but fresh air and easy access matter more to me. Now, when I look at an apartment, I imagine <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">a normal weekday</mark> instead of judging it from photos. That simple habit helps me make more <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">practical choices</mark>.
