# Do Multinational Companies and Globalization Benefit Everyone?

## 题目

The spread of multinational companies and the resulting increase in globalization produce positive effects for everyone. Do you agree or disagree with this statement?

## 共用逻辑提纲

- 立场：完全不同意。“对所有人都有积极影响”过于绝对；全球化虽能促进整体经济增长和就业，但收益并非均匀分配。
- 主体段 1（两组负向模板）：
  - 负向 1：全球化使不同价值观和生活方式直接接触 → 增加文化冲突风险 → 扰乱部分本地文化并削弱社会和谐。
  - 负向 2：贸易自由化和跨国公司扩张使弱势经济体更加依赖强势经济体 → 加剧经济不平等，并导致部分行业失业。
- 主体段 2（一组正向模板＋一组让步反驳模板）：
  - 正向：跨国公司扩张使各国经济联系更紧密 → 推动全球经济增长 → 提供就业并支持较贫穷国家的发展。
  - 让步反驳：这些收益若建立在经济依赖之上便难以持续 → 不平等与部分行业失业会抵消整体增长，因此并非人人受益。
- 结论：跨国公司和全球化可能带来总体收益，但文化冲突与不平等分配证明它们不会使每个人受益。

In summary, I totally disagree with **the claim that the spread of multinational companies and globalization benefits everyone**. Taken together, these considerations clearly demonstrate why my position should be regarded as the more convincing one.

The debate surrounding **the expansion of multinational companies and the globalization it accelerates** has engaged a broad audience recently. From my perspective, I totally disagree that **these developments benefit everyone, because their cultural and economic costs are unevenly distributed**.

It is important to recognize that **rapid globalization** can produce harmful effects on **local cultures and social harmony**. A central problem is that **it brings different values and lifestyles into direct contact**, which **increases the risk of cultural conflict in some communities**. As a consequence, **global interaction may disturb local cultures and reduce social harmony**, instead of delivering **benefits to every group**. Another significant drawback of **globalization driven by multinational corporations** is its tendency to **increase economic dependency**. **Trade liberalization and corporate expansion connect national economies more closely, but they can also make weaker economies dependent on stronger ones.** **This dependence may exacerbate economic inequality and lead to job losses in certain sectors, meaning the gains cannot reach everyone.**

However, opponents may argue that **multinational expansion and globalization drive global economic growth**. **By connecting national economies more closely, they can provide job opportunities and support development in poorer countries.** Nevertheless, this advantage is short-lived **for some groups**, as the initial **growth in employment and economic activity** **can fade** once **weaker economies become dependent on stronger ones**. More importantly, these **gains may conceal** **economic inequality and job losses in certain sectors**. **As these problems accumulate, they can offset overall growth and prevent its benefits from being distributed equally.**

In summary, I totally disagree with **the claim that multinational companies and globalization benefit everyone**. On the one hand, **they may create cultural conflict and economic dependency**. On the other hand, **their economic and employment gains may be uneven and short-lived**. Taken together, these considerations **show why overall growth does not guarantee benefits for every community or worker**.
