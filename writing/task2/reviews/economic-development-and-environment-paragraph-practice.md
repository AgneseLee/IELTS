## Original Draft(215)
First of all, economic development undoubtedly brings substantial benefits, including creating job opportunities, increasing personal income, and improving public infrastructure. One key strength is its ability to create more job opportunities,  thereby building a firm basis for higher individuals quality of life. Furthermore, more decent income often comes from better job prospect as companies generate better outcomes. Meanwhile, much public infrastructure, such as network and transport systems, can be renovated, making it easier and more efficient for people daily use. As a result, such an overall improvement in all aspects of life can contributes to sustainable long-term benefits.

Nevertheless, economic development can produce harmful effects on environment if overexploitation of resources happens. A central problem is that a lopsided emphasis on economic growth may ignore the significance of natural protection. Governments may overexploit the natural resources, such as coal and land, and develop new facilities without proper maintenance. As a result, the ecosystem within the area suffers from habitat loss and the overall environmental condition deteriorate, making it derelict and withering. Yet a viable solution is appropriate regulations. Regulators should introduce clear accountability rules and punish companies when companies harm the environment. Finding a balance between economic development and exploitation helps assure residents that economic improvement will not come at the expense of ecological deterioration.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 三项经济好处清楚，但 `create more job opportunities` 在开头两句重复。补明“获得稳定收入”，才能解释生活质量如何提高。
- [TA] `better job prospect as companies generate better outcomes` 未解释企业表现如何转化为工资。可展开为“企业扩张 → 用工需求增加 → 为吸引员工而提高工资”，并保留 `may`。
- [TA] 基建升级缺少经济增长提供资金的环节；补入税收收入即可，属于展开原有基建论点。
- [TA] `without proper maintenance` 指设施维护，不直接解释开发时的栖息地损失。最小调整版改为 `without proper environmental safeguards`，并明确采矿、建设与栖息地破坏的关系。
- [TA] 是否需要解决方案取决于完整题目。本次保留你已有的监管思路，不把缺少引言、结论作为段落练习的扣分项。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] `Furthermore / Meanwhile / As a result` 基本恰当，但连接词无法代替因果解释。第一段末尾的 `all aspects of life` 超出前文支持范围，应回扣收入和公共服务。
- [CC] 从 `Governments may overexploit` 跳到处罚企业，责任主体变化突然。改为政府允许企业进行开发，使监管对象与致害主体一致。
- [CC] 监管需要说明规则要求什么、处罚如何改变企业行为。明确资源开采与栖息地义务，并补充经济激励。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `more decent income` → `higher incomes`；`decent` 强调收入足以维持体面生活，并非直接表示收入更高。
- [LR] `better outcomes` 过于宽泛；表达版用 `perform better`，最小调整版进一步解释企业扩张和用工需求。
- [LR] `natural protection` → `environmental protection`；`network` → `communications networks`；基础设施升级用 `upgraded` 比 `renovated` 更合适。
- [LR] `derelict` 常形容废弃失修的建筑或土地；`withering` 通常描述植物枯萎，不能自然概括整个生态系统的状态。
- [LR] `at the expense of X` 表示牺牲 X。应牺牲的是环境这一有价值的对象，不能写 `at the expense of ecological deterioration`，否则语义方向颠倒。
- [LR] ⚠ recurring：`companies when companies` 可改成 `companies that`，笔记已有连续重复 companies 的问题。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] ⚠ recurring：`higher individuals quality of life` → `a higher quality of life for individuals`；笔记已记录 quality of life 与所有格问题。
- [GR] `can contributes` → `can contribute`：情态动词后接动词原形。
- [GR] ⚠ recurring：`the overall environmental condition deteriorate` → `the overall environmental conditions deteriorate`，或单数 `condition deteriorates`；笔记已有主谓一致问题。
- [GR] `on environment` → `on the environment`；`better job prospect` → `better job prospects`。
- [GR] `for people daily use` → `for people to use in daily life`，也可写 `for people's daily use`。
- [GR] `much public infrastructure` 本身并非可数性错误，infrastructure 不可数；这里删除 much 是为了自然表达，不能误判为必须改 many。

## Final Integrated Version(188) (7.0–7.5)
First of all, economic development can improve living standards by creating jobs, raising incomes and supporting better public infrastructure. As businesses expand, they hire more workers, allowing more households to earn a stable income. Greater demand for labour may also encourage employers to offer higher wages to attract staff. Meanwhile, increased tax revenue can enable governments to upgrade communications networks and transport systems, making everyday activities and travel more convenient. Together, higher household incomes and more efficient public services can produce lasting improvements in quality of life.

Nevertheless, these gains may come at an environmental cost if economic growth depends on excessive resource exploitation. Governments that prioritise growth may permit intensive coal extraction and land development without <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">adequate</mark> environmental safeguards. Mining and construction can then destroy natural habitats and damage local ecosystems. To address this risk, regulators should establish clear obligations for companies to limit resource extraction and protect habitats, backed by penalties for non-compliance. Enforcing these rules would give businesses a financial incentive to prevent environmental damage. Economic development can therefore support better living standards over the long term if it is accompanied by effective environmental protection.
