## Dynamic-template Version(181) (Estimated Band 7.0–7.5)

The line graph illustrates how the percentages of Internet users in the USA, Canada and Mexico changed between 1999 and 2009.

Overall, all three figures increased, while Canada experienced the fastest growth. The USA and Canada reversed their relative positions over the period, whereas Mexico remained considerably lower. By 2009, Canada recorded the highest proportion, followed by the USA and Mexico.

Looking first at the two higher figures, Internet use in the USA rose from approximately 20% in 1999 to around 40% in 2001, before reaching about 70% in 2005 and 80% in 2009. Canada followed a stronger upward pattern. Its figure climbed from roughly 10% to 30% during the first two years, overtook the American figure by 2003 and reached almost 100% at the end of the period.

By contrast, Mexico's figure remained the lowest throughout, although it also increased steadily. The proportion rose from only about 5% in 1999 to 10% in 2001, then reached just over 25% in 2005. By 2009, approximately 40% of the Mexican population used the Internet, around half the corresponding figure for the USA.
