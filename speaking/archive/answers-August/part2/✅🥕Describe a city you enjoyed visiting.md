## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I used public transport, tried unfamiliar food, and adjusted easily between modern and traditional parts of Kuala Lumpur.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Kuala Lumpur, the first city on my Malaysia trip with Stella.
- Background: We took a ten-day Malaysia trip last year and spent the first three days in Kuala Lumpur.
- Main event: We explored a museum, food streets, and the city centre by public transport.
- Ability shown: I adapted to unfamiliar food, transport, and a multicultural environment.
- Reflection: The city broadened my idea of what a modern Asian city can feel like.
