## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a person who likes to make things by hand (e.g. toys, furniture)**

> **You should say:**
> - Who this person is
> - What kinds of things this person makes
> - How you know this person is good at making things
> - And explain why you admire this person's skills

## 1. Band 7 Answer（最新最小改动版）

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I lay in bed and couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. So that's why I talked to my best friend Stella and asked her for help.

She's the kind of person who likes making things by hand. One of her hobbies is making personalized journals. She often spends a whole weekend drawing weekly layouts, adding little illustrations, and putting photos and tickets on the pages. She really enjoys it. Once she showed me a page about her family memories, and she also showed me one about our trip to Malaysia last year. That's when I realized how good she was at it.

What I admire most is not just how beautiful her journals look, but how she slows down, takes her time, and really immerses herself in the process. When she's doing this kind of craft, she seems to lose track of time. I think that's really charming and impressive. I really admire that quality in her because it's something I think I need to learn too, especially when I'm stressed or worried about my future.

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| She's kind of person | She's the kind of person | 冠词：the kind of person who… 是这里的自然结构。 |
| our Malaysian trip we took last year | our trip to Malaysia last year | 表达冗余；直接用 trip to + 地点。 |
| how fascinating, fancy or beautiful her personalized journals look | how beautiful her journals look | 自然度：避免堆叠形容词；not just how…, but how… 本身正确。 |
| immerses herself completely in her journals | immerses herself in the process | 原表达可理解；强调专注制作过程时 in the process 更自然。 |
| When she making that kind of handwork | When she's doing this kind of craft | 进行时缺少 is；handwork 可以使用，此处 craft 更自然。 |
| I think that was… / I really admired… | I think that's… / I really admire… | 持续至今的感受用现在时更一致。 |

> 核对说明：原回答已经用了 slows down；没有 slow downs，也没有需要删除的 about。这些不计为本次错误。
