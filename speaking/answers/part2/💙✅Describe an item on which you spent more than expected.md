## Cue Card

**Describe an item on which you spent more than expected**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is
> - How much you spent on it
> - Why you bought it
> - And explain why you think you spent more than expected

> Bank: B5 | Modules: airport-headphones / urgency / price-comparison

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I worked out why I had overpaid and changed how I prepare for trips and replace equipment.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: A pair of noise-cancelling headphones from an airport shop.
- Background: My old headphones stopped working just before a long flight.
- Main event: I paid about 2,000 yuan, then found the same model online for around 1,500 yuan.
- Ability shown: I identified the cause of the mistake and made a practical prevention plan.
- Reflection: The item was useful, but buying under time pressure cost me an extra 500 yuan.
