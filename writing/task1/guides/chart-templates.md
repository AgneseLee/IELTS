# IELTS Writing Task 1：图表通用模板

适用：bar chart / line chart / table / pie chart

## 核心判断

先看是否有时间变化，再看图表类型。

- 静态图：具体数值 + 排名/倍数/差距
- 动态图：趋势 + 起点/终点/峰谷
- 通用逻辑：特征/趋势 + 数据证据 + 比较

## 固定四段

1. Introduction：改写题目
2. Overview：两个主要特征；不写具体数据
3. Body 1：同类数据
4. Body 2：对比组或例外

## 静态母模板

**1. Introduction**

> The [chart] compares [metric] across [groups] in [place/year].

**2. Overview**

> Overall, [dominant distribution/ranking], while [main similarity/contrast].

**3. Body 1 — choose one data sentence**

> Looking first at [group 1], ...

- Percentage: `A accounted for X%, followed by B at Y%.`
- Absolute value: `The figures for A and B stood at X and Y, respectively.`
- Add: `[Comparison].`

**4. Body 2**

> By contrast, E and F stood at X and Y, respectively. [Final comparison/exception].

## 动态母模板

**1. Introduction**

> The [chart] illustrates how [metric] changed between A and B.

**2. Overview**

> Overall, [dominant/common pattern], while [main exception/contrast].
>
> By [end year], [final ranking/reversal/convergence].

Overview slots:

- `dominant/common pattern`：多数类别的方向或稳定性
- `exception/contrast`：最明显的不同走势
- `final result`：终点排名、反转或收敛；仅动态图需要

**3. Body 1 — rising/similar group**

> Looking first at [group], X rose from A to B. Y followed a similar pattern, reaching C.

**4. Body 2 — contrast/exception**

> By contrast, Z fell from A to B, whereas W remained stable at around C. [Peak/low/reversal].

## 四类图适配器

- **Bar chart**
  - Introduction: `The bar chart compares...` / `illustrates how... changed`
  - Expressions: `the figure/number/spending for`
- **Line chart**
  - Introduction: `The line graph illustrates how... changed`
  - Expressions: `rose / fell / fluctuated / peaked at`
- **Table**
  - Introduction: `The table compares...` / `shows changes in...`
  - Expressions: `the figure/share for`
- **Pie chart**
  - Introduction: `The pie chart shows the distribution of...`
  - Expressions: `accounted for / represented / comprised`

- Bar chart、table、pie chart：可能静态，也可能动态
- Line chart：通常动态
- 混合图：按数据关系分段，不按图形机械分段

## 安全比较句

- `A accounted for X%, followed by B at Y%.`
- `The figure for A was twice that for B.`
- `A and B stood at X and Y, respectively.`
- `A attracted X participants, compared with Y for B.`
- `A outnumbered B by X.`
- `The figures for A and B were relatively close, at X and Y, respectively.`

- `twice`：只用于精确倍数
- `outnumber`：主语和宾语必须是人或群体
- `respectively`：前后必须一一对应

## 安全趋势句

- `X rose/fell from A to B between [year] and [year].`
- `After peaking at A in [year], X fell to B.`
- `X bottomed out at A before recovering to B.`
- `X fluctuated throughout the period, ending at A.`
- `X remained stable at around A.`

### 排名反转

- 概括：`A and B reversed their relative positions over the period.`
- 展开：`A was higher than B at the beginning, but B overtook A by the end.`
- Overview：`Overall, A and B reversed their relative positions, with A initially higher but B taking the lead by the end.`

## 写作流程

```text
主体句 → 数据句 → 比较句
```

写前判断：

1. y 轴：`number / figure / proportion / spending / allocation / ownership / rate of change`
2. 时态：已结束年份用过去式；预测用 `is/are predicted to...`
3. 分组：同向一段；对比或例外一段

## 表达限制

- `in stark contrast`：最多一次
- `respectively`：必须一一对应
- `slightly/marginally`：不用来描述超过 15% 的变化
- 避免：`readily apparent`、`equally noteworthy`、`transformed from a mere...`
- 一句含两个以上趋势动作：优先拆句
- Overall：只写整体趋势、排名或最大对比；不写具体数字
- 只描述图中信息；不推断原因、偏好或影响

## 90 秒检查

- 数据名词是否匹配 y 轴？
- `to / by / at / from...to...` 是否正确？
- 时态、主谓、单复数是否一致？
- 每句是否有明确主语和完整谓语？
- `which` 是否紧邻其指代对象？否则拆句
- 比较对象是否同类？
- 是否遗漏最高、最低、总体趋势或明显例外？
