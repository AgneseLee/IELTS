## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe an important decision that you made**

> **You should say:**
> - What the decision was
> - How you made your decision
> - What the results of the decision were
> - And explain why it was important

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / daily-routine / career-recovery

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I analysed a career setback and turned it into a practical plan for retraining and job searching.
- Secondary Ability: Learning Ability
- Reason: I committed to developing AI skills through regular study and small projects.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: I decided to rebuild my software career after a layoff.
- Background: Losing an eight-year role damaged my confidence and sense of direction.
- Main event: I sought advice, created a routine, and began learning AI tools.
- Ability shown: Informed decision-making and continuous learning.
- Reflection: The decision changed an uncontrollable setback into a direction I could influence.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The important decision I made was to rebuild my software career after I was laid off from a role I had held for years. At first, the setback damaged my <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">confidence</mark>, and I <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">avoided making plans</mark>. After reading a psychology book and talking with my mother and former colleagues, I compared several options. I decided to remain in technology but update my AI skills. I created a weekday <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">routine</mark>, completed structured lessons, built small projects, and continued applying for suitable roles. I learned that one employer's decision does not define my value and abilities develop over time. I still face <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">uncertainty</mark>, but now have clear evidence of progress. This personal turning point gave me <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">responsibility</mark> for my future direction without pretending the setback was easy.
