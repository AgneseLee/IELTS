## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a person you know who really likes taking photos**

> **You should say:**
> - Who the person is
> - When and how you got to know him/her
> - Where he/she takes photos
> - And explain how you feel about him/her

## 1. Band 7 Answer（最新最小改动版）

About three months ago, I had a problem falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I lay in bed and couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. So that's why I talked to my best friend Stella.

She's the kind of person who really likes taking photos. We met about three years ago when I moved to Beijing, and we worked on the same software project. We had a lot in common, so we gradually became close. One night, I invited Stella and a few of our friends to my apartment in Beijing for a catch-up. Stella brought her camera and took lots of photos of us. With her guidance, we tried some great and funny poses. Anyway, we had a blast that night, and it really lifted my mood and helped me relieve some of my stress.

Besides taking photos, the four or five of us just lay on the sofa talking about literature, movies, music, and anything we were interested in—not money, cars, or houses. Then our conversation drifted into what had been happening in our lives and at work, and we exchanged ideas with each other. Stella comforted me by telling me that “slow is fast,” not to push myself too hard, and that I could ask for help if I needed it. She said she would always stand by me. I felt really understood and supported when I heard that. That night, even my sleep problem improved a little, thanks to what she told me.

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cannot stop | couldn't stop | 叙述过去经历，时态保持一致。 |
| She's kind of person who like | She's the kind of person who likes | 补 the；who 指代单数 person，动词用 likes。 |
| Because we had a lot in common, so we gradually became close. | We had a lot in common, so we gradually became close. | because 和 so 不在此结构中重复使用。 |
| At that night / at that night | That night / that night | that night 作时间状语，不加 at；两次出现合并记录。 |
| took her camera | brought her camera | 表示把相机带到聚会现场，用 brought。 |
| took some photos for us | took some photos of us | of us 明确我们是拍摄对象；for us 强调替我们拍，不一定错。 |
| got several great poses and funny poses | tried some great and funny poses | 摆姿势可用 try poses / strike poses。 |
| our recent life | what had been happening in our lives and at work | 多人各自的近况用 lives；recently 可修饰发生的事情。 |
| each others | each other | 固定表达不加 s。 |
| Stella comfort me | Stella comforted me | 过去发生的安慰行为用过去式。 |
| Don't pushing myself so hard and you can ask for help if you need. | not to push myself too hard, and that I could ask for help if I needed it | 祈使句用 don’t + 动词原形；转述建议用 told me not to，并统一人称和时态。 |
| She will always stand by my side. | She said she would always stand by me. | 与过去的转述衔接；stand by my side 本身可用。 |
| heard about it | heard that | heard that 指听到她刚说的话；heard about it 通常指听说某事。 |
| my sleep problem alleviated a bit | my sleep problem improved a little | alleviate 在此通常用被动 was alleviated；improved 更口语。 |

> 核对说明：原回答用的是 literature，没有 literatures，不计为错误。three or five of our friends → the four or five of us 是所提供修改版对人数与范围的调整，不是单纯语法纠错；本次照所提供版本保存。
