Overall, income spent on food saw a dramatical decline from 1968 to 2018, which stood in stark contrast to leisure soared almost triple over the period. Consequently, the proportion of leisure account for the lion's share by replacing food expenditure by 2018.

Regarding the categories with upward trending, such as leisure, housing, and transport, all of them rose considerably from 9% to 22%, 10% to 19%, and 8% to 14%, respectively.

Conversely, the allocation for food, the most descreased one among all categories, drop substantially from 35% to approximately 17% over the course of period. This was followed by clothing and fuel, falling maginally by 5% and neligibly by 3%, respectively. Furthermore, household goods maintained its original share throughout the period.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 引言把图表写成 `the distribution of families' weekly income`，但图中比较的是收入用于不同项目的**比例**，应写成 `the percentages of weekly income spent on different categories`。
- [TA] Overview 抓到了 food 与 leisure 的主线，但 `leisure soared almost triple` 夸大了 9%→22% 的变化；准确表达是 `more than doubled`。
- [TA] 正文漏掉了 `personal goods`（约 8%→4%），并把 fuel and power 的降幅写成 3 个百分点；实际约为 6%→4%，即下降约 2 个百分点。
- [TA] `household goods maintained its original share` 的数据判断是正确的，但主语宜改成 `spending on household goods`。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 按“上升组—下降/稳定组”组织正文是合理的，无需推翻原有逻辑。
- [CC] `which stood in stark contrast to leisure soared` 结构残缺，`to` 后不能直接接完整谓语。可写成 `whereas the share spent on leisure more than doubled`。
- [CC] `This was followed by clothing and fuel` 容易被理解为排名关系，实际想表达的是同向下降；直接写 `Clothing and footwear halved..., while fuel and power edged down...` 更清楚。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] ⚠ recurring `families' weekly income` → `weekly household expenditure` / `the share of weekly income spent on...`：必须区分收入本身与收入的支出分配。
- [LR] ⚠ recurring `allocation for food` → `spending on food`：`allocation` 更常用于政府或机构预算，家庭消费用 `spending on` 更自然。
- [LR] ⚠ recurring `dramatically / marginally / negligibly` 与实际幅度不匹配：35%→17% 可用 `fell dramatically`，10%→5% 应用 `halved`，6%→4% 可用 `edged down`。
- [LR] `the most descreased one` → `the category that saw the steepest decline`；同时避免 `descreased`、`maginally`、`neligibly` 等拼写错误。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] ⚠ recurring 全文描述过去数据，应保持一般过去时：`account for` → `accounted for`，`drop` → `dropped`。
- [GR] `a dramatical decline` → `a dramatic decline`，形容词修饰名词。
- [GR] `categories with upward trending` → `categories that increased` / `categories with an upward trend`，避免词形误用。
- [GR] `over the course of period` → `over the course of the period`，可数单数名词需要限定词。
- [GR] `soared almost triple` → `more than doubled` 或 `rose to almost two and a half times its original share`；倍数不能直接作 `soar` 的补语。

## Final Integrated Version(177) (7.0–7.5)

The bar chart compares the percentages of weekly income that families in one country spent on eight categories in 1968 and 2018.

Overall, household spending priorities changed markedly over the 50-year period. Food, which was by far the largest expense in 1968, lost much of its share, whereas leisure became the leading category in 2018. Housing and transport also gained importance, while spending on household goods remained stable.

Among the categories that increased, leisure more than doubled from about 9% to 22%. Housing also rose substantially, from 10% to approximately 19%, while the figure for transport climbed from 8% to around 14%. Thus, leisure and housing both exceeded food by the end of the period.

By contrast, the proportion spent on food fell dramatically from 35% to roughly 17%, the steepest decline shown. Clothing and footwear halved from 10% to 5%, while personal goods also dropped by half, from around 8% to 4%. Fuel and power edged down from approximately 6% to 4%. Household goods was the sole stable category, accounting for about 8% in both years.
