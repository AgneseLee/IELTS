## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a new law you would like to introduce in your country**

> **You should say:**
> - What law it is
> - What changes this law has
> - Whether this new law will be popular
> - How you came up with the new law
> - And explain how you feel about this new law

> Bank: B8 | Modules: construction noise / light pollution / sleep

## 1. Band 7 Answer (minimum changes)

The law I'd like to introduce is stronger control on late-night construction noise and excessive light pollution. The reason why I want to introduce this law is strongly related to my own experience. About three months ago, my company went through a layoff, and I suddenly received a much heavier workload. Because of that, I started sleeping badly at night. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my career, my future, and whether AI might replace all of our jobs. Things like that kept bothering me throughout the night. So that's why I want to introduce stronger control on these two kinds of pollution. During the night, when I couldn't fall asleep, the late-night construction noise made it even harder for me to fall asleep naturally and made me even more anxious and frustrated. On top of that, when I realized that I probably couldn't fall asleep and might stay up all night, the harsh light outside just made me more anxious and nervous. It was like torture, and sometimes it even made me feel desperate. So I think this law would be popular if it could be introduced successfully, because it would not only protect the environment but also improve people's well-being in the long run. After all, a lack of sufficient sleep can cause huge problems. For example, it may make it difficult for people to stay focused during the day and undermine their overall well-being. So overall, I think most people would support this law, and I would be happy if it could be introduced.
