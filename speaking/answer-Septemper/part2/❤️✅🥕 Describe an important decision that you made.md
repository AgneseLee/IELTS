## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe an important decision that you made**

> **You should say:**
> - What the decision was
> - How you made your decision
> - What the results of the decision were
> - And explain why it was important

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / daily-routine / career-recovery

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I analysed a career setback and turned it into a practical plan for retraining and job searching.
- Secondary Ability: Learning Ability
- Reason: I committed to developing AI skills through regular study and small projects.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: I decided to rebuild my software career after a layoff.
- Background: Losing an eight-year role damaged my confidence and sense of direction.
- Main event: I sought advice, created a routine, and began learning AI tools.
- Ability shown: Informed decision-making and continuous learning.
- Reflection: The decision changed an uncontrollable setback into a direction I could influence.
