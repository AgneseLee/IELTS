## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a time when you worked in a group**

> **You should say:**
> - What you did
> - Who you worked with
> - What problems you faced
> - And explain why you worked in the group

## 1. Band 7 Version（最新最小改动版）

Last month at work, I was in a team of four colleagues building an internal reporting tool. We had to finish it in about six weeks, so it turned out to be quite demanding. At first, our roles were unclear and things were kind of messy. Actually, Stella and I ended up duplicating some work, while testing got ignored. With the deadline getting closer, it started to feel tense. I even had trouble falling asleep. I would lie in bed and couldn't stop thinking about what might happen if I messed things up or missed the deadline. So all these concerns made me feel extremely nervous and overwhelmed. But I realized that the deadline was approaching, so I suggested that the whole team have a quick meeting to clarify our roles and assign tasks according to each person's interests and strengths. After that, things became much more organized, and luckily, we met the deadline and finished everything on schedule. What impressed me most was how much the result depended on clear communication and shared responsibility. We worked as a group because the project required different skills, including design, coding, and testing, and it would have been very difficult for one person to handle everything alone.

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| turned out to be quite nervous | turned out to be quite demanding | 项目或情境用 demanding / stressful；人感到 nervous。 |
| I had a problem fall asleep | I had trouble falling asleep | 固定搭配 have trouble doing something。 |
| I lying in bed | I would lie in bed / I lay in bed | 原句缺少谓语；would lie 可描述过去反复发生的行为。 |
| what might be there if I messed it up | what might happen if I messed things up | 用 what might happen 表达担心可能发生的后果。 |
| all my concerns make me | all these concerns made me | 叙述过去经历，用 made。 |
| deadlines would—was approaching | the deadline was approaching | 此处指项目的一个截止日期，用单数及 was。 |
| assign tasks to—for everyone | assign tasks according to each person’s… | 去掉自我修正中重复的介词；according to 表示分配依据。 |
| each person's interest and strengths | each person's interests and strengths | 泛指各自的兴趣与长处，使用复数更自然。 |
| met the deadlines | met the deadline | 与本项目的单一截止日期保持一致。 |
| 未直接解释为什么小组合作 | We worked as a group because the project required different skills… | 修改版末句补充设计、编程、测试所需的不同技能，以及一人难以完成的原因，回应最后一个提示点。 |
