## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| at weekday nights | on weekday evenings / after work | 具体某类日子的晚上用 on。 |
| at weekends | on weekends | 英式 at weekends 与美式 on weekends 均正确；本次采用后者。 |
| was-is not far | isn’t far | 描述现在仍成立的位置用现在时；原句有自我修正。 |
| at the center | in the center | 描述池塘位于公园内部中心，用 in the center 更贴切；at the center 并非一律错误。 |
| lotus blossom | lotus flowers / lotus blossoms | 这里指多朵荷花，用复数更明确。 |
| it seemed had withered already | now they seem to have withered | 指荷花用 they；seem to have done；现在观察用 seem，过去观察用 seemed。 |
| hang out and take a walk there and took a walk there | hung out and took a walk there | 过去经历用过去式，合并口述重复。 |
| helped me reset my mind and give me a break | helped me reset my mind and take a break | helped me 后并列 reset 和 take，明确是我休息。 |
| busy day routine | daily routine | 固定搭配。 |
| when seeing those natural scenes, natural scenery | being in that natural setting | 整理自我修正；scenery 不可数。 |
| our job | our jobs | 指各自的工作时用复数。 |
| Stella comfort me | Stella comforted me | 叙述过去用 comforted。 |
| telling me that slow down and don't push myself so hard | telling me to slow down and not push myself so hard | 转述建议用 tell someone to do / not to do。 |
| whenever I need her | whenever I needed her | 过去转述保持时态一致。 |
| who have to | I had to | 简化表达；原 who 指单数 person，现在时也应为 has。 |
| after listen—after hearing | after hearing | 介词 after 后接动名词；原文已自行修正。 |
| when the breeze brushed against my face and listening | when the breeze brushed against my face and I heard | 并列两个完整从句，结构保持一致。 |
| the sounds of beetles | the sounds of beetles / the sounds of insects | 保留本次修改版的 beetles；若只泛指虫鸣，可用 insects，不属于语法错误。 |
| What I valued most wasn't the park itself, but the time I spent with Stella. | What I valued most was the peaceful atmosphere of the park and the time I spent there with Stella. | 采用用户最后补充的扣题结尾，同时体现自然环境和朋友陪伴。 |
