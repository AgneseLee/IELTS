## Cue Card

**Describe a person who makes plans a lot and is good at planning**

> **You should say:**
> - Who he/she is
> - How you knew him/her
> - What plans he/she makes
> - And explain how you feel about this person

> Bank: B1 | Modules: Malaysia-itinerary / offline-map / flexible-planning

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: Stella prepares essential details but leaves room to adjust when transport, weather, or navigation changes.
- Secondary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: Her backups and realistic priorities prevent small travel problems from becoming major ones.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella, my former colleague and one of my closest friends.
- Background: I met her about three years ago at work and later travelled to Malaysia with her.
- Main event: She built a realistic itinerary, saved offline maps, and calmly changed our route when the signal failed.
- Ability shown: Flexible planning supported by preparation and practical problem-solving.
- Reflection: Her approach taught me that a good plan creates freedom instead of controlling every minute.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The person I'd like to describe is Stella, a former colleague I met about three years ago. She makes plans for work, study, and especially travel. Before our Malaysia trip, she compared flights, booked accommodation, set a realistic budget, and prepared a detailed itinerary. However, she deliberately left some free time. She also saved our bookings and an offline map. When the navigation signal disappeared and we took a wrong route, she checked the map, asked other tourists for directions, and adjusted the schedule without panicking. We still reached our destination with little delay.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I admire her because her plans are flexible. She prepares important parts and accepts that life can change.

That shows adaptability. She remains flexible when things go wrong instead of treating the plan as a rule. Her preparation also supports problem-solving because backups help her focus on what matters first. I used to handle travel details at the last minute, which created unnecessary stress. Since travelling with Stella, I have started planning well in advance and saving essential information offline. Her example changed my perspective: good planning should create freedom, not remove it. A clear structure helps people stay calm and optimistic while enjoying unexpected moments. I now understand that preparation and flexibility can work together.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
