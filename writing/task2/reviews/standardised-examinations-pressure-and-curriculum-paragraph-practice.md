## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] “考试压力导致学生不堪重负”是合理的负面角度，但 `high academic performance quickly fades` 过于确定。应使用 `may`，并说明持续追求高分如何造成高负荷、慢性压力和倦怠。
- [TA] `resource misallocation` 需要具体说明资源原本应流向哪里。将 `essential functions` 明确为 `practical activities and open-ended inquiry`，才能支撑“全面智力发展受损”的结论。
- [TA] 段落同时讨论成绩不可持续与课程资源被挤占，两点都与标准化考试相关，可以保留。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] `Nevertheless → 原因 → deeper problem → result` 的总体顺序合理。
- [CC] `Because it is difficult...` 是句子残缺，且与前一句的关系不够顺滑；改为 `This is because...` 可明确承接原因。
- [CC] `it forces schools` 的指代不清。用 `an excessive focus on test preparation` 直接作主语，能明确资源错配的直接原因。
- [CC] 原文从考试制度直接跳到资源错配；加入“过度备考”这一中间机制后，因果链更完整。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `get good grades constantly` 搭配生硬，宜改为 `maintain consistently high grades`。
- [LR] `heavy study load` 缺少冠词；可写作 `a heavy study load`，本段使用 `heavy workloads` 更自然。
- [LR] `pressure coming from` 冗长，可压缩为 `pressure under`。
- [LR] `overbearing educational regime` 语气过强且不够精确；`test-driven educational regime` 能直接指出问题来源。
- [LR] `limited qualified teachers` 容易被误读为“能力有限的教师”；应写为 `limited resources, including qualified teachers and funding` 或重组语序。
- [LR] `steering students towards comprehensive intellectual development` 不够自然，可用 `guiding students towards...`。⚠ recurring：应优先选择准确、常见的动词搭配，而非刻意复杂化。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `the initial students’ high academic performance` 修饰关系错误；应为 `students’ initial high academic performance`，因为 `initial` 修饰 `performance`。
- [GR] `Because it is difficult...` 是从属从句，不能独立成句；应并入前句或改为 `This is because...`。
- [GR] `heavy study load` 是单数可数名词短语，应加冠词，或改为复数 `heavy workloads`。
- [GR] 原句的大部分复杂结构可以成立，但需继续检查每个句子是否具有独立的主句。⚠ recurring：从句残缺仍会显著影响语法分数。

## Final Integrated Version(103) (7.0–7.5)

Nevertheless, the apparent academic benefits of standardised examinations may be difficult to sustain. Constant pressure to achieve high scores can subject students to heavy workloads and chronic stress, eventually causing burnout and undermining their performance. More importantly, an excessive focus on test preparation can distort the allocation of educational resources. Schools may assign qualified teachers and limited funding to intensive exam coaching at the expense of practical activities and open-ended inquiry. This narrows the curriculum and leaves fewer opportunities for students to develop creativity and independent thinking. Consequently, an exam-driven system may create broader educational inefficiencies instead of promoting students’ comprehensive intellectual development.
