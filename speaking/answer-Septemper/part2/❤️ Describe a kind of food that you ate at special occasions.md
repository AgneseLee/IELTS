## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a kind of food that you ate at special occasions**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is
> - What occasion it was
> - Why you would like to eat it
> - And explain how you feel about it

> Bank: B6 | Modules: dumplings / catch-up-with-Stella / emotional-support

## 1. Band 7 Answer (用户提供的完整版本)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my career and my future. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me, and I couldn't stop thinking about things like what might happen if I messed things up at work. Things like that kept making me even more frustrated and upset. So anyway, that's why I talked to my friend Stella and said yes when she asked me to come to her apartment for a catch-up in Beijing last month. That night, I remember we just sat on the sofa talking about literature, movies, the universe, documentaries about the ocean, and basically anything we were interested in. She also made me her own special dumplings, which really stuck in my mind. I had never tried them before, which made them really appealing to me. They were really delicious and made me feel warm and peaceful. When I had the first bite, some of my stress was relieved, and I felt a little more relaxed. Then, during dinner, our conversation drifted to my concerns about work and life. She comforted me by saying, “Don't push yourself so hard. Slow down.” She said she would always stand by me whenever I needed her. I felt supported and understood after hearing what she said, and somehow the dumplings seemed even more delicious and left a lasting impression on me.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Looking back, what impressed me most was not the taste of the dumplings, but the time I spent with Stella, how she comforted me, and the way she gave me strength.
