## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a tall building you like or dislike**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is used for
> - Where it is
> - What it looks like
> - And explain why you like/dislike it

> Bank: B4 | Modules: sleeplessness / shopping-mall / advice

## 1. Band 7 Answer

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. Anyway, that's why I talked to my best friend Stella and invited her to hang out at a nearby shopping mall where we occasionally spend time and have fun. I wanted to take a break from my daily routine and relax a little.

The building is quite tall and big, modern and spacious. It has about ten or fifteen floors, although <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">I'm not sure exactly how many. </mark>There are a lot of clothing stores, jewelry shops and restaurants. That day, I intended to buy some jewelry and clothes, you know, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">the kind of things women are usually interested in</mark>. After shopping for a while, I ended up buying a fancy bracelet on one of the upper floors. Then we had lunch there. During lunch, our conversation drifted to what I was going through and what I was concerned about. Stella comforted me by telling me to slow down and not push myself so hard. She also told me that she would always stand by me whenever I needed her.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
After hearing what she said, I felt like some of my stress had been relieved, and I really felt supported and understood. Somehow, my problems didn't seem as serious as before. I felt a little more relaxed because I used to think I had to handle every challenge alone and immediately to prove that I was strong and capable enough. But what Stella said really changed my view on it. That night, I even slept better.

So I think the reason why I like this tall building is not only because of the building itself, but also because of the time I spent there with Stella. That was the most valuable thing to me.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I accepted Stella’s support and reconsidered my belief that I had to handle every challenge alone and immediately.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: A tall, modern shopping mall nearby, which I visited with Stella.
- Background: I wanted a break after several nights of worrying and sleeping badly.
- Main event: I bought a bracelet on an upper floor and talked to Stella about my worries over lunch.
- Ability shown: Accepting support and changing an unhelpful mindset.
- Reflection: I like the building because it reminds me of feeling supported and spending meaningful time with Stella.
