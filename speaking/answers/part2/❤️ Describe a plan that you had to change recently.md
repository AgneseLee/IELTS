## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a plan that you had to change recently**

> **You should say:**
> - When this happened
> - What made you change the plan
> - What the new plan was
> - And how you felt about the change

> Bank: B3 | Modules: cancelled-boat / local-market / unexpected-benefit

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I accepted unsafe weather conditions and helped replace an island trip with a realistic local activity.
- Secondary Ability: Creativity & Innovation
- Reason: Stella and I turned unfamiliar market ingredients into an enjoyable alternative experience.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella and I changed an island-trip plan in Malaysia.
- Background: Strong wind made the scheduled boat unsafe.
- Main event: We visited a market and cooked a local dish at our accommodation.
- Ability shown: Flexible planning and creative use of available options.
- Reflection: The replacement created a more personal cultural experience.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I had to change a plan during my Malaysia trip with Stella last May. We had booked a boat to visit an island, but strong wind made the sea unsafe, so the operator cancelled it that morning. I was disappointed because the island was one of the main reasons for our trip. Stella suggested visiting a local market instead and cooking a Malaysian dish at our accommodation. We bought unfamiliar spices, asked a vendor for advice, and experimented with the recipe. The meal was imperfect but surprisingly tasty, and the vendor's friendly help made the afternoon memorable.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I initially felt frustrated, but by evening I was pleased. The new plan gave us a more authentic cultural experience.

The event improved my adaptability because flexibility was more useful than protecting a perfect plan. It also encouraged creativity: we used available time, food, and local knowledge. I now keep simple backup ideas, which reduce stress without making travel rigid. Looking back, the cancelled boat changed the day's focus rather than ruining it. An unexpected problem can broaden my horizons when I adjust my approach. Stella and I still laugh about our imperfect dish, so the replacement left a lasting impression on both of us personally.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
