## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a piece of local news that people are interested in**

> **You should say:**
> - What it was about
> - Where you saw/heard it
> - Who was involved
> - And explain why people were interested in it

> Bank: B8 | Modules: Zhanjiang-clean-up / credible-report / personal-connection

> Logic: L2 · steady progress: difficulty → stop rushing → steady action → better result

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I checked the original report, connected the clean-up to a real local problem, and changed one daily habit.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: A report about a coastal clean-up in my hometown, Zhanjiang.
- Background: Plastic waste had built up after a busy holiday.
- Main event: Local residents, students, and fishing businesses cleaned the beach together.
- Ability shown: I checked the source carefully and turned what I learned into a practical change in my daily life.
- Reflection: Steady progress: difficulty → stop rushing → steady action → better result.

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
The problem initially looked too large for one person to solve. However, this example showed me that rushing or making dramatic promises would not create lasting change. Clear priorities, shared responsibility, and small repeated actions produced a visible result. That changed how I judge progress. I now look for practical steps and feedback instead of expecting an instant solution. The outcome may take time, but steady action is easier to maintain and correct. To me, slow does not mean weak; it means building a result that lasts. The change was small, but it made my next step feel manageable.
