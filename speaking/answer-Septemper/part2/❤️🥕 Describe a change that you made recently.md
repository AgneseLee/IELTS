## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a change that you made recently**

> **You should say:**
> - What the change was
> - What caused the change
> - What you did for the change
> - And explain how you feel about the change

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / daily-routine / manageable-steps

## 1. Band 7 Answer (用户提供的完整版本)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, my company went through a layoff, and suddenly I was given a much heavier workload. Then I began feeling overwhelmed and kind of burned out. I just couldn't sleep at night. I had trouble falling asleep. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my career and worrying about what might happen if I messed things up. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. I wanted to make a change because I wanted to live a better life and work better. Anyway, that's when I decided to make a change to my work routine. First, I honestly talked to my manager and asked him for help. He really did, and he helped me reorganize my workload and my work schedule. Then I also pointed out some unrealistic deadlines and stopped responding to those non-urgent messages at night, which helped me build healthier boundaries. Within a few weeks, by tackling these issues, I recovered both mentally and physically.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Looking back, the experience changed my view of resilience because I used to think strong and capable people should <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">continue working even under huge pressure </mark>and handle every challenge on their own. But now my view has changed. I believe that strength can also include asking for help, accepting support, and most importantly, changing direction, especially when things don't go as planned. Now I feel more confident when facing uncertainty, and if I face any career challenges in the future, I'll be more confident dealing with them.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
