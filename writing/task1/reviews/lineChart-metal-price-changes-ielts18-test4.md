## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 图表展示的是 `average monthly percentage change in prices compared with the previous month`，不是金属的实际价格。`6%→1%` 表示价格仍在上涨，但涨幅收窄；只有负值才表示价格较上月下降。
- [TA] `the price of Copper rose gradually ... 1%-2%` 不准确。Copper 的**变化率**从 1 月约 2% 降低，并在 6 月短暂跌至约 -0.5%，下半年大多接近 1%。
- [TA] Overview 中 `a general downward trend ... across all three categories` 过度概括：三条线都在年中走低并在年末回升，而 Zinc 从 1 月约 1% 上升至 12 月约 2%。
- [TA] Nickel 在 7–9 月稳定于约 -1%，10 月才降至约 -2%，因此不能笼统写成 `continued to dip between July and October`。
- [TA] Zinc 只写了 2 月峰值，没有交代其跌至 6 月约 -1% 后回升至 12 月约 2%，导致该系列明显展开不足。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] Overview 应优先比较波动性和共同阶段：`nickel was the most volatile, whereas copper changed the least; all three weakened around mid-year and recovered at the end`。
- [CC] 原稿把 Copper、Nickel 和 Zinc 全部塞进一个正文段。最小调整是保留 Copper 单独成段，再把走势更剧烈且相近的 Nickel 与 Zinc 放入另一段比较。
- [CC] `which was surpassed by Zinc and Copper` 和 `which peaked at 6%` 均有指代不清的问题；让金属或 `the rate` 直接作主语更清楚。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] ⚠ recurring `price` / `figure` → `percentage change` / `rate of change`：本题纵轴是变化率，数据名词必须与单位一致。
- [LR] ⚠ recurring `rise rate` → `growth rate`（仅用于正值）或更通用的 `rate of change`。
- [LR] `became slower` → `the rate of increase slowed`；价格本身不能“变慢”。
- [LR] `depicted a similar trend` → `followed a broadly similar pattern`：`depict` 表示图表“描绘”信息，不适合表示数据线遵循某种走势。
- [LR] `a higher level of 1%` 缺少比较基准，直接写 `recovered to 1%` 更准确简洁。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `how the average monthly prices changed of three different metals` → `the average monthly percentage changes in the prices of three metals`，修复词序与介词结构。
- [GR] ⚠ recurring `throught` / `Januaray` / `onced soar` → `throughout` / `January` / `once soared`，拼写与过去式必须稳定。
- [GR] `the price of Copper and Zinc` → `the rates of change for copper and zinc`：并列主语需用复数，且应改正数据对象。
- [GR] `Nickel ..., which peaked` 中 `which` 的先行词含糊；可改为独立主句 `Nickel's rate of change peaked at 6%`。
- [GR] `while it onced soar` 同时存在连词、拼写和时态问题；可改为 `after peaking at 3% in February`。

## Summary of Corrections

### 审题错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| 把纵轴当作实际价格 | `monthly percentage change in prices` | 每个点表示相对上月的涨跌幅。 |
| 把正增长率下降写成价格下跌 | `the rate of increase slowed` | 只要数值仍为正，价格仍较上月上涨。 |

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |

### 结构改进

## Final Integrated Version(176) (7.0–7.5)

The line graph compares the average monthly percentage changes in the prices of copper, nickel and zinc during 2014.

Overall, nickel experienced the greatest volatility, whereas copper's rate of change was stable. All three rates weakened mid-year before recovering in the final two months, when zinc finished with the highest positive change.

In January, nickel's price rose by 6% month on month, versus 2% for copper and 1% for zinc. Nickel's rate then fell sharply to 1% in March and April, before reaching a low of -3% in June. Zinc's rate peaked at 3% in February and declined to -1% in June. Copper followed a gentler downward path, moving from 2% to around -0.5% over the same period.

From July to September, the monthly changes for copper, nickel and zinc remained stable at roughly 1%, -1% and -0.5%, respectively. Nickel slipped to -2% in October, while zinc fell to about -1% and copper stayed positive. All three recovered thereafter: nickel ended at 1%, copper at approximately 1.5%, and zinc at 2% in December.
