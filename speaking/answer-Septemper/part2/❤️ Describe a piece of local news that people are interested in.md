## 6. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

> 根据本轮原回答和错误汇总整理；“可优化”表示自然度调整，不代表原表达必然有语法错误。

| 原表达 / 问题 | 最小修改 / 推荐表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| seeing some local news | reading some local news | 可优化：这里描述阅读新闻，用 reading 更具体；see news 本身并非必然错误。 |
| had been opened for over ten years | had been open for over ten years | open 作形容词表示持续营业；opened 强调开业动作。 |
| now it's shutting down | was shutting down | 在过去的新闻叙述中，用 was 保持时间线一致。 |
| a jacket which original price is one thousand yuan | a jacket that originally cost 1,000 yuan | 关系从句需要完整结构；也可用 whose original price was 1,000 yuan。 |
| sold at only two hundred | sold for only 200 yuan | 表示售价用 sold for 很自然；sold at a price 也可用，不是 at 一概错误。 |
| a incredible discount | an incredible discount | incredible 以元音音素开头，使用 an。 |
| ending of an era | end of an era | 可优化：惯用表达是 the end of an era。 |
| wasn't only about a shopping place | wasn't just a shopping place | 直接说明商场不只是购物场所；删去此处多余的 about。 |
| sleeping problem | sleep problems | 可优化：sleep problems 更常用；sleeping problem 本身并非必然错误。 |
| I always concerned | I was always worried | 表达担忧可用 be worried / be concerned；原句缺 be。 |
| news of the close of the shopping mall | news about the closure of the shopping mall | 这里表达关闭事件，用名词 closure 更自然。 |
| we should more focus on the current | we should focus more on the present | more 放在 focus 后更自然；“当下”用 the present。 |
| being panic | worrying / panicking | panic 在此不能直接接在 being 后；可用 panicking 或 being in a panic。 |
| It also remind me | It also reminded me | 回顾新闻对自己的影响，用过去式 reminded。 |
| do as better as we can | do the best we can | 表达尽最大努力用 do the best we can；as...as 中不用比较级 better。 |
| trying to control things that we could control | trying to control things that we can't control | 原句结尾逻辑说反；这里要表达别试图控制无法控制的事。 |
