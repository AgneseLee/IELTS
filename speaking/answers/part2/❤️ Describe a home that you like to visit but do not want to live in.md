## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a home that you like to visit but do not want to live in**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - What it is like
> - Why you like to visit it
> - And explain why you would not like to live there

> Bank: B7 | Modules: Stella-studio / welcoming / impractical-layout

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I compared the studio's welcoming atmosphere with its daily limitations and identified the practical features I need for living and working.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella's small studio in central Beijing.
- Background: I visit every few months before we browse the nearby Weekend Art Market and walk in the park.
- Main event: We drink coffee and discuss her paintings before going out, but its noise and lack of space make it unsuitable for my daily life.
- Ability shown: I analysed the difference between an enjoyable visit and a workable home.
- Reflection: The studio taught me to make housing decisions based on daily needs, not atmosphere alone.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The home I enjoy visiting is Stella's studio in central Beijing. It has large windows, warm lighting, and watercolour paintings, so it feels creative and welcoming. I visit every few months. Stella makes coffee and shows me her latest work. Afterwards, we often browse the nearby Weekend Art Market and walk in the park. This simple route makes the visit relaxing. However, the kitchen is tiny, traffic continues late at night, and there is almost no storage or separate desk area. I would not live there because I work from home.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I tried using my laptop there, but the noise and small table made concentration difficult. That experience improved my problem-solving skills. I learned to analyse my daily needs before choosing a home, rather than being influenced only by style or location. What truly matters is practical comfort over time. The studio is perfect for conversations, but living there would mean managing the same compromises every day. Looking back, it taught me that a place can suit one purpose beautifully and fail at another. I still love visiting because its atmosphere reflects Stella's personality, yet I now understand the quiet, storage, and workspace I need in my own home.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
