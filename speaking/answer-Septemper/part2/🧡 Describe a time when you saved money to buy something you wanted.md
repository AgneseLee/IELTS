## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a time when you saved money to buy something you wanted**

> **You should say:**
> - What you wanted to buy
> - Why you wanted to buy it
> - How you saved money for it
> - And explain how you felt after you bought it

## 1. Better Version（最新最小改动版）

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was basically worrying about everything that was bothering me, like whether AI was going to replace a lot of human jobs. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. I wanted to make a change. I didn't want to be replaced by AI, so that's why I decided to buy the latest model of the MacBook Pro, which I thought could help me learn AI technology and master it more efficiently. Then I made a plan to achieve this goal. I cut my budget for new clothes and restaurants, and I also stopped ordering unnecessary takeaway meals. It wasn't always easy because sometimes I really wanted to eat out or buy something new, but I knew the laptop was more important to me. Luckily, several weeks later, I bought the laptop without using any credit. Somehow, I felt relieved because saving patiently made the purchase feel rewarding rather than stressful. I also felt a sense of achievement because I reached my goal with a clear plan. I felt that I'd managed to do what I'd planned. **So yes, saving up for that one thing I really wanted ended up feeling worth it.**

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| would lay | would lie | 躺下用 lie；would 后用原形。 |
| what might be there if AI gonna replace all human labor | whether AI was going to replace a lot of human jobs | 重组为担忧的内容；be going to 不能省略 be，叙述过去用 was。 |
| make change | make a change | 此处 change 为可数名词。 |
| cutting off my budget on | cut my budget for | 削减预算用 cut the budget for；cut off 更像完全切断。 |
| credits | credit | 此处表示信贷，不可数。 |
| felt relief | felt relieved | feel 后用形容词 relieved；也可说 felt a sense of relief。 |
| patience, saving | saving patiently | 用副词 patiently 修饰 saving。 |
| a clear priority plans | a clear plan | 修正单复数和不自然的名词组合。 |
| I've got things done by what I planned | I'd managed to do what I'd planned | 自然表达完成了原先计划的事。 |
