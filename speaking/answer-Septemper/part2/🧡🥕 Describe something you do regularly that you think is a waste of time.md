## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe something you do regularly that you think is a waste of time**

> **You should say:**
> - What the activity is
> - When and where you do it
> - Why you continue doing it
> - Why you think it is a waste of time

> Bank: B5 | Modules: late-night-scrolling / sleep-loss / phone-limit

> Logic: mental fatigue → scrolling for relief → no real rest → phone limits → healthier routine

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The activity I think is a waste of time is scrolling through short videos on social media on my phone. I usually do it in bed before going to sleep, especially when I'm mentally drained after work. After staring at a screen all day, scrolling feels like an easy way to switch off my mind. Once I click on something I'm interested in, like animals or scenery, the algorithm keeps pushing more of the same. I just keep scrolling, sometimes for one or two hours a night.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
**I thought it helped me unwind**, but I realized that I didn't actually feel rested afterwards. I wasn't learning anything useful either. What bothers me most is that it takes time away from things I want to learn after work. Each video is short, so it's easy to tell myself, "Just one more."

So now I'm trying to change this habit with a simple rule. I set **a fifteen-minute timer**, then put my phone across the room when it rings. This gives me a clear point to stop. I'm working on building a healthier routine that helps me **fall asleep more naturally**, instead of spending the whole evening scrolling.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
