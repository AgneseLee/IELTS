By contrast, the groups of eating every day and a few times a year displayed relatively even shares throughout 10 years, with the figures of approximately 3-4% and roughly 4-5%, respectively. People eating a few times a year accounted for 15% in both 2006 and 2013, compared with 13% in 2003.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 你正确指出了最高占比的类别发生变化：2003、2006 年是 `once a week`，2013 年是 `once or twice a month`。
- [TA] 最后一段把类别和数据配错：`roughly 4–5%` 对应的是 `never`，不是 `a few times a year`；后者约为 13–15%。这是本篇最明显的数据准确性问题。
- [TA] `once a week` 在 2013 年约为 28%，写成 `roughly 27%` 略不准确。
- [TA] `several times a week` 只说走势、没有给数据。建议补入 17%→20%→16%，这样趋势才有充分证据。
- [TA] `every day` 和 `never` 始终是占比最低的两类，这一显著特征适合放入 overview。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 你的分组方向基本正确：变化较明显的三个中高频类别放在一起，其余较稳定的类别放在另一段。
- [CC] `both of these group` 指代不清，而且 `these` 后应有复数名词。直接说 `the three more common frequencies varied more noticeably` 更清楚。
- [CC] `By contrast` 后列举的类别与前文不构成完全准确的对比，因为原句还把类别标签写错。先明确“低频且稳定”的类别，再比较具体数值。
- [CC] 第三段的顺序可以更紧凑：先写 `once a week` 与 `once or twice a month` 的领先位置互换，再补充 `several times a week` 的小幅波动。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] ⚠ recurring `the group of eating once a week` 搭配生硬，改为 `people who ate fast food once a week`、`the once-a-week category` 或 `those eating once a week`。
- [LR] `across different frequency` 应改为 `by frequency` 或 `across different frequencies`。
- [LR] `showed great disparity among years` 夸大变化且搭配不自然，可写成 `varied more noticeably over time`。
- [LR] `follow the similar pattern to it` 改为固定搭配 `followed a similar pattern`。
- [LR] `relative similar shares` 中需要副词：`relatively similar shares`；更自然的图表表达是 `changed relatively little`。
- [LR] `the lion's share` 虽然语法上可用，但这里最高值仅约三分之一，`the largest proportion` 更精确、自然。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `compares percentage` 缺冠词且通常用复数，改为 `compares the percentages`。
- [GR] `both of these group` 应为 `both of these groups`；若实际涉及三组，则须改写整个指代。
- [GR] 全图描述过去年份，应保持过去时：`follow`→`followed`，`shows`→`showed`。
- [GR] `the largest proportions ... at 31% and 33%` 指同一类别在两个年份的数值，宜用单数 `the largest proportion`，或直接写 `was the largest category`。
- [GR] `throughout 10 years` 改为 `over the ten-year period`。
