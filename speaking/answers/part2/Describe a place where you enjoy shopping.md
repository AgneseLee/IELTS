**Describe a place where you enjoy shopping**

> **You should say:**

- Reason: I ask local artists about their work, listen to their explanations, and use those conversations to choose more meaningful gifts.

- Background: I discovered it after visiting Stella's nearby studio, and we now go together every few months.
- Main event: I browse independent stalls and usually buy notebooks, small prints, or practical gifts.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
A place where I enjoy shopping is the Weekend Art Market, beside a public park in Beijing. I discovered it after visiting Stella's nearby studio, and we now go together every few months. It has independent stalls selling prints, handmade notebooks, and simple ceramic items. We normally browse the market before walking through the park. I usually buy notebooks for myself or practical gifts for friends. The sellers often explain how they made each object, and those conversations are the main reason I enjoy the market.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
They help me understand different perspectives and choose something with a real story, rather than buying a generic product. The experience has improved my communication skills because I ask specific questions, listen actively, and share honest opinions. I have also become less impulsive. Before I go, I set a modest budget and think about who could genuinely use an item. This makes the outing relaxing without creating unnecessary spending. Looking back, the market changed my view of shopping. It can support local artists and build meaningful connections, even through a short conversation. I leave with fewer things than I might buy in a large mall, but each purchase feels more personal, useful, and memorable.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
