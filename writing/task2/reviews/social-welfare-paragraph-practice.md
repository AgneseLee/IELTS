## Original Draft(130)

A significant drawback of poorly designed welfare cannot be neglected. The main reason is that people may become more reluctant to work due to the provision of welfare system and gradually rely more on the benefit, thereby leading to a lower labour participation rate and increasing fiscal pressure.
Nevertheless, although the problem appears severe, it is far from unsolvable. One practical approach to addressing this issue is to optimize the allocation towards welfare system by maintaining a reasonable financial distribution on it. This is effective because this practice not only support and protect vulnerable groups but also encourage people who are capable of taking jobs to work, ensuring that the problem is dealt with in a concrete way. If implemented properly, it can address the problem while preserving its benefits.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 问题链 `welfare dependency → lower labour participation → fiscal pressure` 相关且清楚。
- [TA] `optimize the allocation` 和 `maintaining a reasonable financial distribution` 只说明要“合理花钱”，没有解释如何恢复就业动力，因此未能直接解决前段提出的福利依赖。
- [TA] 为了保留原有思路，只需把 `optimize the allocation` 具体化为一项机制：随着领取者收入上升逐步减少福利，而不是立即取消。这样既保留基本保障，也能维持就业带来的经济收益。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 两段采用“问题—解决方案”的顺序，整体推进明确。
- [CC] `although the problem appears severe` 与 `it is far from unsolvable` 含义重复，让步表达保留一个即可。
- [CC] `ensuring that the problem is dealt with in a concrete way` 和末句 `it can address the problem` 都是空泛总结，没有继续推进因果链；应把篇幅用于说明措施如何形成就业激励。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `poorly designed welfare` 可改为 `poorly designed welfare systems` 或更精确的 `poorly designed welfare benefits`。
- [LR] `rely more on the benefit` 应改为 `become dependent on welfare benefits`。
- [LR] `allocation towards welfare system` 应改为 `the allocation of welfare funds`；`financial distribution on it` 可改为 `welfare spending` 或 `funding levels`。
- [LR] `people who are capable of taking jobs` 不自然，宜写成 `people who are able to work`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `the provision of welfare system` 中 `system` 是单数可数名词；可写为 `the provision of welfare benefits` 或 `the provision of a welfare system`。
- [GR] `the benefit` 泛指多项或持续领取的福利金时应写为复数 `benefits`。
- [GR] ⚠ recurring：`this practice not only support ... but also encourage` 的主语是第三人称单数，两个并列谓语都应加 `-s`：`supports ... and encourages ...`。
- [GR] `thereby leading to ... and increasing ...` 形式平行，但更直接的有限动词结构 `This can lower ... and increase ...` 更易读。

## Final Integrated Version(134) (7.0–7.5)

A significant drawback of poorly designed welfare systems is that they can discourage employment. If benefits remain unchanged regardless of recipients' efforts or earnings, some people may have less incentive to seek work and gradually become dependent on public support. This can lower labour force participation and place growing fiscal pressure on the government.

Nevertheless, this problem can be addressed without abandoning social protection. Governments can gradually reduce benefits as recipients' earnings rise and require those who are able to work to seek employment or attend vocational training. At the same time, adequate assistance should remain available to people who cannot support themselves because of disability, illness or other serious difficulties. Such a system would continue to protect vulnerable groups while giving other recipients a clear incentive and a practical route back into employment.
