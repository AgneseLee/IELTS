## Cue Card

**Describe a quiet place you like to go**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - How you knew it
> - How often you go there
> - What you do there
> - And explain how you feel about the place

> Bank: B7 | Modules: neighbourhood-park / after-rain / weekly-routine

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I notice when work pressure is affecting my focus, take a short walk, and return with a clearer mind.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: A small neighbourhood park near my apartment in Beijing.
- Background: I discovered it while exploring the area after moving there.
- Main event: I visit about once a week to walk around the pond, read, and take a break from my phone.
- Ability shown: I adjust my routine when stress makes it difficult to concentrate.
- Reflection: The park showed me that a small, practical break can help me manage pressure and work better.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The quiet place I like to go to is a small park near my apartment in Beijing. I found it soon after I moved there, simply by exploring my neighbourhood one evening. Since then, I have gone there about once a week, usually after work or at weekends. I normally walk around the pond, read on a bench, or just watch the birds. After rain, the leaves look brighter and the air smells fresh. Although traffic is close by, the trees block most of the noise, so the park feels surprisingly peaceful.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
What I love most is its practical comfort. It is free, nearby, and needs no planning. That means I can take a break from my busy daily routine without waiting for a holiday. When work feels overwhelming, I put my phone away and walk one full circuit. That small action helps me clear my head and sort out my thoughts.

The park has also made me more adaptable. I used to force myself to keep working even when I could no longer focus. Now I adjust my approach and take a short break before returning to a problem. I usually leave feeling calmer, more focused, and ready to continue my day.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
