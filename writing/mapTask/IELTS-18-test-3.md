## Final Version (7.0–7.5)

The diagrams compare the layout of a public library 20 years ago with its current floor plan.

Overall, the library has been extensively modernised and reorganised, with most former book and reading areas replaced by more specialised facilities. The central tables and chairs have also been removed, while the entrance has maintained its original position.

Twenty years ago, the library was arranged around a central seating area. Immediately to the west and east of this space were sections for adult fiction and adult non-fiction books, respectively. Along the northern edge of the building, CDs, videos and computer games occupied the north-western corner, while children's books were placed in the north-eastern corner. The south-western corner contained a reading room for newspapers and magazines, opposite an enquiry desk in the south-eastern corner.

Today, the north-western area has been transformed into a children's fiction section with sofas and a storytelling area, while the former reading room has been replaced by a computer room. On the eastern side, the old children's book section has become a lecture room, and the enquiry desk has been converted into a cafe. The adult fiction books now occupy part of the east-central area, adjacent to an information desk and self-service machines. All reference books are positioned to the west of the main hall, flanking the central space with the information services opposite them.

## Summary of Corrections

### 审题错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `The most noticeable changes was... accessibility for people` | `most former book and reading areas replaced by more specialised facilities` | 图中没有强调 accessibility；主要变化是功能重新分区、服务现代化 |
| `digital media room` | `CDs, videos and computer games area` | 原图不是 digital media room，不能过度概括 |
| `general children story room` | `children's fiction section with sofas and a storytelling area` | today 图中是 children's fiction books + storytelling events，不是一个单独的 story room |
| `audit fiction books` | `adult fiction books` | 词形错误改变含义 |

### 语法 & 拼写

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `has been undergone` | `has undergone` / `has been modernised` |
| `The most noticeable changes was` | `The most noticeable change was` / `The most noticeable changes were` |
| `Before 20 years ago` | `Twenty years ago` |
| `the seating area situated` | `the seating area was situated` |
| `children book area` | `children's book area` |
| `nowadays, the library experienced` | `today, the library has experienced` / `today, the library is...` |
| `A computer room is constructed in where...` | `A computer room has been created where...` |
| `a cafe and a lecture room located` | `a cafe and a lecture room are located` |
| `southeastern and northeastern corner` | `the south-eastern and north-eastern corners` |
| `of which with the entrance remaining...` | `while the entrance has maintained its original position` |

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `incorporated with` | `integrated with` / `equipped with` | `incorporated with` 搭配不自然 |
| `make way for accessibility for people` | `make way for a more open central area` | `accessibility for people` 表达含糊，且图中无依据 |
| `across to the hall` | `across the hall` / `on the opposite side of the hall` | 介词错误 |
| `in stark contrast` | `Today, ...` / `By contrast, ...` | map task 中可直接按时间分段；这里没有极端对比，不必用 stark |
| `experienced a significant development` | `has been extensively modernised and reorganised` | development 通常不用于室内平面变化 |
| `flanked either side of` | `flanked the central space` / `were positioned on either side of` | `flank` 是及物动词，结构要完整 |

### 结构改进

The original essay covered many rooms, but the overview included an unsupported claim about accessibility and missed the clearest macro-pattern: the library changed from a book-and-reading layout into a more service-oriented and activity-based space. The final version groups the description by time and then by location, using stable spatial phrases such as `along the northern edge of`, `immediately to the west/east of`, `adjacent to`, `replaced by`, and `transformed into`.
