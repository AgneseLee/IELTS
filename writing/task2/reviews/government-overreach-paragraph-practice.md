## Original Draft(62)

Nevertheless, excessive government intervention may produce harmful effects on business markets. A central problem is that it increase compliance and administrative costs for companies, which poses extra pressure on companies, particularly on those struggling with financial pressure already. As a consequence, this approach often generates new problems such as undermining corporate innovation and competition among companies, ultimately weakening the national economic efficiency.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] `excessive intervention → higher compliance and administrative costs → weaker innovation and competition → lower economic efficiency` 是一条切题的负向逻辑链。
- [TA] 从 `compliance and administrative costs` 直接跳到 `undermining corporate innovation` 缺少机制。应说明企业会把资金和人力从研发中转去满足监管要求。
- [TA] 成本上升也不会自动削弱竞争；需要说明小型或财务脆弱的企业承受不成比例的压力，因此更难与大型企业竞争。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] `A central problem` 和 `As a consequence` 正确标示了问题及后果，整体顺序清楚。
- [CC] 第二句重复 `companies` 和 `pressure`，使信息推进显得机械；把后半句改成具体机制可以同时解决重复和逻辑缺口。
- [CC] `this approach` 能回指政府干预，但 `excessive intervention` 更直接，可避免把所有政府措施都笼统视为负面。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `business markets` 含义不清，泛指商业领域应写为 `the business sector`。
- [LR] `poses extra pressure` 应改为固定搭配 `places additional pressure`。
- [LR] `struggling with financial pressure already` 可简化为更自然的 `already under financial strain`。
- [LR] `competition among companies` 可写成 `market competition`，更准确地概括市场层面的结果。
- [LR] `the national economic efficiency` 不自然，应写为 `overall economic efficiency`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `it increase` 应为 `it increases`。⚠ recurring：第三人称单数主谓一致已在笔记中多次出现。
- [GR] `the national economic efficiency` 中的定冠词多余；`economic efficiency` 泛指时不加 `the`。
- [GR] `such as undermining...` 语法上可以成立，但与 `generates new problems` 搭配后结构笨重；直接使用谓语 `weakens` 更清楚。

## Summary of Corrections

### 语法 & 拼写

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `it increase` | `it increases` |
| `the national economic efficiency` | `overall economic efficiency` |

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `business markets` | `the business sector` | 自然概括企业整体 |
| `poses extra pressure` | `places additional pressure` | 固定搭配是 `place pressure on` |
| `financial pressure already` | `already under financial strain` | 搭配和语序更自然 |
| `competition among companies` | `market competition` | 更准确地表达市场层面的竞争 |

### 结构改进

保留“过度干预—合规成本—创新与竞争下降—经济效率受损”的顺序，在成本与结果之间补入两个最小机制：资源被从创新活动中转移，以及小型或财务脆弱企业承担不成比例的成本。

## Final Integrated Version(70) (7.0–7.5)

Nevertheless, excessive government intervention can harm the business sector. Complying with complex regulations raises firms' legal, reporting and administrative expenses, placing disproportionate strain on smaller and financially vulnerable businesses. These costs can divert capital and staff away from product development, limiting firms' ability to innovate. They may also make it harder for smaller businesses to compete with established companies. Consequently, overregulation can weaken market competition and reduce overall economic efficiency.

## Original Logic and Order — Expression-only Version(58) (Estimated Band 6.5)

Nevertheless, excessive government intervention may **have harmful effects on the business sector**. A central problem is that it **increases** compliance and administrative costs for companies, which **places additional pressure on businesses**, **particularly those already under financial strain**. As a consequence, this approach often **creates** new problems **by** undermining corporate innovation and **market competition**, ultimately weakening **overall economic efficiency**.

### 结论与卡点

仅修正表达后，现实上限约为 Band 6.5，尚不足以稳定达到 7.0–7.5。

- [TA] 合规与行政成本如何削弱创新仍未说明，缺少资金或人员被挤出研发活动的机制。
- [TA] 企业承受额外压力与市场竞争下降之间仍有跳跃，没有说明哪些企业受影响最大。
- [CC] 因果方向虽然清楚，但第二句和第三句之间缺少必要的中间环节。
- [LR] 与 [GR] 的主要语言错误已修正，不再是核心卡点。

## 解决卡点问题

| 卡点 | 最小幅度的调整 |
|---|---|
| 合规成本与创新下降之间缺少机制 | 说明资金和人员会从创新活动中被转移 |
| 成本压力与竞争下降之间缺少机制 | 明确小型或财务脆弱企业承担不成比例的压力 |
| 结果表达重复且笼统 | 用 `weakens business innovation and market competition` 直接概括两个后果 |

### Minimally Adjusted Version(59) (Target Band 7.0–7.5)

Nevertheless, excessive government intervention may **have harmful effects on the business sector**. A central problem is that it **increases** compliance and administrative costs for companies, **diverting money and staff away from innovation and placing disproportionate pressure on smaller or financially constrained firms**. As a consequence, this approach often **weakens business innovation and market competition**, ultimately **reducing overall economic efficiency**.
