## Cue Card

**Describe an occasion when many people were smiling**

> **You should say:**
> - When it happened
> - Who you were with
> - What happened
> - And explain why most people were smiling

> Bank: B6 | Modules: birthday-dinner / lottery-joke / reconnecting

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: Stella used a well-timed joke and shared memories to reconnect a group of busy friends.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: My birthday dinner with Stella and two close friends.
- Background: We had not met as a group for several months.
- Main event: Stella joked about our losing Malaysia lottery tickets, and everyone laughed.
- Ability shown: Humour created connection and lifted the mood.
- Reflection: Small shared moments can matter more than expensive celebrations.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The occasion was my birthday dinner at my Beijing apartment last year. I invited Stella and two close friends, and I cooked a simple meal. We had not met as a group for months, so the conversation was quiet at first. After dinner, Stella mentioned two lottery tickets we had bought in Malaysia. Neither had won, and she joked that we had not wasted the money; we had simply bought back our luck. She delivered the line so seriously that everyone laughed. Even a friend who had arrived tired began smiling and sharing travel stories.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
The joke turned disappointment into a shared memory and created a warm and relaxed atmosphere. I remember the laughter more than my gifts.

The occasion changed my view of celebrations. I once thought a memorable birthday needed an expensive plan. That evening showed that attention and communication matter more. Stella understood the mood and helped us reconnect naturally. Kind humour can build trust through shared experience. Looking back, I value the dinner as a shared family memory because those friends feel like family. It encouraged me to organise simple gatherings more often and focus on real, relaxed conversation instead of impressive unnecessary details.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
