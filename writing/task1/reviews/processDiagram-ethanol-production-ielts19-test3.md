- [TA] 图中还显示预处理会释放二氧化碳；正文遗漏了这一支线。
- [TA] `cellulose liquid` 属于误读。图中只标注 `cellulose`，之后才经加工转化为糖。
- [TA] 图示未编号且循环边界可有不同划分，Introduction 使用 `nine-stage` 风险较高；直接写 `the cyclical process used to produce ethanol` 更安全。

- [CC] Overview 应使用 `growth → conversion → combustion → carbon dioxide reabsorption` 的闭环逻辑，而非强行指定固定终点。

The diagram illustrates the cyclical process used to produce ethanol biofuel from plant material.

Overall, plants and trees absorb carbon dioxide while growing before being converted into ethanol. Burning this fuel releases carbon dioxide, which is then reabsorbed by new plants, completing the cycle.

Initially, plants and trees grow by using energy from sunlight and absorbing carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. They are then harvested after reaching maturity. Following this, the harvested plant material undergoes pre-processing, during which it is broken down and some carbon dioxide is released. During the remaining stages, the cellulose is converted into sugars. Subsequently, microbes are added to these sugars to produce ethanol. Finally, the finished biofuel is used to power cars, trucks and aircraft, releasing carbon dioxide into the atmosphere. This gas, together with emissions from pre-processing, is then reabsorbed during the growth of new plants and trees, beginning another production cycle.
