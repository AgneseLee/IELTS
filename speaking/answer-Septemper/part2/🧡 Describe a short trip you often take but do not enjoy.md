## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a short trip you often take but do not enjoy**

> **You should say:**
> - Where you go
> - When you go there
> - Why you go there
> - And explain why you do not enjoy the trip

> Bank: B3 | Modules: car-trip / city / regular-commute

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I often take a short car trip from my flat to my office in Beijing. I usually make it on weekday mornings when I need to attend meetings in person. The distance is only around twelve kilometres, so it looks easy on a map. In reality, rush-hour traffic can turn it into an hour-long journey. Cars move slowly, drivers keep changing lanes, and I spend most of the trip staring at the same buildings. I go because some discussions are simply easier face to face with my team.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I don't enjoy the trip because it leaves me tired before work even begins. Sitting in traffic also feels like wasted time that I could use for breakfast, exercise or quiet preparation. Still, the journey has made me more adaptable. I now check the traffic early, leave extra time and listen to an English podcast instead of getting annoyed. These small changes help me focus on what I can control. I cannot remove the traffic, but I can make the journey less stressful and arrive in a better mood. It has also taught me to plan around uncertainty rather than expect every trip to run perfectly. If public transport becomes more direct, I will happily change the way I travel.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I changed my timing and routine to make an unavoidable journey less stressful.
- Secondary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I checked traffic conditions and used the travel time for useful listening practice.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: My regular car journey from home to my office in Beijing.
- Background: I make the twelve-kilometre trip on weekdays when meetings require me to work in person.
- Main event: Rush-hour traffic often turns a short distance into an hour-long journey.
- Ability shown: I plan around delays and use the time more calmly and productively.
- Reflection: I still dislike the trip, but I have learned to focus on the parts I can control.
