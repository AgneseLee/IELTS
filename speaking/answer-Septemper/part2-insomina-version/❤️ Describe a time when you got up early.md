## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I adjusted to an unfamiliar early schedule to take part in a meaningful travel experience.
- Secondary Ability: Learning Ability
- Reason: I learned from the experience that temporary discomfort can be worthwhile when it has a clear purpose.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella and I got up at 4:30 a.m. for a sunrise boat near Semporna.
- Background: We had little sleep, and early mornings are not part of my normal routine.
- Main event: We boarded a small boat and watched the sky change over the sea.
- Ability shown: Adapting to discomfort and learning from direct experience.
- Reflection: Emotional reset: stress → pause → steady action → clearer mind.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I got up at <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">4:30 </mark>one morning during my Malaysia trip with Stella, near Semporna. Before the trip, work stress had disturbed my sleep for weeks. Getting up for the sunrise gave me a reason to reset my rhythm. We had booked a small boat to watch the sunrise from the open water, so missing the alarm would have meant losing the experience. I felt <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">half awake</mark> and slightly <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">irritable</mark> because we had slept very little. Still, I got ready, followed the guide, and boarded the boat. The quiet harbour and cool air gradually woke me up.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
As the sky changed from dark blue to orange, everyone became <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">silent</mark>, and the light across the sea was <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">breathtaking</mark>. That became the turning point I needed. Instead of pushing harder, I paused and focused on one manageable step at a time. Once I slowed down, I could think more clearly and make fewer mistakes. The improvement was gradual, but my sleep and confidence both recovered. I realised that slowing down does not mean falling behind; steady action can move me forward faster. Since then, when pressure builds, I protect my sleep, reduce unnecessary tasks, and notice ordinary moments. I now feel calmer and enjoy daily life more.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
