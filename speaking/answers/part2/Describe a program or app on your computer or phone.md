## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The program I'd like to describe is ChatGPT, which I use on both my laptop and phone. I first found it through technology news and became curious about its practical uses. Now I use it almost every day for work and English study. It explains unfamiliar technical concepts, helps me brainstorm solutions, and organises my notes. Once, it also pointed out a simple coding mistake within seconds, although I still tested the corrected version before using it.

What I like most is that the program supports exploration without requiring me to know the perfect question at the beginning.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I can ask follow-up questions, compare examples, and gradually make an unclear idea more specific. This process has improved how I acquire knowledge and solve small problems.

However, I do not treat it as an automatic expert. Fluent answers can still contain confident mistakes, so I verify important information and use human judgement before making decisions. Used responsibly, the program can boost my productivity and help turn ideas into working results. It gives me more time to focus on design, meaning, and accuracy instead of repetitive tasks. Overall, I feel positive about it because it works best as a fast, flexible assistant rather than a replacement for my own knowledge.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
