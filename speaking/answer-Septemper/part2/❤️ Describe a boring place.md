## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a boring place**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - Who you went there with
> - What you did there
> - And explain why you think it is a boring place

> Bank: B6 | Modules: sleeplessness / apartment-party / feeling-out-of-place

## 1. Band 7 Answer

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. So that's why I said yes when my best friend Stella invited me to a party at her apartment in Beijing, because I wanted to take a break from my daily routine and relax a little. At the party, there were about ten people. Some were standing in the living room while others were sitting on a sofa, talking about things like cars, money, houses, someone's girlfriend or boyfriend.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Honestly, I had no interest in those topics, and I felt kind of out of place. Actually, I couldn't understand why people would be interested in those kinds of things, which I thought were totally boring, and I felt kind of lonely in such a crowd. I would rather stay at home alone than feel lonely like that. People seemed to be pretending to be social rather than listening carefully to what others said. They were just listening, not really hearing, as if their minds were somewhere else. So I found it one of the most boring places I've ever been to. I literally regretted the decision I made, and I realized that not every gathering is worth saying yes to.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Learning Ability
- Reason: I reflected on an unenjoyable party and learned that accepting every invitation did not necessarily help me relax.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: A party at my best friend Stella’s apartment in Beijing.
- Background: I accepted her invitation to take a break after several nights of poor sleep.
- Main event: About ten guests talked about topics I had no interest in, and I felt out of place.
- Ability shown: Learning from an uncomfortable social experience.
- Reflection: I regretted attending and realized that not every gathering is worth saying yes to.
