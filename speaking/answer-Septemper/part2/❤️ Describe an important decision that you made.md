## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe an important decision that you made**

> **You should say:**
> - What the decision was
> - How you made your decision
> - What the results of the decision were
> - And explain why it was important

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / work-schedule / manageable-steps

## 1. Band 7 Answer

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, my company went through a layoff, and suddenly I was given a much heavier workload. I started feeling overwhelmed and anxious. Also, I couldn't fall asleep naturally at night. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my career, my future, and what might happen if I messed things up. I was extremely nervous about everything at work. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. I wanted to make a change. I wanted to live a better life and do a better job. So that's why I decided to change my work schedule and talk to my manager and ask him for help. My manager listened to me carefully and helped me reorganize my workload and priorities. I also took a fifteen-minute walk after work every day because it helped me relieve some of my stress and relax a little.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Although it didn't fix everything right away, some of my stress was lifted and my mood became much better. Honestly, it wasn't easy at the beginning because sometimes I was just too tired to do anything. Anyway, I reminded myself to break things down. That's what my best friend Stella had told me before. We could handle a huge problem by breaking it down into smaller, more manageable steps. That helped me focus on one small thing at a time and realize what mattered most at the moment. Luckily, thanks to the decision I made, I felt much more relaxed and relieved afterwards. It was important because it helped me regain control of my life, protect my health, and feel more confident moving forward.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I asked my manager to help reorganize my workload and priorities, and broke problems into manageable steps.
- Secondary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I changed my work schedule and added a daily walk when my workload began affecting my sleep.
