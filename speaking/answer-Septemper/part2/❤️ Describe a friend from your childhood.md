## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a friend from your childhood**

> **You should say:**
> - Who he/she is
> - Where and how you met each other
> - What you often did together
> - And explain what made you like him/her

> Bank: B1 | Modules: childhood friend / support

## 1. Band 7 Answer (minimum changes)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The childhood friend I'd like to talk about is Stella. About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future, my career, and what might happen if AI replaced all of our jobs. All of those things kept bothering me. Anyway, that's why I made a call to my friend Stella, my childhood friend. We've known each other for at least twenty years. Even though we don't live in the same city now, we still keep in touch through social media like WeChat, or sometimes we talk on the phone.

So that night, I called her and described what I was going through at the moment and what my concerns were. Then she comforted me by telling me that “slow is fast” and that I shouldn't push myself so hard. She also told me that she would always stand by me whenever I needed her.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
After hearing what she said, I felt really understood and supported. She's the kind of person who always stays calm, whatever the situation is. She's also such a charming and optimistic person, and that's what I've always liked about her.

What she said made me realize that slowing down doesn't mean falling behind. Instead, it allows us to focus on what we can control and realize what truly matters at the moment, so that we can focus our efforts without distractions. Anyway, somehow some of my stress was relieved. The <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">conversation</mark> was kind of magical. After having that talk with her, my sleep improved a bit. Even though it didn't fix everything right away, I felt more confident when facing difficulties at work and in life, because Stella told me that she would always <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">support</mark> me. That's why I like her so much.

## 5. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

> 根据本轮提供的原回答节选及 Main mistakes 栏整理；节选未展示的表达仅来自错误汇总，不补写为完整原始回答。“可优化 / 可精简”不表示原表达必然有语法错误。

| 原表达 / 问题 | 最小修改 / 推荐表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| falling sleep | falling asleep | 固定搭配 fall asleep；sleep 不能直接替换这里的 asleep。 |
| I lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking | I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking | would lie 表示那段时间反复发生的动作；也可用过去式 lay。 |
| if AI replace all of our jobs | if AI replaced all of our jobs | 与 what might happen 搭配，用过去式表达假设；也可改用 whether AI might replace…。 |
| We had know each other | We've known each other for at least twenty years. | 完成时用过去分词 known；持续至今用 have known，过去某个时间之前的关系才用 had known。 |
| even we now didn't live in the same city | Even though we don't live in the same city now | 让步用 even though；描述现在的居住情况用 don't。 |
| talking on phones | we talk on the phone | 习惯表达用 talk on the phone；完整句中与 we keep in touch 衔接。 |
| concerns was | concerns were | concerns 为复数，谓语用 were。 |
| Then she comforts me | Then she comforted me | 叙述当晚发生的事，用过去式 comforted。 |
| she would be always stand by me whenever I need her | she would always stand by me whenever I needed her | would 后直接接动词原形，不加 be；always 放在 would 后，needed 与过去转述保持一致。 |
| always stay calm | always stays calm | 描述她一贯的性格用现在时，she 配 stays。 |
| some of my stress had been relieved | some of my stress was relieved | 可优化：这里按事件顺序讲述，用一般过去时更直接；had been relieved 在有明确过去参照点时也正确。 |
| The conversations was | The conversation was | 这里指当晚的一次谈话，用 conversation was；若指多次谈话，则用 conversations were。 |
| kind of magic | kind of magical | 这里用形容词 magical 描述谈话的感受更自然；magic 在其他语境也可作形容词。 |
