## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a crowded place you went to**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it was
> - When you went there
> - Who you went there with
> - What you did there
> - And how you felt about it

## 1. Better Version（最新最小改动版）

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed and couldn't stop thinking about my career and my future. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. I wanted to make a change and take a break from my daily routine. So that's why I invited Stella, my best friend, to hang out at a shopping mall nearby in Beijing.

I intended to buy some clothes and jewelry, you know, the kinds of things women are usually interested in. <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">But when I arrived there, I was surprised to find that it was extremely crowded. It was totally a disaster because there were hundreds of people on the first floor.</mark> The whole place felt overcrowded and very noisy. I could hardly talk to Stella because there were so many people around us. So we went up to the top floor, where there were fewer people. We browsed some shops there, and luckily, there were some jewelry shops and restaurants. We had lunch there, and I ended up buying a fancy bracelet.

During lunch, our conversation drifted into my concerns about work and life. Stella comforted me by telling me that “slow is fast” and not to push myself too hard. She said she would always stand by me and that I could ask her for help whenever I needed it. After hearing what she said, I felt really understood and supported. Somehow, I felt like some of my stress had been relieved. That night, I even slept better. Although it didn't fix everything right away, it helped me a lot because spending time with Stella always makes me feel relaxed and understood. **So even though the mall was far more crowded than I expected, spending that afternoon with Stella actually made me feel much better.**

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| trouble in falling sleep | trouble falling asleep | 固定搭配 have trouble doing something；入睡用 fall asleep。 |
| jewelries | jewelry | jewelry 为不可数名词；英式拼写为 jewellery。两次出现合并记录。 |
| that kind of things women always interested in | the kinds of things women are usually interested in | 调整 kind / things 的数；be interested in 中缺少 are。 |
| I surprisingly found that whole crowd | I was surprised to find that it was extremely crowded | 用 I was surprised 表达自己的惊讶；crowded 直接描述商场状态。 |
| there was like hundreds of people | there were hundreds of people | people 为复数，用 were；like 是口语填充词，可删。 |
| I hardly could talk | I could hardly talk | hardly 通常放在情态动词 could 后。 |
| less people | fewer people | people 可数，此处用 fewer。 |
| shops about jewelries | jewelry shops | 用 jewelry 作名词修饰语，表示珠宝店。 |
| my concern about my work and life | my concerns about work and life | 多方面的担忧用 concerns 更贴切；concern 单数本身并非必然错误。 |
| Stella comfort me | Stella comforted me | 叙述过去发生的行为，用过去式。 |
| Just don't pushing myself so hard | not to push myself too hard | don’t 后用动词原形；转述建议时用 told me not to push。 |
| I can ask her for help whenever I need it. | She said … that I could ask her for help whenever I needed it. | 嵌入过去的转述，保持时态一致。 |
| I just felt like relieving some of my stress | I felt like some of my stress had been relieved | 原句更像“想缓解压力”；修改版表达“感觉压力已经减轻”。 |
| 结尾主要谈 Stella 和压力 | So even though the mall was far more crowded than I expected, spending that afternoon with Stella actually made me feel much better. | 扣题：把陪伴带来的好心情与商场拥挤联系起来；无需机械重复关键词。 |
