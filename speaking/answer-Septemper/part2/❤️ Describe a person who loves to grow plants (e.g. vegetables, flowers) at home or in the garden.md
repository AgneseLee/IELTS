## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a person who loves to grow plants (e.g. vegetables, flowers) at home or in the garden**

> **You should say:**
> - Who this person is
> - What plants he/she grows
> - How he/she grows the plants
> - And explain why he/she loves growing plants

> Bank: B8 | Modules: home garden / daily routine

## 1. Band 7 Answer (minimum changes)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The person would be my best friend Stella. About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. Lying in bed, I couldn't stop thinking about my career, my future, and whether AI might replace all of our jobs. That's why I said yes when Stella invited me to her apartment for a catch-up, because I wanted to take a break and relax a little.

At her place, I saw plenty of plants and flowers. I didn't know all their names, but they all looked healthy and beautiful. She told me she watered them regularly and moved them around to get enough sunlight. She also paid attention to tiny changes, things like new leaves coming out or tomatoes slowly turning red. I remember we just lay on the sofa, talking about music, movies, and the reasons why she enjoyed gardening.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
She said she enjoyed gardening because it reminded her that everything takes time. You can't force a plant to grow faster, right? Instead, you just give it enough water and sunlight and let it grow at its own pace. That's what she enjoys most, watching those small daily changes and feeling a sense of <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">achievement</mark>. She told me that seeing a new leaf or a tomato slowly turn red always makes her happy because she knows that all the time and effort she puts into taking care of her plants is worth it.

Spending time with her and seeing how she treats her plants and flowers made me realize that slowing down doesn't mean falling behind. Sometimes it's actually a healthier way for both people and plants to grow. That's why her love of gardening left such a lasting impression on me.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: Stella moved her plants to get enough sunlight and paid attention to small changes; watching her care for them helped me rethink my own pace of life.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella, my best friend, who grows plants and flowers, including tomatoes, at home.
- Background: About three months ago, I was having trouble sleeping and accepted her invitation to her apartment for a catch-up.
- Main event: I saw her healthy plants and learned how she watered them, moved them for sunlight, and noticed small daily changes.
- Ability shown: She paid attention to her plants and adjusted their position; her patient approach encouraged me to reconsider my own pace.
- Reflection: Slowing down does not mean falling behind; steady care and small changes can bring a sense of achievement.

## 5. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

> 根据本轮提供的原回答节选及 Main mistakes 栏整理；节选未展示的表达仅来自错误汇总，不补写为完整原始回答。“可优化 / 可精简”不表示原表达必然有语法错误。

| 原表达 / 问题 | 最小修改 / 推荐表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| lying back | lying in bed | 意思更准确：lying back 本身可用，但 lying in bed 明确表达当时躺在床上失眠。 |
| she watered them regularly and move them around | she watered them regularly and moved them around | 过去叙述中并列动词保持一致：watered / moved。 |
| tomato slowly turning red | tomatoes slowly turning red | tomato 是可数名词；泛指这些变化可用复数，也可说 a tomato slowly turning red。 |
| lay on sofa | lay on the sofa | 此处 sofa 前需要限定词；lie 的过去式是 lay。 |
| playing gardening | gardening | gardening 表示园艺活动，不搭配 play；也可说 do some gardening。 |
| enjoy the gardening | enjoyed gardening | 本段回忆谈话用 enjoyed；泛指爱好时 gardening 通常不加 the，特指某次活动时可加。 |
| that's what she enjoy most | that's what she enjoys most | she 配 enjoys；这里说她现在仍有的爱好，可以用现在时。 |
| 过去经历与现在仍适用的观点混用 | She said she enjoyed gardening… / slowing down doesn't mean falling behind | 时态按意义选择：过去谈话用 said / enjoyed；持续成立的观点可用现在时，不必全部改成过去时。 |
| a healthier way for people and plants to grow | a healthier way for both people and plants to grow | 可优化：both 强调人与植物两者；不加 both 的原表达也正确。 |
