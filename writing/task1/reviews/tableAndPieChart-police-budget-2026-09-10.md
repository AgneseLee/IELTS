## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement
- [TA] 总预算增加 13.9 million pounds，约 4.6%；“considerable increase / surged” 夸大幅度，改为 “rose modestly”。
- [TA] “with the building and transport remaining unchanged” 未说明不变的是占比。总预算上涨时，17% 对应的金额从约 51.8 million pounds 增至 54.2 million pounds；写 “the share for buildings and transport remained unchanged”，并在正文补上 17%。无需在作文中额外计算金额。
- [TA] 科技金额约从 24.4 million pounds 升到 44.6 million pounds，所以 “spending almost doubled” 并非事实错误；但饼图直接呈现的是 8% → 14%，写 “Technology’s share almost doubled” 更直接准确。
- [TA] “officers’ salaries” 漏掉 staff，完整写 “salaries for officers and staff”。两张饼图应写 “pie charts”。

### Coherence and Cohesion
- [CC] 保留引言、概述、资金来源、支出去向的四段结构，分组合理。
- [CC] ⚠ recurring：“followed by national government” 通常暗示排名随后，与政府资金最多矛盾。笔记中的 “was followed by” 用于排名，不能只当作“接着介绍”。保留地方税先写的顺序时，改用 “while” 或 “By comparison”。
- [CC] 地方税的增长最大而政府拨款最多，两种比较维度应清晰区分；最小调整版用 11.1 million pounds 与 2.3 million pounds 直接支撑增长幅度比较。

### Lexical Resource
- [LR] ⚠ recurring：依据动态图笔记，程度词须匹配数据，预算增长用 “rose modestly”，不要为了替换 increase 而用 “surged”。
- [LR] “highest increasing rate” → “highest growth rate / fastest growth”。本题地方税的相对增幅确实最大（约 12.2%），因此使用增长率本身不是错误；“largest increase” 则直接比较金额增量，也成立。
- [LR] “a small ease” → “a slight increase / edged up”。在数值趋势中 “ease” 通常表示下降或缓和，38 → 38.5 是上升。
- [LR] “formed the largest group of expenditure” → “accounted for the largest share of expenditure”。“group” 适合描述人群，“share/category” 适合此处支出。
- [LR] “took the lead / dominating all the time” 能理解，但 “remained the largest funding source” 更精确自然。
- [LR] 拼写：“techonogly / fatest / follwed” → “technology / fastest / followed”。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy
- [GR] “the pie chart presented” → “the pie charts show”：图表的展示功能用现在时，与 “compares” 一致；历史数据用过去时。
- [GR] “in total police budget” → “in the total police budget”；“national government” 此处通常写 “the national government”，或直接使用 “national government contributions”。
- [GR] “the salaries” → “salaries”；“the building and transport” → “buildings and transport”。图示类别中的 buildings 使用复数。
- [GR] “while / whereas / with” 结构本身可以使用，但概述连续叠加会显得拥挤，可用更短的主句。

Regarding expenditure, the proportion spent on salaries for officers and staff fell from 75% to 69%. By contrast, technology’s share rose from 8% to 14%, almost doubling. Buildings and transport accounted for an unchanged 17% of the budget in both years.
