## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a time when a person did something to help you solve a problem**

> **You should say:**
> - Who the person is
> - What the problem was
> - How he/she helped you
> - And explain how you felt about the experience

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / practical-support / healthier-boundaries

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The person I'd like to talk about is Stella, my former colleague and one of my best friends. About three months ago, my company went through a layoff, and I received a much heavier workload. I began sleeping badly and feeling overwhelmed, but I thought I should handle everything on my own.

Stella noticed that I was struggling and listened to my concerns. She told me she had been through something similar and helped me identify which tasks were urgent and which deadlines were unrealistic. She also suggested talking honestly to my manager and setting healthier boundaries.

I followed her advice and asked my manager to help me prioritize my workload. I also stopped replying to non-urgent messages at night and broke larger tasks into smaller steps I could handle. Within a few weeks, I was sleeping better and felt more in control of my work.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I felt relieved and supported because she took my concerns seriously and gave me practical steps I could follow. Her support changed my view of resilience. I used to think strong people should solve problems on their own and keep working even under huge pressure, but now I think strength includes asking for help, accepting support, and changing direction when things don't go as planned. <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Since then, I've tried to focus on what I can control and never treat exhaustion as an achievement.</mark>
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: Stella helped me identify urgent tasks and unrealistic deadlines, then suggested practical steps to manage my workload.
- Secondary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: She listened to my concerns and encouraged me to talk honestly with my manager instead of handling everything alone.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella, my former colleague and one of my best friends, helped me manage work pressure.
- Background: I was still working at the company after a layoff and received a much heavier workload, which affected my sleep.
- Main event: Stella shared her experience and helped me sort out my tasks. I followed her advice, spoke to my manager, set healthier boundaries, and broke larger tasks into manageable steps.
- Result: Within a few weeks, I was sleeping better and felt more in control of my work.
- Ability shown: Practical problem-solving, active listening, and willingness to ask for support.
- Reflection: I felt relieved and supported, and learned that resilience includes accepting help rather than handling everything alone.
