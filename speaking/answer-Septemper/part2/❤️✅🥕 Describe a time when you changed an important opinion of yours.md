## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a time when you changed an important opinion of yours**

> **You should say:**
> - When you changed your opinion
> - What the original opinion was
> - Why you changed it
> - And explain how you felt about the experience

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / burnout / healthier-boundaries

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I changed an important opinion several months ago when my former company went through a layoff. Before that, I believed capable and strong people should solve serious problems on their own and keep working even under huge pressure. However, one of my best friends, Stella, who was still working at my previous company, received a much heavier workload after the layoff. She began sleeping badly and had trouble falling asleep. She even started showing signs of serious burnout and insomnia. Instead of pretending everything was all right, she honestly talked to her manager and asked him for help. She also pointed out those unrealistic deadlines and stopped replying to non-urgent messages at night. By tackling these issues, she gradually recovered both physically and mentally within a few weeks.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Her example changed my view of resilience and also changed my opinion of what it means to be a capable and strong person. Instead of solving all problems on their own, I now think real strength often includes asking for help, accepting support, and changing direction when things don't go as planned. Since then, I've stopped believing that pushing myself to the limit is the only way to deal with difficulties. Instead, I believe it's more important to focus on what I can control, set healthier boundaries, and take one manageable step at a time. I'm really glad I changed my opinion because I think it will help me handle future career challenges in a much healthier way.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: Stella's recovery led me to revise my belief that strong people should handle difficulties alone.
- Secondary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I learned to focus on what I can control, set healthier boundaries, and take manageable steps.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: I changed my opinion about what it means to be a capable and strong person.
- Background: My former company went through a layoff, and Stella faced a heavier workload and burnout.
