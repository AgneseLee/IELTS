## Cue Card

**Describe a live sport match you have ever watched** ❤️

> **You should say:**
> - What it was
> - When you watched
> - What it was like
> - And explain how you felt about it

- Reason: Watching professional players closely helped me understand tactics that casual television viewing had hidden.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: A professional badminton final in Beijing.
- Background: Stella and I had both played casually at university.
- Main event: One player changed tactics and made a dramatic comeback.
- Ability shown: Learning through observation and sharing ideas clearly.
- Reflection: The live atmosphere deepened my appreciation of the sport.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I watched a professional badminton final in Beijing with my friend Stella last year. We chose it because we had both played casually at university and wanted to see elite players at close range. The arena was crowded and energetic. One player lost the first game and fell behind in the second, but then changed tactics and made a dramatic comeback. Every long rally created enormous tension, and strangers around us cheered together. Although our seats were quite far from the court, hearing the speed of the shuttle made the skill involved feel real.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I felt excited and surprisingly connected to the crowd. Television can show close-up details, but it cannot fully reproduce the warm and relaxed atmosphere of thousands of people responding at the same moment.

The match also became a learning experience. Watching the player's positioning showed me that badminton is not simply about hitting hard. Stella and I discussed the tactics and played again the next weekend. Trying the ideas made us laugh and gave the event a life beyond the arena. Looking back, the final renewed an old interest, strengthened our friendship, and showed that live sport combines observation with shared emotion. I would gladly attend another reasonably priced final.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
