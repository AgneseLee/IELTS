## Original Draft(165)

It is important to recognize artificial intelligence technology produce harmful effects on communication in the real world. A central problem is that it induce people to communicate with digital tools, which offer much convenience and accessibility. For example, people can decide when and where to talk to an AI assistant on phones, whereas contacting people face-to-face requires much realistic conditions, such as appropriate time and places. As a result, people may have less desire to connect with real people offline when they have a more convenient choice online.

Furthermore, digital tool often serve as a comprehensively knowledgeable assistant, who can answer almost all questions people ask, which greatly promotes efficiency of our work and lives. Nevertheless, these techniques cannot help people build real emotional connections with others. Yet it makes people feel distant from each other if they lack sufficient interactive engagement consistently. This is because real connections usually requires deep talks and much shared memories, allowing people to share insights and overcome difficulties together.

## Final Integrated Version(185) (7.0–7.5)

It is important to recognize that artificial intelligence can have harmful effects on communication in the real world. One concern is that the convenience and accessibility of AI assistants may encourage people to use them as substitutes for human conversation. For example, people can choose when and where to talk to an AI assistant on their phones, whereas meeting someone face-to-face involves coordinating schedules and finding a suitable place. When an easier alternative is available, people may become less willing to make the effort to connect with others offline.

This reliance on AI for conversation may also weaken emotional bonds. Digital tools can answer a wide range of questions and help people complete tasks more efficiently, but these practical benefits do not replace the emotional connections formed through human interaction. If conversations with AI regularly take the place of meaningful contact with others, people may gradually feel more distant from one another. Close relationships usually develop through meaningful conversations and shared experiences in which people exchange insights and overcome difficulties together. Spending less time on these activities may therefore make such relationships harder to maintain.
