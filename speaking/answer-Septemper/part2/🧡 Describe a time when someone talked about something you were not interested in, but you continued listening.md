## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a time when someone talked about something you were not interested in, but you continued listening.**

> **You should say:**
> - Who the person was
> - What they talked about
> - Why you continued listening
> - How you felt about the experience

## 1. Band 7 Answer（最新最小改动版）

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed and couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was worried about everything that was bothering me. After several bad nights, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. So when my friend Stella invited me to a party at her apartment in Beijing, I said yes.

At the party, there were about eight or ten people. Some were standing in the living room, while others were sitting on the sofa, talking about things like cars, money, and houses. Honestly, I had zero interest in those topics, but I continued listening because I didn't want to be rude and there wasn't really anyone else I knew well enough to talk to.

Later, I realized that going there might have been a mistake. I kept asking myself why I'd said yes. I felt so out of place. People were making small talk and making noise, but they weren't really connecting with each other. Everyone was speaking, but no one was really listening. I felt lonely in a room full of people. Honestly, I'd rather stay at home alone than experience that kind of loneliness in a crowd. So that was a moment that taught me **not every gathering is worth saying yes to**.

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| asked me to a party in her apartment | invited me to a party at her apartment | invited 更明确；at 强调活动地点，in 强调公寓内部，并非必然错误。 |
| on a party | at the party | 聚会场合用 at；前文已提到这个聚会，用 the。 |
| eight or ten guys | eight or ten people | people 更中性；guys 是口语用法，不宜无条件判错。 |
| Some of people | Some people | some people 或 some of the people。 |
| I realized I made a huge mistake. It turned out to be a huge mistake I made. | I realized I'd made a mistake. / I realized that going there might have been a mistake. | 过去完成时更清楚地表示先作决定，后意识到；避免连续重复同一意思。 |
| As I reflect why I said yes | I kept asking myself why I'd said yes. | reflect on + 内容；回顾过去时也应注意时态。 |
| I was so regret | I really regretted | regret 是动词或名词，此处不能放在 was so 后当形容词。 |
| making sound about something that was very superficial | making small talk / making noise | 原搭配不自然；small talk 指闲聊，本身不一定带贬义。 |
| No one was hearing. They were just listening. | Everyone was speaking, but no one was really listening. | 原句没有表达“大家都在说，却没人认真听”；这里用 listen 表示主动倾听。 |
| a torture | torture | 比喻经历令人煎熬时通常作不可数名词。 |
| I would rather stay at home alone instead of feeling lonely among a lot of people. | I'd rather stay at home alone than experience that kind of loneliness in a crowd. | 优先使用 would rather…than…；in a room full of people / in a crowd 更自然。 |
| 原回答未直接解释为何继续听 | but I continued listening because I didn't want to be rude and there wasn't really anyone else I knew well enough to talk to | 题卡内容补充：这是所提供修改版新增的理由，不属于原始回答。 |

> 核对说明：原回答已经用了 I'll never say，没有 I'll never said，不计为错误。继续倾听的理由只保留在修改版，不补写进原始回答。
