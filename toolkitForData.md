# Task 1 Vocabulary Toolkit

## 第一组：占比、排名与余数 Proportions & Ranking

**Accounted for** — 占据（用于描述具体比例）
> Local taxes accounted for roughly a third of the total income.

**Comprised** — 构成（替代简单的 make up）
> Salaries comprised the largest part of the expenditure in both years.

**The lion's share** — 最大份额
> Officer salaries took the lion's share of the total police budget.

**Occupied the largest portion of** — 占据了最大的部分
> National Government occupied the largest portion of the income sources.

**Was followed by** — 紧随其后（描述排名第二/三）
> This was followed by local taxes, which rose significantly to £102.3m.

**Account for the remaining amount** — 占据剩余数额（替代口语化的 leftover）
> Other sources, such as grants, accounted for the remaining amount of the funding.

**Allocation for sth** — 对某项的拨备/分配（用于预算、资金）
> The allocation for technology was transformed from a mere 8% to a more significant 14%.

## 第二组：趋势、变迁与目标转移 Trends & Shifts

**Upward / Downward trend** — 上升/下降趋势（综述段必用）
> Overall, there was a clear upward trend in total income over the one-year period.

**Increase marginally from... to...** — 轻微增长
> Funding from the National Government increased marginally from £175.5m to £177.8m.

**Rise sharply / Soared** — 急剧上升
> Expenditure on leisure soared by approximately 10% in 50 years.

**Transform from... to...** — 由...转化为...
> Technology transformed from a mere 8% to a more significant 14%.

**Its share saw a slight decline in favor of sth** — 份额略有下降，转而投向...
> Salaries saw a decline in favor of increased technology spending.

**Dropped slightly / Dipped** — 轻微下降
> The figure for salaries dropped slightly from 75% to 69%.

**A dramatic fall / Plummeted** — 骤降
> In 2015, there was a dramatic fall in closures to roughly 700 shops.

**Rebounded** — 反弹
> The number of new shop openings rebounded by roughly 50% by 2014.

## 第三组：稳定性、波动与极值 Stability & Extremes

**Maintain its original share at...** — 保持其原始份额
> Spending on buildings and transport maintained its original share at 17%.

**Remain constant / steady at...** — 保持稳定
> The proportion of expenditure on household goods remained constant throughout the year.

**Peaked at / Bottomed out** — 达到顶峰 / 跌至谷底
> Shop closures peaked at 7,000 in 2011, while openings bottomed out at 3,000 in 2018.

**Fluctuated** — 波动
> The figures for shop openings fluctuated significantly over the next four years.

**Plateaued** — 进入平台期（不升不降）
> The number of openings plateaued at around 4,000 between 2015 and 2017.

**Reversed their relative positions** — 相对位置互换（分析感强，适合 Overall 段）
> The two categories reversed their relative positions, with openings initially dominant but ultimately surpassed by closures.

---

## 第四组：逻辑衔接与分布 Logic & Cohesion

**Respectively** — 分别地（一句话对应多个数据）
> Expenditure on food and leisure stood at 15% and 22%, respectively, in 2018.

**In stark contrast** — 形成鲜明对比（每篇最多用一次）
> In stark contrast, the number of new shop openings showed a wider range of figures.

**Distribution** — 分布
> The pie charts describe the distribution of money spent by the police.
