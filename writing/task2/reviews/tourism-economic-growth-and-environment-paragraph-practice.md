## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 第一段的主链 `tourist spending → business revenue → local employment → living standards` 清楚且切题。
- [TA] `Unlike other approaches simply addressing surface-level issues` 没有说明比较对象是什么，属于无法验证的空泛比较。把它替换为 `tax revenue → reinvestment in infrastructure and public services`，能够继续推进原有经济链。
- [TA] 第二段指出游客数量超过环境承载力会造成污染，但没有明确说明污染为何会使经济收益消失。需要补上 `environmental deterioration → weaker destination appeal → fewer repeat visits → lower tourism revenue`。
- [TA] `this advantage is short-lived` 和 `quickly fades once...` 过于绝对。游客超过承载力会威胁收益，但结果仍取决于政府管理，因此宜使用 `may` 和条件结构。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 两段采用“经济收益—环境限制”的对照结构，段间关系清楚。
- [CC] `Because overcrowded tourism may...` 是只有从属连词、没有主句的句子残缺；删除 `Because` 或把它接到前句。
- [CC] `when these issues accumulate` 与末句 `if the environment keeps deteriorating` 含义重复，却没有说明游客行为和收入如何变化。
- [CC] `it` 在 `allocating proportionate funds towards it` 中指代不清；应直接写明资金投向 `public services`。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `improving more job opportunities` 应改为 `creating more job opportunities`；就业机会是被“创造”，不是被“改善”。
- [LR] `tax income` 在政府财政语境中更自然地写成 `tax revenue`。
- [LR] `greater resource use` 可精确为 `increased resource consumption`，`produce more environmental pollution` 可简化为 `generate more pollution`。
- [LR] `constant environmental pressure` 更自然地写成 `persistent environmental pressure`。
- [LR] `declines the long-term appeal` 搭配和动词用法错误；应写成 `undermines/reduces the long-term appeal`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] ⚠ recurring：`which directly improve local business revenue` 中，`which` 指代前面的旅游消费这一整体事实，谓语应为 `increases`。
- [GR] `individual and families' higher quality of live` 应为 `a higher quality of life for individuals and families`；固定名词是 `life`，不是动词 `live`。
- [GR] `the government dose not` 中 `dose` 是拼写错误，应为 `does`。
- [GR] `local ecosystem` 是单数可数名词，需有限定词；泛指各地生态系统时可写成 `local ecosystems`。
- [GR] `pressure ... declines the appeal` 中 `decline` 通常为不及物动词，不能直接接宾语；改用及物动词 `undermines`。

## Final Integrated Version(148) (7.0–7.5)

Developing tourism can stimulate regional economic growth by bringing new spending into local communities. Visitors pay for accommodation, food and transport, directly increasing revenue for local businesses. As demand rises, these firms hire more residents, which raises household incomes and improves families' quality of life. Tourism also generates tax revenue that local authorities can reinvest in infrastructure and public services. If this cycle is managed effectively, tourism can become a sustainable driver of the local economy.

However, these benefits may be short-lived if visitor numbers exceed a destination's environmental capacity. Overcrowding increases resource consumption, waste and pollution, particularly when local governments fail to expand public services in line with tourist demand. As environmental damage accumulates, the destination becomes less attractive, discouraging repeat visits and eventually reducing tourism revenue. Persistent environmental pressure therefore not only threatens local ecosystems but also undermines the very economic gains that tourism initially creates.
