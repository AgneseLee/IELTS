# IELTS Writing Task 1：LR 与 GRA 错误复盘

*基于 `writing/task1/reviews/` 中的已批改作文整理；新增模式应持续合并到本页。*

## 一、当前水平与核心诊断

| 评分项 | 9 篇平均估分 | 主要瓶颈 |
|---|---:|---|
| Lexical Resource | 约 5.6 | 词义边界不清、搭配不稳定、套词和程度词使用不准确 |
| Grammatical Range and Accuracy | 约 5.5 | 时态、主谓一致、介词、从句结构和被动语态错误反复出现 |

你的主要问题不是“没有高级词汇”，而是**知道一个词的大致意思，却没有掌握它的使用对象和完整搭配**。例如，你会使用 `allocation`、`figure`、`proportion`、`plummet` 和 `bottom out`，但有时会把它们放进不合适的语境或接错介词。

复盘优先级：

1. 先保证句子骨架正确：主语 + 完整谓语 + 正确时态。
2. 再准确选择数据名词：`number / figure / proportion / allocation / ownership / rate of change`。
3. 以完整搭配为单位记忆，而不是孤立地背单词。
4. 最后才增加“高级”趋势词和复杂句。

## 二、Lexical Resource：高频错误归纳

### 1. 数据名词混用——最高优先级

这是最反复、影响也最大的一类 LR 问题。写作前必须先判断 y 轴表示什么。

| 数据类型 | 应使用 | 你的典型错误 | 正确表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 绝对数量 | `number` | `allocation for new shops` | `the number of shop openings` |
| 某个具体数值 | `figure` | `the figure of refrigerator` | `the figure for refrigerator ownership` |
| 百分比或份额 | `proportion / percentage` | `occupied the largest two proportions`（数据为百万户） | `recorded the two highest figures` |
| 预算分配 | `allocation` | `allocation for food`（家庭消费） | `spending on food` |
| 电器拥有率 | `ownership` | `the portion of vacuum cleaner` | `vacuum cleaner ownership` |
| 价格变化率 | `rate of change` | `the rise rate / the price rose` | `the rate of change increased` |

记忆口诀：

> 绝对数量用 `number`，单个数据用 `figure`，百分比用 `proportion`，机构预算用 `allocation`，拥有率用 `ownership`，变化率图用 `rate of change`。

### 2. 单词会用，但搭配和介词不完整

不要只背 `plummet = 暴跌`，要背 `plummet to + 终点`、`plummet by + 幅度`。

| 错误 | 正确搭配 | 用法 |
|---|---|---|
| `plummeted at 23m` | `plummeted to 23 million` | `to` 表示终点 |
| `having bottom out at approximately at 62%` | `bottoming out at approximately 62%` | `at` 表示触底所在水平；同时修正分词和重复介词 |
| `climbed marginally at 50%` | `climbed marginally to 50%` | `to` 表示终点 |
| `remained stable in 17%` | `remained stable at 17%` | `at` 表示稳定水平 |
| `peaked at 33m by 2015` | `peaked at 33 million in 2015` | 明确年份用 `in` |
| `figure of the Philippines` | `figure for the Philippines` | 某主体的数据用 `for` |
| `shared a similar trend as` | `followed a similar trend to` | `similar to` 是固定搭配 |
| `over $100,000 on annual income` | `with an annual income of over $100,000` | 收入表达 |
| `production for sugar` | `production of sugar` | `production of` 是固定搭配 |
| `across to the hall` | `across the hall` | `across` 后直接接地点 |

趋势表达的介词系统：

- `rise / climb / fall / drop / plummet + to + 终点`
- `rise / fall / drop + by + 变化量`
- `peak / bottom out / remain stable + at + 所在水平`
- `from A to B`：同时给出起点和终点
- `in + 具体年份`；`by + 截止时间`

### 3. 词义大致相关，但不适合当前图表

| 你的表达 | 问题 | 更自然的表达 |
|---|---|---|
| `families' weekly income` | 图表展示支出，不是收入 | `weekly household expenditure` |
| `consumption aspects` | 不适合正式预算语境 | `expenditure categories` |
| `complicated transformation` | 太空泛，未说明线的行为 | `fluctuated sharply` |
| `largest urbanization country` | 词性和搭配均不对 | `the most urbanised country` |
| `the closing number` | 名词组合不自然 | `the number of closures` |
| `took the leftover` | 口语且含糊 | `accounted for the remaining amount` |
| `experienced a significant development` | 不适合室内布局变化 | `was extensively modernised and reorganised` |
| `proceeding steps` | `proceeding` 不表示“后续的” | `subsequent / remaining stages` |

原则：词汇的分数来自**准确和自然**，不是来自“看起来高级”。

### 4. 套词过度与语义冗余

你反复使用 `in stark contrast`、`in favor of` 和 `transformed from a mere X to a more significant Y`。这些表达有时掩盖了真正的数据关系。

| 问题表达 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `saw a dramatic decline and plummeted` | `declined dramatically, plummeting to...` |
| `sharp surge` | `surge` 或 `rise sharply` |
| `climbed up to` | `climbed to` |
| `in favor of the prevalence of appliances` | `as appliances became more common` |
| `transformed from a mere 8% to a more significant 14%` | `rose from 8% to 14%` |

`in stark contrast` 每篇最多使用一次，并且只用于最明显的对比。很多时候 `By contrast`、`whereas` 或直接分组描述更自然。

### 5. 程度词与真实幅度不匹配

| 数据变化 | 你的表达 | 更准确的表达 |
|---|---|---|
| 35% → 17% | `substantially` | `dramatically` / `more than halved` |
| 10% → 5% | `marginally` | `considerably` / `halved` |
| 9% → 22% | `considerably` | `more than doubled` |
| 6,000 → 3,000 | `slightly` | `sharply / considerably` |

写程度副词前，先计算相对变化。下降一半不能写 `slightly` 或 `marginally`。

### 6. 词形和拼写降低准确度

反复出现的例子：

- `dramatical` → `dramatic`
- `upward trending` → `upward trend`
- `urbanization country` → `urbanised country`
- `descreased` → `decreased`
- `maginally` → `marginally`
- `neligibly` → `negligibly`
- `occured` → `occurred`
- `throught` → `throughout`
- `up-comming` → `upcoming`
- `Overfall` → `Overall`

不要在考场临时使用自己拼写不确定的“高级词”。优先使用拼写确定、搭配稳定的词。

## 三、Grammatical Range and Accuracy：高频错误归纳

### 1. 时态不统一——最高优先级

你经常在描述过去数据时突然切换到一般现在时。

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `account for` | `accounted for` |
| `the allocation drop` | `the allocation dropped` |
| `the openings figure constantly maintain` | `the number of openings consistently declined` |
| `which are...`（过去图表） | `which were...` |
| `salaries still rank first`（2017–2018） | `salaries still accounted for the largest share` |

按题型预设时态：

| 题型 | 主时态 |
|---|---|
| 已结束年份的图表 | 一般过去时 |
| 含未来预测 | 过去数据用过去时；预测用 `is/are predicted to` |
| 无时间的流程图 | 一般现在时 + 被动语态 |
| 过去与现在的地图 | 过去布局用过去时；当前变化可用现在完成时/一般现在时 |

### 2. 主谓一致与单复数

| 错误 | 修正 | 检查点 |
|---|---|---|
| `seven stages, which involves` | `seven stages, which involve` | `which` 指代复数 `stages` |
| `The most noticeable changes was` | `The most noticeable changes were` | 复数主语配 `were` |
| `household goods maintained its...` | `spending on household goods maintained its...` | 明确真正主语 |
| `slightly changes occur` | `slight changes occurred` | 形容词 + 复数名词 + 过去式 |
| `building and transport maintained its share` | `buildings and transport maintained their share` | 并列主语配 `their` |

写完每句后先找谓语，再反查它的主语是单数还是复数。

### 3. 句子缺少完整谓语

这是地图题和流程图中最明显的问题。

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `the seating area situated at the centre` | `the seating area was situated at the centre` |
| `a cafe and a lecture room located...` | `a cafe and a lecture room are located...` |
| `once sugar canes fully grown` | `once the sugar cane has fully grown` |
| `before go crushing` | `before being crushed` |
| `purifying juice converts into syrup through heated` | `the purified juice is heated and thickens into syrup` |

尤其注意：过去分词不能自动充当完整谓语。`situated`、`located`、`created` 前通常需要 `be`。

### 4. 被动语态结构不稳定

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `has been undergone` | `has undergone` / `has been modernised` | `undergo` 本身是及物动词 |
| `undergo harvest` | `is harvested` | 流程中原料是动作承受者 |
| `go crushing` | `is crushed / is fed into a crusher` | 使用被动结构 |
| `converts into syrup` | `is converted into syrup / thickens into syrup` | 区分被动动作和状态变化 |
| `A computer room is constructed...` | `A computer room has been created...` | 过去到现在的地图变化 |

流程图的安全句型：

> `The raw material is + 过去分词 + 地点/方式, before being + 过去分词.`

例：`The cane is harvested manually or mechanically before being fed into a crusher.`

### 5. 介词错误

介词问题同时影响 LR 的搭配准确度和 GRA 的语法准确度。

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `distribution annual household income` | `distribution of annual household income` |
| `approximately at 62%` | `approximately 62%` / `at approximately 62%` |
| `throughout the most of the year` | `throughout most of the year` |
| `in where the reading room was` | `where the reading room was` |
| `within an evaporator` | `in an evaporator` |
| `share in 17%` | `share at 17%` |

### 6. 冠词、限定词与所有格

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `Philippines` | `the Philippines` |
| `a steepest rise` | `the steepest rise` |
| `over the course of period` | `over the course of the period` |
| `children book area` | `children's book area` |
| `southeastern and northeastern corner` | `the south-eastern and north-eastern corners` |

最高级前通常用 `the`；可数单数名词通常不能“裸奔”，必须检查 `a/an/the/所有格`。

### 7. `which` 从句指代混乱或结构残缺

你倾向于用 `which` 把多个信息塞进一个长句，导致读者无法判断 `which` 指什么。

典型问题：

- `Malaysia surpassed it and became..., which saw a sharp increase...`
- `The figure of Nickel stood in contrast to it, which peaked...`
- `shop closures..., which peaked... and bottomed out...`

改法：

1. 把 `which` 从句紧跟在它修饰的名词后。
2. 如果一句有两个以上趋势动作，优先拆句。
3. 可用分词结构描述连续变化：`peaking at X before falling to Y`。

示例：

> `Malaysia, which experienced a sharp increase, surpassed the Philippines by 2020.`

> `Shop closures fluctuated sharply, peaking at about 7,100 in 2013 before collapsing in 2015.`

### 8. 并列、比较与标点

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `in contrast to leisure soared` | `in contrast to leisure, which soared` / `whereas leisure soared` |
| `became slower than they were` | `decelerated compared with their January levels` |
| `stood..., recorded...` | 用 `and` 连接，或拆成两句 |
| `But it continued...` | `This was followed by...` / 与前句合并 |
| `manually ,` | `manually,` |

比较时必须比较同类对象：`the 2020 figure was higher than the 2000 figure`，不能让“速度”与“年份”或“国家”直接比较。

## 四、你的错误模式：从根源理解

### 模式 A：先想中文，再逐词替换

例如“收入水平”“消费方面”“后续步骤”被直译为 `income level`、`consumption aspects`、`proceeding steps`。解决方法不是背更多同义词，而是积累题型专属的英语词块：

- 柱图：`income bracket / spending category / accounted for`
- 线图：`the number of / ownership / rate of change / stabilised at`
- 地图：`was replaced by / adjacent to / along the northern edge`
- 流程图：`is harvested / is fed into / is passed through / is placed in`

### 模式 B：为了展示复杂度而写过长句

长句中容易同时出现时态、主谓一致、指代和标点错误。Band 7 并不要求每句都复杂；它要求**有一定复杂句范围，同时多数句子准确**。

建议每个主体使用以下三步：

1. 主题句：说明总体方向。
2. 数据句：用 `from A to B` 给出证据。
3. 比较句：用 `whereas / compared with / respectively` 建立关系。

### 模式 C：把“高级”误认为“复杂和夸张”

`transformed`、`in stark contrast`、`lion's share` 并非越多越好。准确的 `rose from 8% to 14%` 通常优于不自然的长套语。

## 五、建议重点背诵的正确句型

### 图表通用

- `The figure rose from A to B between YEAR and YEAR.`
- `After peaking at A in YEAR, the figure fell to B.`
- `The figure bottomed out at A before recovering to B.`
- `X accounted for the largest proportion, followed by Y.`
- `X and Y reached A and B, respectively.`
- `By contrast, X remained stable at approximately A.`

### 绝对数量与收入

- `The number of households in the highest income bracket rose to 33 million.`
- `Households with an annual income of over $100,000 recorded the highest figure.`

### 支出与预算

- `Spending on food fell dramatically from 35% to 17%.`
- `Salaries accounted for the lion's share of total expenditure.`
- `The allocation for technology rose from 8% to 14%.`

### 流程图

- `Once the sugar cane has fully grown, it is harvested manually or mechanically.`
- `The harvested cane is fed into a crusher to extract its juice.`
- `The purified juice is heated in an evaporator until it thickens into syrup.`

### 地图

- `The library has undergone extensive modernisation.`
- `The former reading room has been replaced by a computer room.`
- `The entrance has maintained its original position.`

## 六、四周复盘计划

### 第 1 周：数据名词 + 介词

- 每天默写 `number / figure / proportion / allocation / ownership / rate of change` 的适用对象。
- 每天完成 10 组 `to / by / at / from...to...` 填空。
- 重写所有含 `plummet`、`bottom out`、`peak`、`remain stable` 的错句。

### 第 2 周：时态 + 主谓一致

- 从旧作文中每天抽 5 句，只标出主语和谓语。
- 把过去图表全部统一为过去时。
- 流程图只练一般现在时被动语态。

### 第 3 周：句子拆分 + `which` 指代

- 每天挑 3 个旧长句，拆成两个准确句子。
- 再尝试用 `whereas`、`before + -ing` 或 `which` 合并一次。
- 合并后检查：`which` 紧邻先行词了吗？句中有完整谓语吗？

### 第 4 周：限时输出 + 错误统计

- 隔天写一篇 Task 1，控制在 20 分钟。
- 每篇只统计本笔记中的 8 类 GRA 错误和 6 类 LR 错误。
- 同类错误连续三篇不再出现后，再增加新词汇和复杂句。

## 七、考场 90 秒检查清单

### Lexical Resource

- [ ] y 轴究竟是数量、比例、预算、拥有率，还是变化率？
- [ ] 是否误用了 `figure of`、`allocation` 或 `proportion`？
- [ ] `to` 是终点、`by` 是幅度、`at` 是水平吗？
- [ ] 程度副词与实际变化幅度一致吗？

- [ ] 每个句子都有明确主语和完整谓语吗？
- [ ] 全文时态符合图表时间吗？

## 八、下一篇作文的最低目标

下一篇先不要刻意追求更多生词。只完成以下目标：

1. 数据名词零误用。
2. `to / by / at` 零误用。
3. 过去图表全篇时态统一。
4. 每句都有完整谓语，主谓一致。
5. 全文最多使用一次 `in stark contrast`。
6. 至少准确使用一次比较句和一次分词结构。

当以上六项能够稳定做到时，你的 LR 和 GRA 才更有可能从 5.5–6.0 稳定提升到 6.5，之后再通过更灵活的词汇和复杂句冲击 7.0 以上。
