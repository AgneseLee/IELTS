## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] `governments → regulations → companies reduce emissions → less pollution → public health and natural resources` 是一条相关且可发展的解决方案逻辑链。
- [TA] `companies are required ... due to relevant regulations` 只是重复“法规要求企业”，没有解释严格执行为何有效。最小补充是明确 `legal obligations` 与 `penalties for non-compliance`。
- [TA] `cut carbon emissions` 不能直接等同于改善本地空气质量。二氧化碳主要与气候变化相关；若结论是 `local air quality`，原因应准确写成减少 `harmful industrial emissions/pollutants`。
- [TA] `preserving its benefits` 逻辑方向错误：环境问题没有需要保留的益处。应改为该措施带来持续的环境收益。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 第二句已经表达“污染减少并改善空气”，第三句 `This ensures air pollution is dealt with...` 基本重复，没有推进论证。
- [CC] `which` 在长句中可能指向 `regulations` 或整个前句，导致改善空气质量的直接原因不够清楚。拆成“监管机制”和“环境结果”两句更稳。
- [CC] 末句的 `it` 与 `its` 指代不清；用 `this approach` 明确回指政府监管。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `set up regulations` 不如 `introduce regulations` 自然；“严格执行”可写成 `strictly enforce regulations`。
- [LR] `quality of local atmosphere` 应改为固定搭配 `local air quality`。
- [LR] `industrial pollution is discharged` 搭配生硬；具体写成 `industrial pollutants are released into the air` 更准确。
- [LR] `deal with air pollution in a concrete way` 含义较空泛，可用明确结果 `reduce residents' exposure to harmful pollutants` 代替。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `improves quality of local atmosphere` 缺限定词；即使保留原结构也应写成 `improves the quality of the local atmosphere`。
- [GR] `improving public health and protect the natural resources` 并列形式不一致，应为 `improving public health and protecting natural resources`。`natural resources` 泛指时不加 `the`。

## Summary of Corrections

### 审题错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| 把减少碳排放直接等同于改善本地空气质量 | 改为减少有害工业排放或污染物 | 二氧化碳与本地空气污染物并非同一概念 |
| `preserving its benefits` | 监管措施带来持续环境收益 | 环境问题本身没有应被保留的益处 |

### 语法 & 拼写

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `improves quality of local atmosphere` | `improves local air quality` |
| `improving public health and protect the natural resources` | `improving public health and protecting natural resources` |

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `set up ... regulations` | `introduce ... regulations` | 法规通常用 `introduce` 表示制定或出台 |
| `quality of local atmosphere` | `local air quality` | 后者是自然的固定搭配 |
| `industrial pollution is discharged` | `industrial pollutants are released into the air` | 对工业污染物排放的表达更具体、自然 |
| `dealt with in a concrete way` | `reduce residents' exposure to harmful pollutants` | 用可观察的结果替代空泛评价 |

### 结构改进

保留“政府制定并执行法规—企业减排—环境与健康受益”的原有顺序，但在法规和减排之间补充明确的执行机制，并删除重复的结果句。这样每个句子都会推进因果链。

## Final Integrated Version(67) (7.0–7.5)

Governments can tackle air pollution by introducing and rigorously enforcing environmental regulations. Binding emissions limits give companies clear reduction obligations, while inspections and penalties discourage non-compliance. As firms adopt cleaner processes, fewer harmful pollutants are released into the air, improving local air quality and reducing residents' exposure to them. Properly implemented, such regulation addresses industrial pollution directly and creates lasting benefits for public health and natural resources.

## Original Logic and Order — Expression-only Version(75) (Estimated Band 6.5)

This is effective because companies are required to **reduce harmful emissions under these regulations, thereby directly improving local air quality as fewer industrial pollutants are released**. If implemented properly, **this approach** can address the environmental problem while **delivering benefits** such as improving public health and **protecting natural resources**.

### 结论与卡点

仅修正表达后，现实上限约为 Band 6.5，尚不足以稳定达到 7.0–7.5。

- [TA] `under these regulations` 仍只是重复法规要求减排，没有说明“严格执行”如何改变企业行为。
- [CC] 第三句只是再次声称空气污染得到处理，与第二句结果重复，没有继续推进因果链。
- [CC] 一句内同时放入企业义务、污染减少和空气改善，因果层次不够清楚。
- [LR] 与 [GR] 的主要语言错误已修正，不再是核心卡点。

## 解决卡点问题

| 卡点 | 最小幅度的调整 |
|---|---|
| 严格执行与企业减排之间缺少机制 | 补充明确的法律义务与违规处罚 |
| 空气污染结果重复 | 用居民接触有害污染物减少这一具体影响替代空泛重复 |
| 单句承载过多因果环节 | 把监管机制与环境结果分成两个句子 |

### Minimally Adjusted Version(73) (Target Band 7.0–7.5)

A realistic approach to addressing the issue is **for governments to introduce and strictly enforce environmental regulations**. **These regulations give companies clear legal obligations to reduce harmful emissions and impose penalties for non-compliance, so fewer industrial pollutants are released into the air.** **This directly improves local air quality and reduces residents' exposure to harmful pollutants.** If implemented properly, **this approach can therefore protect public health and natural resources while producing lasting environmental improvements**.
