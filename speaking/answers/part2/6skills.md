# Six Transferable Skills

## 01 / 06 — Communication Skills

### Core idea

The ability to express ideas clearly, understand others and build meaningful connections.

### Keywords

express my ideas clearly • communicate effectively • explain my thoughts • share my opinions • listen actively • understand different perspectives • avoid misunderstandings • build trust

### Example usages

- This experience improved my ability to communicate effectively and express my ideas clearly.
- I learned that good communication is not only about speaking, but also about listening and understanding others.
- It taught me how to explain my thoughts clearly and avoid unnecessary misunderstandings.
- This experience helped me build stronger relationships with people around me.
- I realised that effective communication plays an important role in both personal and professional life.

## 02 / 06 — Problem-solving Skills

### Core idea

The ability to identify problems, analyse situations and find practical solutions.

### Keywords

identify problems • analyse situations • find practical solutions • think critically • make informed decisions • overcome challenges • take effective action • focus on what I can control

### Example usages

- This experience improved my ability to analyse problems and find practical solutions.
- I learned that challenges can be handled by breaking them down into smaller steps.
- It taught me to stay calm and focus on what I can control.
- I became better at making informed decisions when facing uncertainty.
- This experience showed me that problems are opportunities to improve my problem-solving skills.

## 03 / 06 — Learning Ability

### Core idea

The ability to acquire knowledge, develop skills and improve continuously.

### Keywords

learn new skills • acquire knowledge • develop expertise • improve through practice • explore new ideas • expand my knowledge • keep learning • learn from experience

### Example usages

- This experience encouraged me to develop a habit of continuous learning.
- I learned how to acquire new knowledge more effectively.
- It helped me improve my skills through consistent practice.
- The most valuable thing was the progress I made throughout the learning process.
- It reminded me that abilities can be developed over time with effort and persistence.

## 04 / 06 — Adaptability

### Core idea

The ability to adjust my approach when facing new situations or uncertainty.

### Keywords

adapt to changes • adjust my approach • remain flexible • deal with uncertainty • respond to unexpected situations • step out of my comfort zone • become more resilient

### Example usages

- This experience improved my ability to adapt to unfamiliar situations.
- I learned that flexibility is often more important than having a perfect plan.
- It taught me how to adjust my approach when things did not go as expected.
- I became more confident when dealing with uncertainty and unexpected changes.
- This experience helped me become more resilient and open-minded.

## 05 / 06 — Leadership & Collaboration

### Core idea

The ability to work with others, coordinate tasks and take responsibility.

### Keywords

work effectively with others • coordinate with team members • respect different opinions • take responsibility • organise tasks • support others • build trust • motivate people

### Example usages

- This experience taught me the importance of teamwork and shared responsibility.
- I learned how to coordinate with others and achieve a common goal.
- It improved my ability to respect different opinions and cooperate with people.
- I realised that effective teamwork requires trust, communication and responsibility.
- This experience helped me become a better team member and take more responsibility.

## 06 / 06 — Creativity & Innovation

### Core idea

The ability to generate new ideas, explore possibilities and create better solutions.

### Keywords

think creatively • come up with new ideas • explore possibilities • find innovative solutions • turn ideas into reality • challenge traditional thinking • improve existing methods

### Example usages

- This experience encouraged me to think creatively and explore new possibilities.
- I learned how to come up with new ideas and turn them into practical solutions.
- It changed the way I approach problems by encouraging innovative thinking.
- I realised that creativity is not only about artistic ability, but also about solving problems differently.
- This experience helped me become more open-minded and willing to try new approaches.
