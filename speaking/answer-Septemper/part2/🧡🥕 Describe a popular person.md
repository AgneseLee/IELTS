## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a popular person**

> **You should say:**
> - Who this person is
> - What kind of person he or she is
> - When you see him/her normally
> - And explain why you think this person is popular

> Bank: B6 | Modules: badminton-player / TV-interview / tactical-adjustment

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I'd like to describe a well-known Chinese badminton player. I first heard of him several years ago because he'd won many trophies. Some of my friends are his fans too. I normally see him on TV or watch his matches online. About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep and kept worrying about my career and future. One night, I came across an interview with him on TV. He described changing tactics during a match. He came across as calm and determined, and his story made me rethink my situation.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I think he's popular because he wins international tournaments and shows fans how to respond to setbacks. What I admire most is his willingness to change a strategy that isn't working.

My takeaway from that interview was "slow is fast." It made me think about my sleep problems and the pressure I put on myself. I realised I could apply a similar approach to my work. When I'm struggling with my workload, I now pause and try a different approach instead of repeating the same mistake. Watching him helped me see that staying determined can mean changing direction when things don't go as planned. That's why I enjoy watching his matches.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I applied the player's willingness to change tactics to my workload, pausing and trying a different approach instead of repeating mistakes.
