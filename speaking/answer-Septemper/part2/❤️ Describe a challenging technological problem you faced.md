I think I should also figure out other ways to deal with my sleep problems rather than completely relying on an app.

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I I just literally | I literally | 去掉重复和堆叠的强调词。 |
| which can help / it can play | which helped / it could play | 叙述当时使用应用的经历，保持过去时态。 |
| play soft music by my ear | play soft music to help me relax | 直接描述音乐的作用更自然。 |
| I didn't have any idea about it | I had no idea what was wrong | 明确表示不知道应用出了什么问题。 |
| concerned about I'm gonna… | worried that I might… | about 后不能直接接这样的完整陈述句；worried that 可接从句。 |
| couldn't fall asleep a whole night | might stay up all night / wouldn't be able to fall asleep at all | 表达整晚可能无法入睡。 |
| a disaster, a trauma | 修改版省略 | 此处用 trauma 语气过重；说明担心整晚睡不着即可，不必换成另一个强烈词。 |
| from App Store | from the App Store | 专有名称前用 the。 |
| found… and download it | found… and downloaded it | 并列的过去动作保持时态一致。 |
| can be more prodigal and easy to use | was more practical and easier to use | prodigal 意为挥霍的；此处用 practical，比较结构用 easier。 |
| couldn't become so over-reliant | shouldn't become too reliant | 表达反思和建议，用 shouldn’t；too reliant 更简洁。 |
| I gonna figure out | I should figure out | 原句缺少 am；此处按反思语境改为 should。 |
