## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a building you enjoy visiting (e.g. a library, a shopping mall, etc.)**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - How you knew it
> - What it looks like
> - And explain why you enjoy visiting it

## 1. Better Version（复用拥挤商场答案）

**A building I enjoy visiting is a shopping mall near my apartment in Beijing. I first noticed it while walking around the neighbourhood. It's a large building with several floors, with clothes shops, jewelry shops and restaurants inside.**

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed and couldn't stop thinking about my career and my future. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. I wanted to make a change and take a break from my daily routine. So that's why I invited Stella, my best friend, to hang out at that shopping mall.

I intended to buy some clothes and jewelry. But when I arrived there, I was surprised to find that it was extremely crowded. There were hundreds of people on the first floor, and I could hardly talk to Stella because it was so noisy. So we went up to the top floor, where there were fewer people. We browsed some shops there, and luckily, there were some jewelry shops and restaurants. We had lunch there, and I ended up buying a fancy bracelet.

During lunch, our conversation drifted into my concerns about work and life. Stella comforted me by telling me that “slow is fast” and not to push myself too hard. She said she would always stand by me and that I could ask her for help whenever I needed it. After hearing what she said, I felt really understood and supported. Somehow, I felt like some of my stress had been relieved. That night, I even slept better.

**That's why I enjoy visiting this mall. It's close to home, and I can browse the shops, have a meal and spend time with Stella there. Although the lower floors can get crowded, the top floor gives us a quieter place to sit and talk. For me, it's a place where I can take a break from my daily routine and enjoy some time with a friend.**
