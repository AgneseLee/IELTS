## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a boring place**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - Who you went there with
> - What you did there
> - And explain why you think it is a boring place

> Bank: B7 | Modules: office-skyscraper / city-view / daily-comfort

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I stopped focusing on the delayed meeting and used the waiting time to organise my notes and adjust my approach.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: An office skyscraper near my former workplace in Beijing.
- Background: Stella and I went there for a client meeting that was delayed for nearly two hours.
- Main event: We waited in the bright atrium, bought coffee, checked emails, and watched people enter crowded lifts.
- Ability shown: I adjusted my approach and used the delay to organise our meeting notes.
- Reflection: The wait taught me that an impressive building can still feel boring when visitors have no purpose or freedom to explore.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The boring place I remember is an office skyscraper near my former workplace in Beijing. I went there with Stella, my former colleague, for a client meeting. It has a glass exterior, a bright lobby, and upper floors with a wide city view. However, our meeting was delayed for nearly two hours. We bought coffee, checked our emails, and sat in the lobby. Visitors could <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">not explore</mark> the upper floors without a pass, and the lifts were crowded. After a while, every activity felt <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">repetitive</mark>. I found the whole experience boring.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
The building looked impressive but gave us <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">nothing meaningful</mark> to do. Still, the experience improved my <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">adaptability</mark>. Instead of complaining about the delay, I adjusted my approach and organised our meeting notes with Stella. That gave the waiting time a clear purpose and helped us feel calmer. Looking back, the experience <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">changed</mark> how I judge buildings. A dramatic skyline and practical comfort can make a place attractive, but they do not automatically make it <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">interesting</mark>. What <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">matters</mark> is how people can use the space. That is why my opinion is <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">mixed</mark>: I admire its design and city view, but the long wait was dull.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
