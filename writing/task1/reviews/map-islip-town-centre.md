## Prompt

The maps below show the centre of a small town called Islip as it is now, and plans for its development.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

## Original Draft(248)

The two maps illustrate a planned development of a small town called Islip, compared with its current structure.

Overall, the town center will be substantially modernized and reorganized in the future, transforming from a linear layout into a circled one with a surrounding dual carriageway. A large number of public facilities, such as bus station, shopping centre, car park and park, are planned to be built, with some new housing along with them. Several features that will remain unchanged are the part of shops, the school and original housing, while the northern countryside will be removed.

Currently, Main road runs along the northern part of the area, flanked by plenty of shops, separating housing from the countryside at the northern edge. Housing is located immediately to its south. A school and a park both connect southwards to Main road,  situated west and east of the area of housing.

Looking at the future plan, Main road running eastwards and the coutryside will be demolished to make way for a new circled road with a dual carriageway. Encircled by it, there will be a new bus station, a shopping center, a car park, as well as a pedestrain path, where the nothern shops are now located. Housing is planned to be expanded, flanking shops with the northern part extending westwards of the new road, while the park nearby will shrink to make way for housing. In contrast, the school and housing located at southest edge will maintain their original positions.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] “Main road ... will be demolished” 不准确；东西两端道路仍保留，中央路段将改为 “a pedestrian precinct”，外围才是新增的双车道环路。
- [TA] “bus station, shopping centre, car park and park ... are planned to be built” 把现有公园误写成新建设施；公园会保留但面积缩小。
- [TA] “where the northern shops are now located” 不能同时修饰步行区和全部新设施。北排商店将被公交站、购物中心、停车场及新住宅取代，步行区则位于两排商店之间的原主路位置。
- [TA] 新住宅有两处，分别位于环路内的东北部和东南部；“the northern part extending westwards of the new road” 与图示不符。
- [TA] 现有学校、南排商店、环路内西南侧住宅及南部住宅基本保留，这些稳定项可在概述中准确分组。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 现状段应按“中央主路与商店—北部乡村—南部住宅、学校和公园”的空间顺序展开。
- [CC] 规划段先写道路和步行区，再写北侧替换，最后写住宅、公园与保留项，位置关系会更清晰。
- [CC] “In contrast” 可用于引出保留项，但前一句必须先完成对新增住宅和公园缩小的准确描述。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] “a circled one / a new circled road” → “a centre encircled by a ring-shaped dual carriageway”：`circled` 不能自然描述道路形状。
- [LR] “the part of shops” → “the southern row of shops”：应明确指出保留的是哪一排商店。
- [LR] “connect southwards to Main road” → “lie to the south of the main road”：地图仅显示方位，公园并未直接连到主路。
- [LR] “a large number of public facilities” → “several facilities”：只有少数几项设施，`a large number` 夸大数量。
- [LR] `coutryside / pedestrain / nothern / southest` → `countryside / pedestrian / northern / south-eastern`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] “such as bus station...” → “such as a bus station...”：单数可数名词需要冠词。
- [GR] “A school and a park both connect..., situated...” 修饰关系悬空；改为 “A school and a park are situated ... respectively”。
- [GR] “Main road” → “the main road”：普通道路名称在此不是专名，需要定冠词且无需大写。
- [GR] “will maintain their original positions” → “will remain in their original positions”：`remain in` 更适合描述位置不变。
- [GR] “housing located at southest edge” → “housing located along the southern edge”：方位名词前需加冠词和正确介词。

## Final Integrated Version(178) (7.0–7.5)

The maps compare the current layout of Islip town centre with its proposed redevelopment.

Overall, the centre will be reorganised around a ring-shaped dual carriageway, while the main road will become a pedestrian precinct. New facilities and housing will replace the northern shops and countryside, whereas the school, southern shops and existing housing will remain.

At present, the main road runs east to west through the centre, with shops lining both sides and countryside beyond the northern row. Housing occupies the area to the south, while a school and park are situated to the south-west and east of this residential area, respectively.

Under the plan, the dual carriageway will encircle the centre and connect the eastern and western approaches. The northern shops will be replaced, from west to east, by a bus station, shopping centre, car park and new housing. The southern shops and housing to their south-west will remain inside the ring, while further homes will be added in the south-east and the park reduced. The school and southernmost housing will retain their positions outside the ring.
