**❤️ Describe an environmental law you would like your country to introduce**

> **You should say:**
> - What law it should be
> - Why people should follow the law
> - Whether the law will be popular
> - And explain how you feel about this law

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I used what I learned from an ocean documentary to design a focused law with practical alternatives.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: A ban on single-use plastic in protected coastal areas.
- Background: I watched a British ocean documentary after seeing a sea turtle while freediving near Semporna.
- Main event: Coastal businesses would offer reusable alternatives, while tour operators would bring their waste back to shore.
- Ability shown: I turned the programme's explanation of marine plastic into a focused law with practical steps.
- Reflection: I strongly support the law, provided small businesses receive affordable alternatives during the transition.
