## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a noisy place you have been to**

> **You should say:**
> - Where this place is
> - When you usually go there
> - What kinds of noises you can hear there
> - And explain how you feel about this place

## 1. Better Version（最新最小改动版）

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed and couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. I wanted to make a change and take a break from my daily routine. So that's why I said yes when Stella invited me to a party at her apartment in Beijing.

At the party, there were about eight or ten people. Some were standing in the living room, while others were sitting on the sofa, talking about things like cars, money, houses, and someone's boyfriend or girlfriend—those kinds of boring things. Honestly, I had zero interest in any of those topics. Everyone was talking at the same time, and some people were laughing loudly. It was so hard to follow a conversation because their voices sometimes overlapped. I could barely hear the background music because the apartment was so noisy.

Honestly, I felt so out of place. All that noise made it difficult for me to genuinely relax, especially when I was already stressed and sleeping badly. People were talking, but they weren't really connecting with each other. Everyone was speaking, but no one was really listening. It felt like they were just pretending to be social. I felt lonely in a room full of people. Honestly, I'd rather stay at home alone than experience that kind of loneliness in a crowd. So that was a moment that taught me that **not every gathering is worth saying yes to. I really regretted it.**

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| anything that was bothering me | everything that was bothering me | everything 更贴合“所有让我烦恼的事”；属于意思和自然度调整。 |
| I wanna make a change. I wanna take a break… | I wanted to make a change and take a break… | 叙述过去的想法，用 wanted to；wanna 是 want to 的口语形式。 |
| that kind of boring things | those kinds of boring things | 保持指示词、kind 和 thing 的数一致；也可说 that kind of boring thing。 |
| all of these topics I had zero interest in | I had zero interest in any of those topics | 原句可作话题前置；修改后语序更顺畅，不是硬性语法错误。 |
| their voice sometimes overlapped | their voices sometimes overlapped | 多人的声音相互重叠，用复数 voices。 |
| I couldn't tell whether the background music was playing | I could barely hear the background music | 原句语法成立；修改版表达“几乎听不见音乐”，与“不确定有没有播放”意思有区别。 |
| I'd rather stay at home, honestly. I'd rather experience that kind of loneliness in a crowd. | I'd rather stay at home alone than experience that kind of loneliness in a crowd. | 原文第二句反转了本意；用 would rather A than B 明确更愿意待在家里。 |
| not every gathering worth saying yes to | not every gathering is worth saying yes to | worth 前缺少系动词 is。 |
