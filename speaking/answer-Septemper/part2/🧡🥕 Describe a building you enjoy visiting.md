## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a building you enjoy visiting (e.g. a library, a shopping mall, etc.)**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - How you knew it
> - What it looks like
> - And explain why you enjoy visiting it

## 1. Better Version（最新最小改动版）

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. Anyway, that's why I talked to my best friend Stella and invited her to hang out at a nearby shopping mall, where we occasionally spend time together. I wanted to take a break from my daily routine and relax a little.

**The mall itself is quite modern and spacious, with several floors. There are lots of clothing stores, restaurants, and some jewelry shops as well. It's also very bright and easy to walk around.** That day, I intended to buy some clothes and jewelry, you know, the kind of things women are usually interested in. After shopping for a while, I ended up buying a fancy bracelet on the top floor of the mall, and then we had lunch there.

During lunch, our conversation drifted into my concerns about my work and life. Stella comforted me by telling me to slow down and not push myself so hard. She also told me that she would always stand by my side whenever I needed her. After hearing what she said, I really felt understood and supported, because I used to think I had to finish every task and handle every challenge immediately. I thought that would prove that I was capable and competent enough, but it actually made me feel exhausted and nervous. After talking to Stella, I felt like some of my stress had been relieved. That night, I even slept better.

**So I think that's why I enjoyed visiting the building so much. What I valued most wasn't the shopping itself, but the time I spent with Stella.**

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I lie | I would lie / I lay | 过去习惯用 would lie；lay 是 lie 的过去式。 |
| usually and occasionally | occasionally | 两个频率词含义冲突。 |
| take a relaxing breathe | relax a little | breathe 是动词，breath 才是名词；此处直接用 relax 更自然。 |
| that kind of things women usually interested in | the kind of things women are usually interested in | 补上 are；也可用 those kinds of things。 |
| at a top floor | on the top floor | 楼层用 on；特指顶楼用 the。 |
| telling me that slow is down, don't push myself so hard | telling me to slow down and not push myself so hard | 转述建议用 tell someone to do / not to do。 |
| it would always stand by my side whenever I need it | she would always stand by my side whenever I needed her | 指 Stella 用 she / her；过去转述用 needed。 |
| which can prove myself capable and competent enough | I thought that would prove that I was capable and competent enough | 明确想法与时态；prove myself capable 本身可成立。 |
| clothes shops | clothing stores | 属于表达替换；clothes shops 在英式英语中自然且正确。 |
| What I value most wasn't | What I valued most wasn't | 讲这次经历时保持过去时。 |
| 缺少明确建筑外观描述 | modern and spacious, with several floors; bright and easy to walk around | 采用用户提供修改版中的补充，不写入原始口述。 |
