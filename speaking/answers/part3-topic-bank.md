# IELTS Speaking Part 3 核心观点卡

> 用 22 个可组合模块覆盖 Part 3。社会议题逻辑来自 [`../../writing/task2/views-v2.md`](../../writing/task2/views-v2.md)；人物与经历题复用现有六项综合能力。只记逻辑链和关键词，不背整篇答案。
>
> 优先级后缀：`❤️` = 高频，`💙` = 中频，`💚` = 低频。

## 七类问法

| 编码 | 问法 | 最短展开 |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 原因 | 原因 → 机制 → 结果 |
| Q2 | 比较 | A 特征 → B 特征 → 差异来源 |
| Q3 | 今昔变化 | 过去 → 驱动力 → 现在 |
| Q4 | 未来预测 | 趋势 → 驱动力 → 限制 |
| Q5 | 评价、利弊、重要性 | 主判断 → 主要影响 → 限定 |
| Q6 | 解决、责任 | 责任方 → 措施 → 为什么有效 |
| Q7 | 描述、列举 | 类别 → 代表例子 → 选择依据 |

## 最短回答骨架

普通问题以自然口述 30–45 秒（4–6 句）为目标，并按问法控制结构：

- **Q5 默认 6 句：**立场 → 主链机制 → `For example` / `For instance` 贴题短例 → reflection → `However` + 反向链或必要限制 → `Overall` + 总结。
- **Q2 默认 5 句：**明确差异 → A、B 特征及差异来源 → 贴题短例 → reflection → 总结。A、B 已构成两面，不额外添加反方。
- **Q1/Q3/Q4/Q6/Q7 默认 5 句：**直接回答 → 主链机制 → 贴题短例 → reflection → 总结。只有反向链与题目直接相关时，才在总结前加第六句。

每题必须有贴题短例，例句后必须紧接一句不兼作总结的 reflection，说明例子如何印证逻辑链。可自动生成常识性的具体人物、场景、物品、地点、活动或类别；Q7 可用这些例子直接完成列举，但选择依据和解释机制必须来自观点卡。例子和 reflection 不得承担卡片外的新核心原因、事实判断或统计结论。反方或限制必须回指已选卡片的现有反向链或限制；没有可回指内容时不得临时编写。

## M01-💚 便利与效率

- **正向链：** save time and effort → complete tasks more easily → improve efficiency
- **反向链：** excessive convenience → less active effort → greater dependence
- **适用：** 科技、App、购物、交通、远程工作、网络服务

## M02-💚 公平与机会

- **正向链：** wider access to basic resources → narrower opportunity gaps → greater social mobility
- **反向链：** unequal access → fewer opportunities → entrenched disadvantage
- **适用：** 教育、医疗、住房、公共设施、博物馆、城乡差异

## M03-❤️ 🥚学习与技能

- **正向链：** regular practice and feedback → stronger practical skills → better performance or employability
- **反向链：** passive or exam-driven learning → limited application → weaker independent thinking
- **适用：** 学校、自学、语言、职业培训、儿童发展、天赋

## M04-💙🥚 健康与福祉

- **正向链：** healthy habits and early support → fewer serious problems → better long-term well-being
- **反向链：** inactivity or chronic pressure → poor sleep and anxiety → illness or burnout
- **适用：** 运动、睡眠、工作、食物、休闲、心理健康

## M05-💙 沟通与信任

- **正向链：** clear expression and active listening → fewer misunderstandings → stronger trust
- **反向链：** unclear or impersonal communication → uncertainty → weaker relationships
- **适用：** 消息、建议、语言、朋友、家庭、团队、领导

## M06-💙 规则与责任

- **正向链：** clear and fair rules → predictable consequences → more responsible behaviour
- **反向链：** excessive or inconsistent rules → resistance and unfairness → weaker trust
- **适用：** 法律、学校纪律、手机规则、环保政策、儿童责任

## M07-💚 成本与资源

- **正向链：** targeted spending → better essential services → wider public benefit
- **反向链：** high costs or poor allocation → financial pressure → less funding for other needs
- **适用：** 政府投入、医疗研究、博物馆、住房、创业、建筑

## M08-💙 信息与判断

- **正向链：** reliable information from varied sources → informed judgement → better decisions
- **反向链：** misinformation or overload → distorted judgement → poor choices
- **适用：** 新闻、建议、计划、广告、网络、决策、教育

## M09-❤️ 关系与归属感

- **正向链：** shared time and mutual support → emotional connection → stronger relationships
- **反向链：** limited interaction or constant distraction → emotional distance → weaker belonging
- **适用：** 朋友、家庭、社区、宠物、拜访、团队

## M10-💙 创造力与探索

- **正向链：** exposure to ideas and open-ended practice → new perspectives → greater creativity
- **反向链：** rigid instruction or fear of mistakes → less exploration → weaker creativity
- **适用：** 绘画、想象力、音乐、儿童教育、科技创新、解决问题

## M11-❤️ 经济与就业

- **正向链：** useful products or services → business revenue and jobs → economic growth
- **反向链：** unstable demand or job loss → income pressure → wider inequality
- **适用：** 商业、旅游、职业、工资、购物、全球化、创业

## M12-💙 环境与可持续性

- **正向链：** practical infrastructure and incentives → greener behaviour → lower pollution and resource loss
- **反向链：** excessive consumption → waste and pollution → long-term environmental damage
- **适用：** 交通、种植、旅游、消费、环保法律、城市生活

## M13-❤️ 年龄与代际

- **比较链：** different experience, digital familiarity and responsibilities → different needs and habits
- **变化链：** easier technology and wider education → gradually narrower generational gaps
- **适用：** 年轻人与老人、儿童学习、消费、手机、音乐、休闲

## M14-❤️ 计划与执行

- **正向链：** clear priorities and realistic sequencing → actionable plan → lower risk and better results
- **反向链：** unclear priorities or rigid scheduling → impractical plan → delays or poor results
- **适用：** 目标、职业规划、旅行准备、日程安排、改变计划、执行
- **边界：** M08 判断“相信什么、如何选择”；M14 安排“决定方向后如何行动”。

## M15-💙 名人与广告

- **正向链：** visibility and credibility → greater public attention → wider influence or opportunity
- **反向链：** persuasive advertising or constant public exposure → less careful judgement or loss of privacy → misleading choices or stress
- **适用：** 名人、儿童成名、广告、社交媒体、明星新闻

## M16-💚 动物与宠物

- **正向链：** daily care and companionship → empathy and emotional support → stronger responsibility
- **反向链：** cost and long-term care needs → pressure on owners → possible neglect
- **适用：** 宠物、动物故事、儿童教育、城市养宠

## M17-❤️ 🥚艺术、娱乐与想象力

- **正向链：** artistic exposure and participation → emotional expression and new ideas → creativity or relaxation
- **反向链：** passive entertainment or limited access → shallow engagement → fewer cultural benefits
- **适用：** 绘画、音乐、电影、节目、视频、博物馆、想象力

## M18-❤️ 地点、建筑与设施

- **正向链：** practical design and accessible facilities → easier daily use → better quality of life
- **反向链：** appearance without function or poor maintenance → wasted resources → limited public value
- **适用：** 城市、住宅、建筑、购物场所、安静地方、公共空间

## M19-❤️ 习惯、情绪与休闲

- **正向链：** balanced routines and meaningful activities → lower stress → better concentration and mood
- **反向链：** repetition, inactivity or constant pressure → boredom and fatigue → poorer well-being
- **适用：** 作息、早起、无聊、微笑、安静、休闲、购物情绪

## M20-❤️🥚 科技、媒体与隐私

- **正向链：** faster access and communication → greater convenience → wider participation
- **反向链：** data collection, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">distraction</mark> or weak verification → privacy loss and misinformation → lower trust
- **适用：** AI、App、手机、网络、新闻、社交媒体、在线沟通

## M21-💚 合作、领导与帮助

- **正向链：** clear roles and mutual support → stable cooperation → stronger collective results
- **反向链：** unclear responsibility or reluctance to communicate → coordination problems → weaker teamwork
- **适用：** 团队、领导、帮助、家务、企业、学校合作

## M22-💚 文化、身份与传统

- **正向链：** participation and cultural exposure → stronger identity and understanding → continued transmission
- **反向链：** commercialisation or dominant global content → weaker local engagement → cultural loss
- **适用：** 节日、食物、历史建筑、旅游、国家认同、跨文化交流

## 组合规则

- 简单题只用一个模块。
- 比较、利弊和政策题通常组合两个模块。
- 同一回答最多使用两个模块，避免观点过多。
- 模块提供论证，题目中的具体名词提供语境。
- 如果一个问题无法由任何模块自然回答，才需要加入困难题答案库。
