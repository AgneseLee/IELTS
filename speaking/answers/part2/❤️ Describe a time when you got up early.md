## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a time when you got up early**

> **You should say:**
> - When it was
> - What you did
> - Why you got up early
> - And how you felt about it

> Bank: B3 | Modules: 4:30-a.m. / sunrise-boat / worth-the-effort

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I adjusted to an unfamiliar early schedule to take part in a meaningful travel experience.
- Secondary Ability: Learning Ability
- Reason: I learned from the experience that temporary discomfort can be worthwhile when it has a clear purpose.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella and I got up at 4:30 a.m. for a sunrise boat near Semporna.
- Background: We had little sleep, and early mornings are not part of my normal routine.
- Main event: We boarded a small boat and watched the sky change over the sea.
- Ability shown: Adapting to discomfort and learning from direct experience.
- Reflection: The quiet sunrise made the early start completely worthwhile.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I got up at 4:30 one morning during my Malaysia trip with Stella, near Semporna. We had booked a small boat to watch the sunrise from the open water, so missing the alarm would have meant losing the experience. I felt half awake and slightly irritable because we had slept very little. Still, I got ready, followed the guide, and boarded the boat. The quiet harbour and cool air gradually woke me up. As the sky changed from dark blue to orange, everyone became silent, and the light across the sea was breathtaking.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
The scene left a lasting impression because it differed completely from Beijing traffic. I felt tired but calm and grateful.

The experience improved my adaptability by showing that I can adjust my routine for a meaningful opportunity. It also taught me to judge discomfort by its result. Doing this once felt worth the effort. Since then, I have become more willing to step outside my comfort zone while travelling. I would happily rise early for another sunrise. The memory reminds me that wonderful travel moments sometimes require careful preparation and temporary inconvenience, but the final result can fully justify both.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
