## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe an interesting video**

> **You should say:**
> - When and where you watched it
> - What it is about
> - Why you watched it
> - And explain how you feel about it

> Bank: B5 | Modules: psychology-video / sleep-pressure / self-reflection

## 1. Band 7 Answer

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my career and my future. One night, I couldn't sleep again, so I opened my laptop and randomly picked a video on social media, and an interesting one caught my attention. It was a psychology story with several cute animated characters. I found it really interesting, and what they said left a lasting impression on me. I still remember one character saying, “Slow is fast.” That made me think a lot because I used to think I had to finish everything immediately and handle every challenge alone because that would prove that I was capable and strong enough. However, that really put a lot of pressure on me and made me feel exhausted and nervous. Anyway, I ended up watching that video again and again. After finishing it, I realized that slowing down didn't mean falling behind. Instead, it helped me focus on what I could control and what really matters most at the moment. That really relieved some of my stress, and my sleep improved a little bit that night.

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I reconsidered the need to rush and focused on what I could control, which eased my stress.
