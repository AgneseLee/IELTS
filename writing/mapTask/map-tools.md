# 雅思地图题：16高分终极工具包

整合"12词精简版"与"城市扩张类4词增强版"，覆盖规划类、变迁类、扩张类 99% 的地图题，目标 7.5+。

---

## 第一组：宏观边界与区域界定

> 用于开头段或背景描述，替代简单的 north/south。

| 短语 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Along the northern edge of... | 沿……的北缘 |
| Within the confines of... | 在……的范围内 |

**例句**

- Along the northern edge of the site, a river currently separates the industrial zone from the adjacent farmland.
- Currently, within the confines of the industrial park, various factory buildings are arranged along a single main road.

---

## 第二组：邻里关系与空间坐标

> 描述"点对点"的逻辑，使考官能在脑海中构图。

| 短语 | 中文 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|
| Adjacent to... | 紧邻/相邻 | |
| Immediately to the east/west of... | 就在……的正东/西侧 | |
| Respectively | 分别地 | **7.5分核心词** |

**例句**

- Adjacent to the new school, a playground will be established to serve the local residents.
- A school is planned to be built immediately to the east of the central roundabout.
- Shops and a medical center will be situated to the south-west and south-east of the roundabout, respectively.

---

## 第三组：动态轨迹与连通性

> 描述道路延伸、桥梁跨越及线性分布。

| 短语 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Extending northwards from... | 从……向北延伸 |
| Crossing the [river] via [a bridge] | 通过[桥]跨越[河] |
| Positioned at the eastern end of... | 位于……的东尽头 |

**例句**

- The road network will be expanded, with a main thoroughfare extending northwards from the roundabout.
- The extended road will reach the northern housing area by crossing the river via a newly constructed bridge.
- A smaller roundabout will be positioned at the eastern end of the internal road to facilitate traffic flow.

---

## 第四组：功能转化与改造

> 用于描述旧设施被新设施取代。

| 短语 | 中文 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|
| ...demolished to make way for... | 被拆除以为……腾出空间 | |
| ...replaced by... situated to the... | 被位于……的……所取代 | |
| ...transformed from... lying... | 由位于……的……转化而来 | |
| ...integrated into... | 整合/融入进…… | **综述段万能词** |

**例句**

- Existing factories will be demolished to make way for a series of housing estates distributed across the site.
- The current factory site will be replaced by shops situated to the south-west of the center.
- New housing will be transformed from the farmland lying north of the river.
- Overall, the planned development shows how the industrial zone will be integrated into a modern residential community.

---

## 第五组：城市扩张与稳定性

> 专针对"过去 vs 现在"的城市规模增长类地图。

| 短语 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Flanking [a bridge/road] | 位于……的两侧 |
| Radiating from [a center] | 以……为中心向外辐射 |
| Maintains its original position | 保留在原位 |
| Encircled by / Enveloped by | 被……环绕/包围 |

**例句**

- The river has been dammed, forming two lakes flanking either side of the original bridge.
- The infrastructure has expanded into a grid system radiating from the city centre.
- While the residential area has grown, the airport maintains its original position at the eastern edge.
- The business district, once on the edge, is now encircled by a vastly enlarged residential zone.

## 提分小贴士

### 时态切换

| 题型 | 时态 |
|------|------|
| 规划图 (Future) | `is planned to be` / `will be` + 工具短语 |
| 变迁图 (Past → Present) | `has been` + 工具短语 |

### 拼写易错词

务必确认以下词拼写正确，7分以上扣分非常显眼：

- `respectively`
- `adjacent`
- `demolished`

### 长句组合技

在一个长句中组合两个工具，快速拉高 Grammatical Range：

> A medical center will be erected **adjacent to** the shops, both of which are **positioned at the eastern end** of the road.

## 个人地图题快速提分清单

### 1. Overview 只写图中看得见的宏观变化

地图题的 overview 不要推测设计目的。只概括布局、功能、保留与替换。

| 不推荐 | 推荐 |
|---|---|
| `make way for accessibility for people` | `the central area has been cleared of tables and chairs` |
| `the library experienced a significant development` | `the library has been modernised and reorganised` |
| `a digital media room` | `a room for CDs, videos and computer games` |

**可直接套用：**

> Overall, the library has been modernised and reorganised, with several former book and reading areas replaced by facilities for technology, events and refreshments. One feature that has remained unchanged is the entrance.

### 2. 地图题被动结构必须零错误

地图题高频使用 `be located / be situated / be replaced by / be transformed into`。基础错误会明显拉低 Grammar 分。

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `has been undergone` | `has undergone` / `has been modernised` |
| `the seating area situated` | `the seating area was situated` |
| `a cafe and lecture room located` | `a cafe and a lecture room are located` |
| `is constructed in where...` | `has been created where...` |

**核心句型：**

- `X was situated at the centre of the library.`
- `X was located in the north-western corner.`
- `The former X has been replaced by Y.`
- `X has been transformed into Y.`
- `Y has been created where X used to be.`

### 3. 保持原空间顺序，不要边写边跳

如果原图是室内平面图，可以用以下顺序：

1. 中心区域
2. 中心左右两侧
3. 四个角落：north-west / south-west / north-east / south-east
4. 入口是否保留

**过去图模板：**

> Twenty years ago, X was situated at the centre of the library. Immediately to its west and east, there were A and B, respectively. C occupied the north-western corner, with D in the south-western corner. Meanwhile, E and F were positioned in the north-eastern and south-eastern corners, respectively.

**现在图模板：**

> Today, the former A has been transformed into B. C has been created where D used to be, while E and F are now located in the south-eastern and north-eastern corners, respectively. X and Y are positioned on either side of the main hall, with the entrance maintaining its original position.

### 4. `respectively` 只在两组信息完全配对时使用

`respectively` 的结构必须是 A and B 对应 C and D。

| 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|
| `A and B were positioned in the north-eastern and south-eastern corners, respectively.` | A 对 north-eastern，B 对 south-eastern |
| `A cafe and a lecture room are now located in the south-eastern and north-eastern corners, respectively.` | cafe 对 south-eastern，lecture room 对 north-eastern |

不要在后半句没有清晰配对时强行使用 `respectively`。

### 5. 少用重型连接词，优先用空间词

地图题不是议论文，不需要频繁使用强对比连接词。

| 少用 | 多用 |
|---|---|
| `In stark contrast` | `Today, ...` |
| `experienced a significant development` | `has been modernised and reorganised` |
| `Furthermore` | `Meanwhile` / `On the eastern side` / `Along the northern edge` |

### 6. 写完后 30 秒自查

- [ ] Overview 有没有加入图中没有的信息？
- [ ] `has undergone` / `has been modernised` 有没有写错？
- [ ] 每个 `situated / located / positioned` 前是否有 be 动词？
- [ ] 四个角落是否写对：north-west / south-west / north-east / south-east？
- [ ] `respectively` 是否真的形成 A-B 对 C-D？
- [ ] 是否保留了一个稳定空间顺序，而不是来回跳？
