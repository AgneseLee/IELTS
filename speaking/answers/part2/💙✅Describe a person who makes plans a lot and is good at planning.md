## Cue Card

**Describe a person who makes plans a lot and is good at planning**

> **You should say:**
> - Who he/she is
> - How you knew him/her
> - What plans he/she makes
> - And explain how you feel about this person

> Bank: B1 | Modules: Malaysia-itinerary / offline-map / flexible-planning

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: Stella prepares essential details but leaves room to adjust when transport, weather, or navigation changes.
- Secondary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: Her backups and realistic priorities prevent small travel problems from becoming major ones.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella, my former colleague and one of my closest friends.
- Background: I met her about three years ago at work and later travelled to Malaysia with her.
- Main event: She built a realistic itinerary, saved offline maps, and calmly changed our route when the signal failed.
- Ability shown: Flexible planning supported by preparation and practical problem-solving.
- Reflection: Her approach taught me that a good plan creates freedom instead of controlling every minute.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The person I'd like to describe is Stella, a former colleague I met about three years ago. She makes plans for work, study, and especially travel. Before our Malaysia trip, she compared flights, booked accommodation, set a realistic budget, and prepared a detailed itinerary. However, she<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;"> deliberately left some free time.</mark> She also saved our bookings and an offline map. When the navigation signal disappeared and we took a wrong route, she checked the map, asked other tourists for directions, and adjusted the schedule without <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">panicking</mark>. We still reached our destination with little delay.

I <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">admire</mark> her because her plans are <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">flexible</mark>. She prepares important parts and accepts that life can change. When things go wrong, she doesn't follow the plan blindly. She adjusts it and uses a <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">backup</mark>. This helps her stay calm and deal with the <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">main problem </mark>first.

I used to <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">handle travel details</mark> at the last minute, which was stressful. Since travelling with Stella, I have started planning well in advance and downloading important travel information in case I lose internet access.

Her example changed my <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">perspective</mark>: good planning should create <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">freedom</mark>, not remove it. A clear structure helps people stay calm and optimistic while enjoying surprises. Now I know preparation and flexibility can work together.
