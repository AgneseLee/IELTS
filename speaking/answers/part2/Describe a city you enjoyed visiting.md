## Cue Card

**Describe a city you enjoyed visiting**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - When you visited it
> - How long you stayed there
> - What you did there
> - And explain why you enjoyed visiting it

> Bank: B3 | Modules: Kuala-Lumpur / cultural-variety / transport

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I used public transport, tried unfamiliar food, and adjusted easily between modern and traditional parts of Kuala Lumpur.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Kuala Lumpur, the first city on my Malaysia trip with Stella.
- Background: We took a ten-day Malaysia trip last year and spent the first three days in Kuala Lumpur.
- Main event: We explored a museum, food streets, and the city centre by public transport.
- Ability shown: I adapted to unfamiliar food, transport, and a multicultural environment.
- Reflection: The city broadened my idea of what a modern Asian city can feel like.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The city I enjoyed visiting was Kuala Lumpur, the capital of Malaysia. Stella and I took a ten-day Malaysia trip last year and spent the first three days there. We used public transport to visit a museum, walked around the modern city centre, and ate at older street-food stalls in the evening. Within a short walk, we could see glass towers, traditional shops, and people from different cultures. A local vendor patiently explained a dish we had never tried. I enjoyed the city because it felt varied but easy to explore.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Hearing several languages and trying unfamiliar food helped me step outside my comfort zone without feeling overwhelmed. The trip improved my adaptability because I had to read transport signs, change plans when it rained, and communicate with local people. Each small success made me more confident. More importantly, Kuala Lumpur broadened my horizons. I realised a modern city does not have to lose its older neighbourhoods or local character. The contrast made the place feel genuine rather than staged for tourists. Looking back, those three days left a lasting impression because they made international travel feel manageable. I would happily return for a longer stay and explore the city's ordinary rhythm, not only its famous sights.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
