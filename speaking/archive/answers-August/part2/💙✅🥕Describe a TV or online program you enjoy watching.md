## Cue Card

**Describe a TV/online program you enjoy watching**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is
> - What it is about
> - Which country it is from
> - When you watch it
> - And explain why you like to watch it

> Bank: B8 | Modules: Semporna / marine-plastic / daily-habits

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Learning Ability
- Reason: The programme helps me understand threats to marine life and turn that knowledge into daily habits.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: A British online documentary series about ocean life and protection.
- Background: I started watching it after seeing a sea turtle while freediving near Semporna.
- Main event: It shows marine animals, plastic pollution, beach clean-ups, and simple ways to reduce waste.
- Ability shown: I expand my knowledge and use it to make more responsible daily choices.
- Reflection: It combines beautiful wildlife scenes with practical environmental action.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The programme I enjoy is <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">a British online documentary series</mark> about ocean life and protection. I watch an episode at home on Sunday evenings. I began watching it after returning from Semporna with Stella, where we saw a sea turtle while freediving. The series <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">follows marine animals</mark> in different ocean habitats. One episode <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">explained</mark> how plastic travels from streets and rivers into the sea. It also showed volunteers cleaning a beach and explained how reusable bottles and bags can reduce waste. I watched it because I wanted to understand the risks our guide had mentioned.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I like the programme because it <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">connects</mark> beautiful wildlife with practical environmental problems. Before Semporna, ocean pollution felt distant. The series made it clearer and more concrete.

It also changed my <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">habits</mark>. Stella and I now carry reusable bottles and refuse unnecessary bags. These actions are small but easy to repeat.

More importantly, I realised that protecting nature is <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">a shared responsibility</mark>. The series can be <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">worrying</mark>, but it does not leave me feeling <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">helpless</mark>. It shows that small daily choices can make a real difference. That <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">practical message</mark> is why I keep watching the programme and trying to improve my habits over time.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
