## 浪费时间的活动🧡

### 1. Why are routines important?

题型：Q1
主题：T01 学习、创造与执行 + T02 健康、习惯与休闲
机制：M02 时间与精力成本
逻辑链：lower time or effort → greater willingness → wider participation
答案：Routines reduce the time and effort needed to make repeated daily decisions. When the next step is clear, people are more willing to act consistently and maintain useful habits. For example, someone who exercises immediately after work does not need to choose a new time each day. So, routines make good behaviour easier to continue.

### 2. Do you think having a routine is generally beneficial?

题型：Q5
主题：T01 学习、创造与执行 + T02 健康、习惯与休闲
机制：M02 时间与精力成本
逻辑链：lower time or effort → greater willingness → wider participation
答案：Yes, a flexible routine is generally helpful. It lowers the effort of planning ordinary tasks, so people can participate consistently in work, exercise or study. For example, preparing lunch the night before can make healthy eating easier on a busy weekday. However, a rigid routine can become stressful when circumstances change. Overall, routines work best when they provide structure without removing flexibility.

### 3. How can people establish a useful daily routine?

题型：Q6
主题：T01 学习、创造与执行 + T02 健康、习惯与休闲
机制：M06 长期能力与短期便利
逻辑链：independent practice and feedback → stronger ability → better long-term results
答案：They should begin with one realistic habit, practise it and review the result. Repeated effort and feedback help them build the ability to manage a fuller routine over time. For example, someone can set a regular bedtime for one week and adjust it if they still feel tired. So, useful routines develop through small experiments and steady practice.

### 4. Why do some people dislike following routines?

题型：Q1
主题：T01 学习、创造与执行 + T02 健康、习惯与休闲
机制：M03 自主权与责任感
逻辑链：excessive control → passive compliance → weak independence
答案：Some dislike routines because they feel that fixed schedules remove personal choice. Too much control can produce passive compliance and weaken a person's motivation to continue. For example, someone may abandon an exercise plan that tells them exactly what to do every minute. So, routines become unpopular when they leave no room for independence.

主题：T01 学习、创造与执行
机制：M01 实用价值
逻辑链：real need → practical usefulness → continued use or participation
答案：Practical plans have clear priorities, realistic timing and a sensible order of steps. These features turn a general goal into actions and reduce delays. For example, a study plan can assign one topic to each evening and leave a spare day for review. So, a useful plan must fit the time and resources available.
