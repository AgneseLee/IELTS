## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a way/change that helps you save a lot of time**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is
> - How you implement it
> - How difficult it is
> - And explain how you feel about the way/change

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / advice

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
One change that saves me a lot of time is making a short daily plan. I got the idea from Stella, my former colleague. After a layoff increased her workload, she started writing down her priorities. I now do this before studying English each morning. I choose three tasks, put them in order, and break bigger ones into manageable steps. Getting started was easy, but estimating the time was harder. At first, I planned too much, so I began leaving gaps for tasks that took longer.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I'm pleased with this change because I spend less time wondering what to do next. I used to jump between different exercises and leave several unfinished. Now I know where to start, and I can see what I've actually completed.

Stella's advice helped me feel understood and supported when I struggled with my schedule. I learned to focus on what I could control, especially the size and order of my tasks. This helped me find practical solutions instead of simply trying to study faster. Looking back, saving time means making realistic choices for me. I feel less rushed, and I have more time to relax after studying.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
