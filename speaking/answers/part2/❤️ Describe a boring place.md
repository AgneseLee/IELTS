## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a boring place**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - Who you went there with
> - What you did there
> - And explain why you think it is a boring place

> Bank: B7 | Modules: office-skyscraper / city-view / daily-comfort

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I stopped focusing on the delayed meeting and used the waiting time to organise my notes and adjust my approach.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: An office skyscraper near my former workplace in Beijing.
- Background: Stella and I went there for a client meeting that was delayed for nearly two hours.
- Main event: We waited in the bright atrium, bought coffee, checked emails, and watched people enter crowded lifts.
- Ability shown: I adjusted my approach and used the delay to organise our meeting notes.
- Reflection: The wait taught me that an impressive building can still feel boring when visitors have no purpose or freedom to explore.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The boring place I remember is an office skyscraper near my former workplace in Beijing. I went there with Stella, my former colleague, for a client meeting. It has a glass exterior, a bright atrium, and upper floors with a wide city view. However, our meeting was delayed for nearly two hours. We bought coffee, checked our emails, and sat in the lobby. Visitors could not explore the upper floors without a pass, and the lifts were crowded. After a while, every activity felt repetitive. I found the whole experience boring.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
The building looked impressive but gave us nothing meaningful to do. Still, the experience improved my adaptability. Instead of complaining about the delay, I adjusted my approach and organised our meeting notes with Stella. That gave the waiting time a clear purpose and helped us feel calmer. Looking back, the experience changed how I judge buildings. A dramatic skyline and practical comfort can make a place attractive, but they do not automatically make it interesting. What matters is how people can use the space. That is why my opinion is mixed: I admire its design and city view, but the long wait was dull.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
