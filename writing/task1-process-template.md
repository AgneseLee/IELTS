# IELTS Writing Task 1：Process Diagram 模板

基于 sugar production、plastic-bottle recycling 与 ethanol production 三次练习。目标：首两段直接复用；正文只替换流程动作。

## 四段模板

### 1. Introduction

> The diagram illustrates the [number]-stage process used to [produce/recycle] [product/material].

### 2. Overview

> Overall, this is a linear process that begins with [starting point] and ends with [end point].

> [Optional defining feature: duration / phases / possible repetition.]

### 3. Body 1 — Preparation

> Initially, [input] is/are [step 1].

> It/They is/are then [step 2], before being [step 3].

> Following this, [next step].

### 4. Body 2 — Transformation

> During the remaining stages, [material] is/are [step].

> Subsequently, [next transformation].

> Finally, [final action], producing [end product].

## 循环流程适配器

正文沿用四段模板；只替换以下句子。

### Introduction

> The diagram illustrates the cyclical process used to [produce/transform] [product/material].

### Overview

> Overall, [input] passes through [main phases] before becoming [output].

> When [output] is [used/processed], [returning material] is released and returns to [starting stage], completing the cycle.

### Body 2 — Cycle closure

> Finally, [output] is [used/processed], releasing/returning [returning material].

> This material is then [reabsorbed/reused/returned] during [starting stage], beginning another cycle.

### Ethanol example

> The diagram illustrates the cyclical process used to produce ethanol biofuel from plant material.

> Overall, plants and trees absorb carbon dioxide while growing before being converted into ethanol.

> Burning this fuel releases carbon dioxide, which is then reabsorbed by new plants, completing the cycle.

## 首两段验证

### Sugar production

> The diagram illustrates the seven-stage process used to produce sugar from sugar cane.

> Overall, this is a linear process that begins with the cultivation of sugar cane and ends with the drying and cooling of sugar crystals.

> The agricultural phase takes between 12 and 18 months.

### Plastic-bottle recycling

> The diagram illustrates the nine-stage process used to recycle plastic bottles into new products.

> Overall, this is a linear process that begins with the disposal of used bottles and ends with the manufacture of consumer goods.

> Some products can potentially re-enter the same process.

## 使用规则

- `[number]-stage`：步骤明确才写。
- 线性 Overview：起点 + 终点 + 一个显著特征。
- 循环 Overview：主要转化 + 回流物质 + 回到哪个阶段。
- 正文连接器只需：`Initially / Following this / Subsequently / Finally`。
- 使用一般现在时；加工对象作主语时优先使用被动语态。
- `step` 表示具体动作；`stage` 表示较大阶段。
- 只写图中信息；不补充未展示的运输、地点、原因或结果。
