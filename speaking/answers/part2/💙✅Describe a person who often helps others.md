## Cue Card

**Describe a person who often helps others**

> **You should say:**
> - Who this person is
> - How often he/she helps others
> - How/why he/she helps others
> - And how you feel about this person

> Bank: B4 | Modules: new-colleague / setup-notes / cooperative-culture

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: Stella listens to newcomers, explains complex systems clearly, and makes it safe for them to ask questions.
- Secondary Ability: Leadership & Collaboration
- Reason: She shares reusable notes and encourages colleagues to support the next newcomer.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella, my former colleague, who quietly supports new team members.
- Background: Our software systems were complicated and often confused newcomers.
- Main event: She helped me set up my environment and regularly guided other colleagues through the same process.
- Ability shown: Clear explanation and practical support that strengthen the whole team.
- Reflection: Her help taught me that kindness can become a cooperative working culture.
