## Final Version (7.5)

The table and pie charts illustrate the sources of funding and the distribution of expenditure for the police in a British area between 2017 and 2018.

Overall, there was an upward trend in total income, which rose from £304.7m to £318.6m. While salaries comprised the predominant part of the spending in both years, its share saw a slight decline in favor of technology.

As the table displays, the National Government occupied the largest portion of income, increasing marginally from £175.5m to £177.8m. This was followed by Local Taxes, which rose sharply by £11.1m. Other sources, such as grants, accounted for the remaining small amount, showing a negligible increase.

In terms of expenditure, salaries for officers and staff still ranked first, though the figure dropped slightly from 75% to 69%. In stark contrast, the allocation for technology was transformed from a mere 8% to a more significant 14%. Meanwhile, spending on buildings and transport maintained its original share at 17%.
