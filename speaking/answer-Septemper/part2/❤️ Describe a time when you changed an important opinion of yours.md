## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a time when you changed an important opinion of yours**

> **You should say:**
> - When you changed your opinion
> - What the original opinion was
> - Why you changed it
> - And explain how you felt about the experience

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / burnout / healthier-boundaries

## 1. Band 7 Answer

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, my company went through a layoff. Stella, one of my friends and colleagues, suddenly received a much heavier workload. Then she started feeling overwhelmed and burned out. She also started losing sleep. She said she just couldn't fall asleep naturally because she was worried about what might happen if she messed things up, which made her really exhausted. But instead of panicking or pretending everything was all right, she honestly talked to her manager and asked him for help. She also pointed out those unrealistic deadlines and stopped responding to non-urgent messages at night, which helped her set clearer boundaries. By tackling these issues, she gradually recovered both mentally and physically within a few weeks.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Her experience really changed my view of resilience and my opinion of what it means to be a capable and strong person. I used to think a competent person should continue working even under huge pressure. But her example changed my view of it. Now I think resilience can sometimes mean asking for help, accepting support, and changing direction if things don't go as planned. What she did reminded me that pushing myself to the limit is not the only way to deal with difficulties. Instead, I believe it's more important to focus on what I can control and realize what matters most at the moment. I changed my opinion because I believe this mindset will help me handle future career challenges in a much healthier way. Since then, I've become more confident about moving forward.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Reason: Stella’s recovery led me to revise my belief that a competent person should keep working even under huge pressure.
