**Describe a quiet place you like to go**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is

> - What you do there
> - And explain how you feel about the place

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I use a simple park routine to adjust my mood and regain focus when work becomes stressful.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: A small park near Stella's studio and the Weekend Art Market in Beijing.

- Main event: I visit weekly to walk or read, while Stella joins me every few months after our usual studio-and-market route.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The quiet place I like to visit is a small park near Stella's studio and the Weekend Art Market in Beijing. I discovered it after visiting her one weekend. We browsed the market and then walked into the park. Now I go roughly once a week, usually in the evening. I walk one full circuit, read on a bench, or leave my phone in my bag. Stella joins me every few months after our usual studio-and-market route. The pond, flowers, and tall trees make the park feel separate from the traffic outside.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
What I value most is its practical comfort. It is free and requires no planning, so I can use it regularly instead of waiting for a holiday. More importantly, the park has taught me to adjust my routine when I feel stressed. I used to continue working after my concentration disappeared. Now, a short walk helps me clear my head and organise my thoughts before I return to a problem. Looking back, adaptability is not always about dramatic change. Sometimes it means recognising my mental state and choosing a healthier response. I usually leave feeling refreshed, flexible, and ready to focus again. The park turns the same weekend route into a simple escape from screens and work pressure.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
