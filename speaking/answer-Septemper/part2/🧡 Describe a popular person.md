## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a popular person**

> **You should say:**
> - Who this person is
> - What kind of person he or she is
> - When you see him/her normally
> - And explain why you think this person is popular

> Bank: B4 | Modules: group project / advice / support

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I'd like to describe Stella, my former colleague and one of my best friends. She's friendly, patient and very popular with the people we worked with. We met on a software project in Beijing about three years ago. These days, I normally see her when we meet for dinner or a walk. At work, she listened without interrupting and explained problems without making anyone feel foolish. When someone struggled with a task, she helped them break it into manageable steps. That made people comfortable asking her for advice.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I think she's popular because people feel understood and supported around her. She doesn't need to dominate every conversation or agree with everyone. Instead, she asks questions and takes other people's concerns seriously. Her advice helped me focus on what I could control when work became stressful. Watching her also changed the way I talk to colleagues. I used to concentrate on getting my own point across, but now I leave more room for others to speak. I've realised that being likeable isn't mainly about being entertaining. It's about making people feel respected, especially when they are unsure of themselves.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: Stella listens carefully, and I have learned to give others more room to speak.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: Stella, a popular former colleague and close friend.
- Background: We met on a software project in Beijing three years ago.
- Main event: Her patient explanations and practical advice made colleagues comfortable asking for help.
- Ability shown: Listening and explaining without making others feel foolish.
- Reflection: People value respect and attention more than constant entertainment.
