## Summary of Corrections

### 审题错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `The line chart` | `The bar chart` | The time-series visual uses vertical bars, not a line. |

| Approximately `22,000` loans in 2011 | Approximately `21,000` | The 2011 bar is around 21,000. |
| 13% was `exactly twice` 6% | 13% was `just over twice` 6% | Exactly twice 6% is 12%, not 13%. |

### 语法 & 拼写

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
