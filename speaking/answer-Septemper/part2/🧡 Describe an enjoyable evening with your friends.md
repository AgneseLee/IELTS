## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe an enjoyable evening with your friends**

> **You should say:**
> - When and where it was
> - Who you spent the evening with
> - What you did
> - And explain why you think it was an enjoyable evening

## 1. Band 7 Answer

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
It was about three months ago, when my company went through a layoff. I received a much heavier workload, and that's when I started sleeping badly and feeling overwhelmed. At the time, I believed that strong people should handle every challenge and solve every problem on their own because I wanted to prove that I was a strong and capable person. But after several nights of insomnia, I was so desperate and frustrated that I felt I couldn't continue living like that. So I invited my best friend Stella to my apartment in Beijing. That night, we simply lay on the sofa, had some wine, and talked about everything from Harry Potter and The Great Gatsby to the universe. Eventually, our conversation drifted into what I was concerned about. She listened very carefully and said, “Never treat exhaustion as an achievement.”
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
That saying made me rethink my view of resilience and gradually shifted my old mindset. That evening was enjoyable, not because something big happened, but because it gave me a healthier way of looking at my situation. Instead of thinking I had to solve every problem alone, I now think real strength includes asking for help, accepting support, and adjusting when things don't go as planned. I still have bad days, but I don't feel completely lost anymore, thanks to that evening and what Stella told me that night.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: I shared my concerns with Stella, who listened carefully and helped me rethink my situation.
- Secondary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I became more willing to ask for help, accept support, and adjust when things did not go as planned.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: An evening with my best friend Stella at my apartment in Beijing.
- Background: About three months ago, a layoff left me with a heavier workload, poor sleep, and a sense of being overwhelmed.
- Main event: We lay on the sofa, had some wine, and talked about books and the universe before sharing my concerns about work.
- Ability shown: Sharing concerns and accepting a healthier way of responding to pressure.
- Reflection: Stella's advice changed my view of resilience; real strength includes asking for help and accepting support.
