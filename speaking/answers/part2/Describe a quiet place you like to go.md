## Cue Card

**Describe a quiet place you like to go**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - How you knew it
> - How often you go there
> - What you do there
> - And explain how you feel about the place

> Bank: B7 | Modules: neighbourhood-park / after-rain / weekly-routine

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I use a simple park routine to adjust my mood and regain focus when work becomes stressful.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: A small neighbourhood park near my apartment in Beijing.
- Background: I discovered it while walking around the area after moving there.
- Main event: I visit weekly, especially after rain, to walk, read, or talk with Stella.
- Ability shown: Adjusting my routine and managing pressure in a practical way.
- Reflection: The park helps me create distance from screens and respond to stress more calmly.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The quiet place I like to visit is a small park near my apartment in Beijing. I discovered it while exploring the neighbourhood after I moved there. I go roughly once a week, usually in the evening or at weekends. The park has a pond, flowers, and tall trees. After rain, the air has a fresh, earthy smell. I normally walk one full circuit, read on a bench, or talk with my friend Stella without constant interruptions.

What I value most is its <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">practical comfort</mark>. It is free, nearby, and requires no planning.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
That means I can use it regularly instead of waiting for a holiday. Leaving my phone in my bag also gives me a simple escape from screens and work pressure.

More importantly, this place has taught me to adjust my routine when I feel stressed. I used to continue working even when my concentration had disappeared. Now, a short walk helps me clear my head and organise my thoughts before I return to a problem. Looking back, I have realised that adaptability is not always about dramatic change. Sometimes it means recognising my mental state and choosing a healthier response. I usually leave feeling refreshed, flexible, and ready to focus again.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
