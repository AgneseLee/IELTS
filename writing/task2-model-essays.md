## 1. 观点型

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Question type | `agree/disagree` |
| Position | Largely agree with free subject choice |
| Main logic | Interest improves learning; society needs diverse knowledge |

**题目**

> Some people believe that university students should study whatever they like. Others believe they should only study subjects that will be useful in the future, such as science and technology. Do you agree or disagree?

**范文**

Some people believe that university students should be free to choose any subject they like, while others think they should focus only on practical fields such as science and technology. I largely agree that students should be allowed to follow their own interests, although future employment should also be considered.

One important reason is that genuine interest often leads to better learning outcomes. When students are passionate about a subject, they are more likely to read widely, ask questions and develop original ideas. For example, a student who loves history may become a committed researcher, teacher or cultural adviser. If this student is forced to study engineering simply because it seems more useful, they may lose motivation and perform poorly.

Another reason is that society needs more than technical skills. Science and technology are certainly important, but fields such as literature, philosophy, law and the arts also shape how people think, communicate and make decisions. A country with only engineers and scientists would still need writers, educators, designers and policy makers. Therefore, allowing students to study different subjects can create a more balanced society.

Of course, universities should help students understand the job market. Students need to know what skills are required after graduation, and they should be encouraged to build practical abilities alongside their academic interests. However, this does not mean their academic choices should be restricted completely.

In conclusion, I agree that university students should have the freedom to choose subjects they enjoy, because interest supports deeper learning and society benefits from diverse forms of knowledge.

## 2. 讨论型

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Question type | `discuss both views + give your opinion` |
| Position | Improvement should be the first choice when change is possible |
| Main logic | Acceptance reduces stress; action creates better possibilities |

**题目**

> Some people think it is better to accept a bad situation, while others believe it is better to try and improve it. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**范文**

People respond to difficult situations in very different ways. Some believe it is wiser to accept a bad situation, while others argue that people should try to change it. In my view, acceptance may sometimes be necessary, but improvement should be the first choice whenever change is possible.

Those who support acceptance often believe that it helps people stay calm and realistic. Some problems are beyond personal control, such as a serious illness, an economic crisis or a sudden job loss. In these cases, refusing to accept reality may only create more stress. For example, a person who loses a job during a recession may need to accept the situation first before making practical plans. From this perspective, acceptance can prevent emotional exhaustion.

However, others argue that people should try to improve bad situations rather than remain passive. I agree more with this view because many problems can be changed through effort. If people simply accept poor conditions, they may miss the chance to create a better future. For instance, a student with weak grades can improve through better study habits, and a worker in an unsatisfying job can learn new skills or look for better opportunities. Action often brings possibilities that passive acceptance cannot provide.

That said, trying to improve a situation does not mean ignoring reality. People should first understand what can and cannot be changed, and then take practical steps. Blind optimism is not useful, but reasonable effort can make a real difference.

In conclusion, although accepting a bad situation is sometimes sensible, I believe people should try to improve their circumstances whenever they have the ability to do so.

## 3. 利弊型

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Question type | `advantages outweigh disadvantages?` |
| Position | Disadvantages outweigh advantages |
| Main logic | Short-term energy benefits are weaker than environmental damage and delayed renewables |

**题目**

> The increasing demand for oil and gas has made it necessary to look for these energy sources in remote and untouched natural places. Do you think the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?

**范文**

As the demand for energy continues to rise, some countries have started searching for oil and gas in remote and untouched natural areas. Although this may bring certain short-term benefits, I believe the disadvantages outweigh the advantages.

One possible advantage is that new oil and gas reserves can help meet immediate energy needs. Many industries, transport systems and households still depend heavily on fossil fuels. If a country discovers more energy resources, it may reduce energy shortages and support economic activity. In addition, exploration projects can create jobs and increase government income, especially in regions where employment opportunities are limited.

However, the environmental costs are much more serious. Remote natural places are often home to fragile ecosystems, and drilling may cause habitat destruction, pollution and loss of biodiversity. Once these areas are damaged, it can be extremely difficult to restore them. For example, an oil spill in an untouched coastal area could harm wildlife for many years and destroy the livelihoods of local communities that depend on fishing or tourism.

Another drawback is that continued investment in oil and gas may delay the development of renewable energy. Governments and companies have limited money and attention. If they keep focusing on fossil fuels, they may invest less in cleaner alternatives such as solar, wind and hydropower. This makes long-term sustainable development harder to achieve.

In conclusion, while exploring remote areas for oil and gas may provide temporary economic and energy benefits, I believe the environmental damage and delay in clean energy development make it a negative choice overall.

## 4. 原因解决型

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Question type | `causes + solutions` |
| Position | Congestion is caused by car ownership and planning problems |
| Main logic | Improve transport, urban planning, and work flexibility |

**题目**

> In many cities, traffic congestion has become a serious problem. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can be suggested?

**范文**

Traffic congestion has become a serious issue in many cities. It wastes time, increases stress and contributes to air pollution. This problem is mainly caused by rising car ownership and poor urban planning, but several practical solutions can help reduce it.

One major cause is the growing number of private cars. As incomes rise, more people are able to buy their own vehicles. Many people also prefer driving because public transport is often crowded, slow or inconvenient. As a result, roads become especially busy during rush hours. Another cause is poor city design. In some cities, people live far away from their workplaces, schools and shopping areas, so they have to travel long distances every day.

To deal with this problem, governments should improve public transport. If buses and trains are affordable, reliable and comfortable, more people will be willing to use them instead of driving. Cities can also create more bus lanes and bicycle paths so that people have more travel choices. In addition, better urban planning is necessary. New residential areas should be built closer to offices, schools and essential services, reducing the need for long daily journeys.

Employers can also help by allowing flexible working hours or remote work. If fewer people travel at the same time each morning and evening, pressure on the roads will be reduced. This does not require major construction, but it can still make commuting easier.

In conclusion, traffic congestion is caused mainly by too many private cars and inefficient city planning. However, with better public transport, smarter urban design and more flexible working arrangements, the problem can be significantly improved.
