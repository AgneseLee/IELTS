### 1. Environmental protection 环境保护

**主题链：** `T07 环境与动物` — practical infrastructure and incentives → encourage greener choices → lower pollution and resource waste

**Your original answer**

> Personally, I think both government and individuals should take visible steps to protect the environment. - `both government and individuals` → `both governments and individuals`
- `For government` → `For governments`
- `greener choices for people` → 直接说 `encourage greener choices`
- `people would like to use them` → `people are more likely to use public transport`
- `resource losses` → `resource waste`
- `greenable choices` → `greener choices`
- `a reusable bags` → `reusable bags` / `a reusable bag`
- `pollution and carbon emissions` 有一点语义重复

**Band 7 version（最小改动）**

For governments, providing better infrastructure and financial incentives can encourage greener choices. For example, with better public transport, people are more likely to commute without using private cars, which reduces emissions and resource waste. As for individuals, even small daily choices can add up and make a big difference. For example, people can use reusable bags instead of single-use plastic bags when shopping. Over time, these habits can help reduce environmental damage.

- `behave bad` → `behave badly / misbehave`
- `get punishments` → `face consequences` 更自然
- `while they behave better` → `while those who behave well...`，让对比结构完整
- `get reward` → `get rewards / are rewarded`；`consequence` → `consequences`
- `realize the rules` → `understand the rules`
- `take responsible for` → `take responsibility for`；也可以说 `be responsible for`

**Band 7 version（最小改动）**

> I suppose clear and fair rules can shape children's behavior and help them develop a greater sense of responsibility. For example, when children misbehave, they face consequences, while those who behave well may get rewards. ### 20. Can excessive or inconsistent rules have negative effects?

**主题链：** `T04 公平、规则与公共资源` — excessive or inconsistent rules → resistance and unfairness → weaker trust

**Your original answer**

> That said, excessive or inconsistent rules may weaken trust between parents and children. If kids realize outcomes don't really match what they behave, they might feel spoiled or start resisting the rules because they see them as unfair. For example, if people misbehaved but they ultimately get reward by the end, they don't have anything to incentivize them to behave well.

**主要错误 / 不自然**

- `match what they behave` → `match how they behave / match their behavior`
- `feel spoiled` 不表示“觉得不公平”；`spoiled` 是“被宠坏的”，此处删去即可
- `if people misbehaved... get` → `if children misbehave... get`，一般情况用一般现在时
- `ultimately... by the end` → `in the end`，去掉重复
- `get reward` → `get rewarded / get a reward`
- `don't have anything to incentivize them` → `there's no real incentive for them`

**Band 7 version（最小改动）**

> That said, excessive or inconsistent rules may weaken trust between parents and children. If kids realize that outcomes don't really match how they behave, they may start resisting the rules because they see them as unfair. For example, if children misbehave but still get rewarded in the end, there's no real incentive for them to behave well or follow the rules.

### 21. How should governments spend public money?

**主题链：** `T04 公平、规则与公共资源` — targeted spending → better essential services → wider public benefit；high costs or poor allocation → financial pressure → less funding for other needs

**例子关联：** `T07 环境与动物` — practical infrastructure and incentives → encourage greener choices → lower pollution and resource waste

**Your original answer**

> I think the government should prioritize funding for essential public services. For example, allocating sufficient funding to public transport allows people to enjoy better public transportation in their daily lives. On the one hand, it can encourage greener choices and nudge people to commute by bus or subway. On the other hand, we shouldn't overlook the risks of poor public spending. If the government poorly manage public funds or overspend on infrastructure, it may leave fewer resources for other essential services.

**主要错误 / 不自然**

- `the government poorly manage... or overspend` → `the government manages... poorly or overspends`，主语按单数处理
- `funding... funding` 重复，可以将第二处换成 `money`
- `enjoy better public transportation` 可以；`have access to better public transport` 更贴近资源投入的逻辑
- `On the one hand` 前面已经开始讲优点，可改成 `This` 直接接续
- 保留 `prioritize funding for essential public services` 和 `nudge people to commute by...`

**Band 7 version（最小改动）**

> I think the government should prioritize funding for essential public services. For example, allocating sufficient money to public transport allows people to have access to better public transportation in their daily lives. This can encourage greener choices and nudge people to commute by bus or subway. On the other hand, we shouldn't overlook the risks of poor public spending. If the government manages public funds poorly or overspends on infrastructure, it may leave fewer resources for other essential services.

### 重点句型：be likely to + 动词原形
