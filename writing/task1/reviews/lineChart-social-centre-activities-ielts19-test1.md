## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] Table tennis did not merely rise “at a highest rate”; it showed the largest **absolute increase**, from about 16 to 54 participants. Write `recorded the strongest growth`.
- [TA] Correct the endpoints for amateur dramatics (`about 6`, not `8`) and table tennis (`about 54`, not `55`). Musical performances stood at zero in both 2000 and 2005, which is a useful starting feature.
- [TA] `overtook` means moving from behind to ahead. Since the film club ranked first throughout, write `remained the most popular activity`.

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] The upward-trend paragraph is sensibly grouped, but musical performances did not follow exactly the same pattern as table tennis: it stayed at zero initially and then rose steadily. Use `also increased` rather than `followed a similar pattern`.
- [CC] In the final paragraph, distinguish the declining series from the two broadly stable series so that `By contrast` has a precise function.

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] ⚠ recurring: Use `participation in table tennis/amateur dramatics`, not `the participation of...`; use `the number of film-club participants`, not `the numbers of film club`.
- [LR] `musical performances curriculum` is inaccurate because the chart shows an activity, not a course syllabus. Write `participation in musical performances`.
- [LR] Use `grew steadily` rather than `rose constantly`; `consistently` describes an unbroken ranking, while `steadily` describes gradual change.
- [LR] Correct the spelling `permormances` → `performances`.

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] The introduction needs a reporting verb: `The line graph shows how... changed`.
- [GR] Historical data require the past tense: `remained`, not `remain`.
- [GR] Use `an upward trend`, `the highest number`, `zero participants`, and `over the 20-year period`; these require the correct article, plural or preposition.
- [GR] Avoid the dangling structure `with zero participant in 2005, and rose...`; give the clause a clear subject: `the figure stood at zero ... before rising...`.

## Final Integrated Version(185) (7.0–7.5)

The line graph compares participation in five activities at a social centre in Melbourne, Australia, between 2000 and 2020.

Overall, the film club remained the most popular activity, while martial arts participation was broadly stable. Table tennis recorded the strongest growth and ranked second by 2020, whereas amateur dramatics declined markedly. Musical performances became more popular after 2005.

The film club attracted about 64 participants in 2000. Its figure slipped gradually to 60 in 2010 before recovering to approximately 66 by the end of the period. Participation in martial arts fluctuated within a narrow range: it fell from 36 to 32 during the first five years, peaked at roughly 38 in 2010 and finished at its initial level of 36.

The remaining activities changed more noticeably. Table-tennis participation rose from 16 in 2000 to 20 in 2005, remained unchanged in 2010 and then surged to about 54 by 2020. By contrast, amateur dramatics edged up from 26 to 28 before falling continuously to only 6. Finally, musical performances had no participants in either 2000 or 2005, but the figure subsequently grew steadily to approximately 18.
