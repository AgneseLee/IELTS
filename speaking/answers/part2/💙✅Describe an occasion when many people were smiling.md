## Cue Card

**Describe an occasion when many people were smiling**

> **You should say:**
> - When it happened
> - Who you were with
> - What happened
> - And explain why most people were smiling

> Bank: B6 | Modules: birthday-dinner / lottery-joke / reconnecting

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: Stella used a well-timed joke and shared memories to reconnect a group of busy friends.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: My birthday dinner with Stella and two close friends.
- Background: We had not met as a group for several months.
- Main event: Stella joked about our losing Malaysia lottery tickets, and everyone laughed.
- Ability shown: Humour created connection and lifted the mood.
- Reflection: Small shared moments can matter more than expensive celebrations.
