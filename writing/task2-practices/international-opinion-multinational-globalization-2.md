# Positive Effects of Multinational Companies and Globalization

## 题目

The spread of multinational companies and the resulting increase in globalization produce positive effects for everyone. Do you agree or disagree with this statement?

## 共用逻辑提纲

- 立场：总体同意，但不接受“每个人都必然受益”的绝对说法；跨国公司和全球化的整体影响是积极的。
- 主体段 1（两组正向模板）：
  - 正向 1：贸易自由化和跨国公司扩张使各国经济联系更紧密 → 推动全球经济增长 → 创造就业并支持较贫穷国家的发展。
  - 正向 2：全球互动增加不同文化之间的接触 → 促进文化交流与包容 → 加强国家之间的互信。
- 主体段 2（一组负向模板＋一组解决方案模板）：
  - 负向：不同价值观和生活方式的直接接触可能引发文化冲突 → 扰乱本地文化并削弱部分社区的社会和谐。
  - 解决方案：推动尊重本地文化的国际合作 → 增强国家互信并改善全球治理 → 在保留全球化收益的同时缓解文化冲突。
- 结论：全球化的经济和文化收益更广泛；文化风险需要治理，但不足以否定其整体积极影响。

## 模板直替版（381 words，Estimated Band 6.5）

The debate surrounding **the spread of multinational companies and the resulting increase in globalization** has engaged a broad audience recently. From my perspective, I partly agree that **these developments produce positive effects for everyone**, as my standpoint rests not only on ample theoretical discussion but also on practical evidence observable across different cases.

First of all, **the expansion of multinational corporations and globalization** undoubtedly brings substantial benefits. One key strength is its ability to improve **global economic growth and job opportunities**, thereby building a firm basis for **development in poorer countries**. As a result, this practice not only generates short-term benefits but also contributes to sustainable long-term outcomes. Another notable advantage of **global interaction** is its ability to directly optimize the current problem. The main reason is that it provides immediate and targeted improvements in **cultural exchange and cultural inclusivity**, allowing **people from different cultures** to see visible progress without delay. As a result, this approach not only stabilizes the situation in the short term but also creates favorable conditions for further long-term solutions.

However, opponents may argue that **rapid globalization can create cultural conflicts and disturb local cultures**. A central problem is that it increases **the risk of conflict between different values and lifestyles**, which places additional pressure on **local communities**. As a consequence, this approach often generates new problems such as **the disturbance of local cultures and reduced social harmony**, instead of delivering the intended benefits. This clearly shows why its negative impact should not be underestimated. A practical approach to addressing this issue is to promote **international cooperation that respects local cultures**. This is effective because it directly improves **mutual trust among nations and global governance**, ensuring that the problem is dealt with in a concrete way. If implemented properly, it can address the problem while preserving its benefits.

In summary, I partly agree with **the claim that multinational companies and globalization produce positive effects for everyone**. On the one hand, I recognize that **they can promote economic growth, create jobs, support poorer countries and enhance cultural exchange**. On the other hand, I also maintain that **cultural conflicts can be mitigated through international cooperation that respects local cultures**. Taken together, these considerations clearly demonstrate why my position should be regarded as the more convincing one.

## 模板最小修改版（281 words，Target Band 7.0–7.5）

The debate surrounding **the expansion of multinational companies and the globalization it accelerates** has engaged a broad audience recently. From my perspective, I **largely agree that their overall effects are positive, although the benefits are not literally shared by every individual**.

First of all, **multinational expansion and globalization** undoubtedly **bring** substantial benefits. **By connecting national economies more closely, trade liberalization and international business can drive global economic growth, create employment and support development in poorer countries.** **These gains can improve living opportunities and provide a basis for longer-term progress.** **As a result, these connections can provide immediate opportunities while creating favourable conditions for more sustainable development in poorer countries.** **Another notable advantage is** **greater cultural exchange**. **As globalization increases contact between cultures, it can encourage inclusivity and strengthen mutual trust among nations, creating social as well as economic benefits.** **Greater contact between cultures can also make societies more inclusive and build the mutual trust needed for continued international cooperation.**

However, opponents may argue that **rapid globalization creates cultural conflicts and disturbs local cultures**. **Different values and lifestyles can come into direct contact, placing pressure on local communities and social harmony.** **This concern should not be underestimated, but a practical response is to promote international cooperation that respects local cultures.** **Such cooperation can strengthen mutual trust and improve global governance, allowing countries to manage cultural tensions while preserving the wider benefits of international interaction.**

In summary, I **largely agree that multinational companies and globalization have positive overall effects**. **They can promote economic growth, employment, development and cultural exchange, while their cultural risks can be reduced through respectful international cooperation.** **Therefore, although not every individual benefits equally, the broader impact remains positive.**
