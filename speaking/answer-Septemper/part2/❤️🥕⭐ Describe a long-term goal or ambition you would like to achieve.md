## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a long-term goal/ambition you would like to achieve**

> **You should say:**
> - How long you have had this goal/ambition
> - What it is
> - How you will achieve it
> - And explain why you set it

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / daily-routine / manageable-steps

## 1. Band 7 Answer (用户提供的完整版本)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
My long-term goal is <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">to build a healthier, more sustainable work and life rhythm</mark>. I've had this goal for a while, but it became much clearer several months ago when my company went through a layoff. During that time, I just couldn't fall asleep. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career and worrying about everything that was bothering me. I ended up feeling overwhelmed and kind of burned out. That's when I realized I couldn't keep pushing myself to the limit because that made me so nervous and burned out. What I really wanted<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;"> was a routine with clear boundaries, realistic deadlines, and regular rest.</mark> So, to move toward that, I started talking honestly with my manager and asked him for help. He did, and he reorganized my workload. I also pointed out some unrealistic deadlines and stopped responding to those non-urgent messages at night. By tackling these issues, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">my daily routine and work schedule gradually improved</mark>. Although it didn't fix everything right away, at least my sleep improved.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">I set this goal because I don't want burnout to define my life. </mark>I used to think I had to handle every challenge and complete every task immediately to prove to myself that I was strong and capable enough. But that mindset just put a lot of pressure on me and made me even more anxious. So I want a different lifestyle with a new mindset, a more resilient mindset. I realize real strength can also involve asking for help, accepting support, and changing direction, especially when things don't go as planned. And if I face any career challenges in the future, I think I'll be more confident dealing with them after all this.
