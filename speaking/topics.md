# IELTS Speaking Question Bank

> Priority: **High-frequency overlapping new topics** > Classic carried-over topics > Single-source new topics

---

## Part I — High-Frequency Overlapping Topics
*Confirmed across all three sources (先行版 · 安小雅 · 雅思哥)*

### Part 1

| Topic | Key Questions |
|---|---|
| **Morning Time 早晨时光** | Do you like getting up early? · What do you usually do in the morning? · Are your mornings very different now from when you were younger? |
| **Work or Studies 工作/学业** | What subjects are you studying? · Do you sometimes feel you have to work/study too hard? · What technology do you use when you study? |
| **Food 食物** | What is your favourite food? · Do you eat different foods at different times of the year? |
| **Pets & Animals 宠物与动物** | Do you prefer to keep your pet indoors or outdoors? · What is the most popular animal in China? |
| **Walking 散步** | Do you walk a lot? · Why do people like to walk in parks? |
| **Typing 打字** | Do you type on a desktop or laptop keyboard every day? · How do you improve your typing? |
| **Gifts 礼物** | Have you ever sent handmade gifts to others? · What do you consider when choosing a gift? |
| **Reading 阅读** | Do you prefer to read on paper or on a screen? · When do you need to read carefully, and when not? |
| **Hobby 爱好** | Do you have any hobbies? · Do you have the same hobbies as your family members? |
| **Sports Team 运动队** | Have you ever been part of a sports team? · What are the differences between team sports and individual sports? |

### Part 2 & 3

| Cue Card Topic | Part 3 Follow-up |
|---|---|
| **Perfect Job 理想工作** — Describe your perfect job. | What should people consider when choosing jobs? |
| **Famous Person 名人** — Describe a famous person you would like to meet. | What can today's children do to become famous? |
| **No Mobile Phone 禁用手机** — Describe an occasion when you were not allowed to use your phone. | Is it necessary to have laws on the use of mobile phones? |
| **Giving Advice 建议** — Describe a time when you gave advice to others. | Is it good to ask advice from strangers online? |
| **Technology 科技产品** — Describe a piece of technology (not a phone) you would like to own. | What are the differences between the technology of the past and today? |
| **Person Who Plans 计划达人** — Describe a person who makes plans a lot. | Should children plan their future careers? |
| **Friend Who Draws 爱画画的朋友** — Describe a friend/child who loves drawing. | Why do some people visit galleries instead of viewing artworks online? |
| **App or Program** — Describe an app or program on your phone or computer. | What are the differences between old and young people when using apps? |
| **Smiling Occasion 微笑场合** — Describe an occasion when many people were smiling. | Do women smile more than men? |
| **Proud of Family Member 家人骄傲** — Describe a time you felt proud of a family member. | Should parents reward their children? |
| **Important Family Object 家庭珍物** — Describe something important kept in your family for a long time. | What is the difference between things valued by people in the past and today? |

## Part II — Classic Carried-Over Topics
*Confirmed in two or more sources*

### Part 1

- **Plants 植物** — Do you keep plants at home? · Do Chinese people send plants as gifts?
- **Public Places 公共场所** — Do you often go to public places with your friends?
- **Rules 规则** — Do you prefer more or fewer rules at school?
- **Advertising 广告** — Do you see a lot of advertising on trains or other transport?
- **Museums 博物馆** — Do you think museums are important?
- **Having a Break 休息** — What do you usually do when you are resting?
- **Sharing 分享** — What kinds of things are not suitable for sharing?

### Part 2 & 3

- **Wild Animal 野生动物** — Describe a wild animal you want to learn more about.
- **Musical Friend 擅长音乐的朋友** — Describe a friend who is good at music or singing.
- **Lost Way 迷路** — Describe an occasion when you lost your way.
- **Creative Person 有创造力的人** — Describe a creative person you admire.
- **Useful Book 有用的书** — Describe a book you read that you found useful.
- **Natural Place 自然之地** — Describe a natural place.

## Part III — Single-Source & Regional Topics

### 安小雅 — Part 1 Deep Topics (20+)

| Topic | Sample Question |
|---|---|
| **Birthdays 生日** | What did you like to do on your birthday when you were a child? |
| **Writing 写作** | Do you prefer typing or writing things by hand? |
| **Geography 地理** | How good are you at reading maps? |
| **Weather 天气** | Has the weather changed much in recent years in your country? |
| **Social Media 社交媒体** | Do you think your friends use social media too much? |

**Additional 安小雅 topics:** Puzzles · Names · Singing · Snacks · Flowers · Memory · Machines · Patience · Being Busy · Photography · Praise · Phone · Staying Up Late · Being Alone

### 先行版 / 雅思哥 — Part 1

- **Scenery 风景** — Do you prefer the mountains or the sea?
- **Buildings 建筑** — Is there a building you would like to visit?
- **Life Stages 人生阶段** — At what age do you think people are the happiest?
- **Keys 钥匙** — Do you think it's a good idea to leave your keys with a neighbour?

### 先行版 / 雅思哥 — Part 2 & 3

- **Bicycle/Motorcycle/Car Trip 车类旅行** — Describe a trip you would like to go on.
- **Person Who Loves Nature 爱护自然的人** — Describe a person who likes to look after the natural world.
- **A Story You Read Recently 故事** — Describe a story you have read recently.
- **A Disappointing Movie 失望的电影** — Describe a movie you watched recently that disappointed you.
