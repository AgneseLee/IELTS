## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a short trip you often take but do not enjoy**

> **You should say:**
> - Where you go
> - When you go there
> - Why you go there
> - And explain why you do not enjoy the trip

> Bank: B3 | Modules: city / regular-commute

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
Well, that would be my daily metro journey, which takes me from my apartment to my workplace in Beijing. I usually take it every weekday morning, and it usually takes about twenty minutes. Maybe that sounds short, but it feels like torture sometimes because it's so packed, especially during rush hour in the morning. I usually don't get a seat, so I have to stand in the crowd with barely enough room to move. It sucks, actually, and that's why I usually feel exhausted before starting my whole day at work.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
What truly bothers me is the lack of personal space in such a crowd, and the noise makes it hard to relax too. But the metro fare is cheaper than taking a taxi, so that's why I still take it every weekday, because I can save money for my personal interests. I've also realized that no matter what transport I take to go to work, it wouldn't be enjoyable. What truly matters is the destination, because I don't like my job, and the trip serves as a reminder of it. I keep telling myself that someday, when I have enough savings, I could stay at home and spend time with my family and friends, and that would be perfect.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I leave earlier and adjust how I spend the journey to cope better with a crowded commute.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: My daily metro journey from my flat to my office in Beijing.
- Background: I travel to work every weekday morning; the journey takes about twenty minutes.
- Main event: Crowded, noisy trains mean I usually stand and sometimes arrive feeling tired.
- Ability shown: I leave slightly earlier and listen to a podcast when space allows.
- Reflection: Small changes make the commute easier, although I still dislike it and keep taking it for its price and reliability.
