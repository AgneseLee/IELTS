## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a noisy place you have been to**

> **You should say:**
> - Where this place is
> - When you usually go there
> - What kinds of noises you can hear there
> - And explain how you feel about this place

## 1. Band 7 Answer（聚会故事复用版）

I'd like to talk about my friend Stella's apartment in Beijing, which was really noisy during a party about three months ago.

At that time, I had trouble falling asleep. I would lie in bed and couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. After several bad nights, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. So when Stella invited me to a party at her apartment, I said yes.

At the party, there were about eight or ten people. Some were standing in the living room, while others were sitting on the sofa, talking about things like cars, money, and houses. Everyone was talking at the same time, and some people were laughing loudly. It was hard to follow a conversation because their voices overlapped.

Honestly, I felt so out of place. All that noise made it difficult for me to relax, especially when I was already stressed and sleeping badly. People were talking, but they weren't really connecting with each other. Everyone was speaking, but no one was really listening. I felt lonely in a room full of people.

I'd rather stay at home alone and enjoy some peace and quiet than experience that kind of loneliness in a crowd. So that was a moment that taught me not every gathering is worth saying yes to.

## 2. 复用说明

- 来源：[[🧡🥕 Describe a time when someone talked about something you were not interested in, but you continued listening]]。
- 保留：三个月前失眠、担忧未来与事业、Stella 邀请参加北京公寓聚会、八到十人、话题缺少共鸣，以及在人群中感到孤独的反思。
- 本题重点：开头直接点出地点与吵闹场景；用声音重叠、难以跟上对话说明噪音如何影响感受。

- 时间与频率：正文保留已知的三个月前这次到访；题卡中的 usually 未有实际频率信息，不编写经常去或只去过一次。
- 本篇为已有回答的改编，不新增原始口述或错误记录；对应口述错误保留在来源文档。
