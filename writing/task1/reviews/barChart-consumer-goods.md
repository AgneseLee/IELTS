## Original Draft(189)

The bar chart compares consumer spending on six types of goods in Britain, France, Italy and Germany, measured in thousands of pounds sterling.

Overall, Britain spent the most on every product, while Germany generally recorded comparatively low figures. Photographic film and toys attracted the highest levels of expenditure, whereas tennis racquets and personal stereos tended to receive less spending.

Looking first at photographic film, British expenditure stood at approximately £172,000, compared with about £166,000 in France. The corresponding figures for Italy and Germany were lower, at roughly £156,000 and £146,000 respectively. Britain also led spending on toys and CDs, at around £168,000 and £163,000. By comparison, expenditure on these two products in the other countries ranged from approximately £147,000 to £158,000.

Regarding the remaining goods, Britain spent about £160,000 on perfumes and roughly £155,000 on both tennis racquets and personal stereos. Italy recorded the second-highest figures for these products, at just over £150,000 in each case. France spent around £145,000 on perfumes and tennis racquets, although its expenditure on personal stereos was slightly higher, at approximately £148,000. German figures for these three products ranged from about £148,000 to £150,000.
