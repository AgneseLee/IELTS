## 嘈杂地🧡

### 1. Which environment do you prefer, a noisy one or a quiet one? Why?

题型：Q2
主题：T02 健康、习惯与休闲 + T10 地点、建筑与设施
机制：M02 时间与精力成本
逻辑链：lower time or effort → greater willingness → wider participation
答案：I prefer a quiet environment because it requires less mental effort, while a noisy place constantly pulls my attention away. Lower distraction makes me more willing to read, work or simply relax. For example, I can study for an hour in a quiet library but lose focus quickly in a busy café. Overall, quiet surroundings suit my daily needs better.

### 2. Why are some places so noisy?

题型：Q1
主题：T10 地点、建筑与设施
机制：M01 实用价值
逻辑链：real need → practical usefulness → continued use or participation
答案：Some places are noisy because they serve practical needs and attract many users at the same time. Continued participation brings traffic, conversation and commercial activity into a limited area. For example, a railway station is loud because thousands of people use its trains, shops and announcements. So, useful and busy places naturally produce more noise.

### 3. Why can quiet places help people reflect on themselves?

题型：Q1
主题：T02 健康、习惯与休闲 + T10 地点、建筑与设施
机制：M02 时间与精力成本
逻辑链：lower time or effort → greater willingness → wider participation
答案：Quiet places reduce the effort needed to control outside distractions. People can then focus more willingly on their thoughts and understand their feelings clearly. For example, someone sitting alone in a calm park may think through a difficult career choice without checking every sound. So, less mental noise makes reflection easier.

### 4. How can noise affect people's daily lives?

题型：Q7
主题：T02 健康、习惯与休闲
机制：M02 时间与精力成本
逻辑链：high cost or effort → lower willingness → avoidance
答案：Noise can make work, sleep and conversation require much more effort. When this cost stays high, people may avoid certain places or struggle to take part in normal activities. For example, traffic outside a flat can keep a resident awake and leave them too tired to concentrate at work. So, persistent noise can seriously disrupt daily routines.

### 5. What can governments do to reduce noise pollution?

题型：Q6
主题：T04 公平、规则与公共资源 + T10 地点、建筑与设施
机制：M01 实用价值
逻辑链：real need → practical usefulness → continued use or participation
答案：Governments should introduce practical limits and improve facilities that reduce unnecessary noise. Rules work better when they meet a real public need and are easy for residents and businesses to follow. For example, a city can restrict late-night construction and create a simple system for reporting violations. So, useful rules and infrastructure can make neighbourhoods quieter.
