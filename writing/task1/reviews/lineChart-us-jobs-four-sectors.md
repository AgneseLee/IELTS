## Prompt
The graph below gives information about the number of jobs in four sectors of the economy in the US between 1960 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

单位：百万个岗位。图示读数：制造业 15 → 20 → 17 → 13；零售业 6 → 10 → 15 → 约16；农业 6 → 3 → 3 → 2；医疗业 2 → 5 → 11 → 约16。年份依次为 1960、1980、2000、2020。

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement
- [TA] 主要数据与排名判断基本准确，农业从1980年起最低也正确。不能把语言问题误判为读图错误。
- [TA] 概述补充制造业“先升后降”的特征；正文明确医疗业最终追平零售业，两者均约为1600万。
- [TA] “remaining unchanged in 2000” 应为 “remained unchanged until 2000”，表达1980—2000年的稳定区间。

### Coherence and Cohesion
- [CC] 保留现有四段及零售→医疗、农业→制造的顺序，无须重排。
- [CC] “respectively” 的年份可从前句推知，不能说完全没有对应对象；但将医疗业数据写成趋势句，并点明终点比较，更容易阅读。

### Lexical Resource
- [LR] ⚠ recurring “the jobs number” → “the number of jobs”；笔记已提醒数量名词的搭配及顺序。
- [LR] “the figure of the manufacruing sector” → “the figure for manufacturing” 或 “manufacturing employment”。
- [LR] “a completely reverse trend” → “an overall decline”。农业有稳定阶段，用 overall 更准确。
- [LR] ⚠ recurring 程度词要与图示相称：“declining dramatically” 简化为 “declining”；不必强加程度副词。
- [LR] 修正拼写 “manufacruing” → “manufacturing”；“milllions” → “million”。sector 比反复使用 category 更贴合就业领域。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy
- [GR] “6 millions” → “6 million”：具体数字后 million 不加 s；泛指数百万才用 “millions of jobs”。全文同类错误应统一修正。
- [GR] “illustrates how ... changes” → “illustrates how ... changed”：图表介绍用现在时，历史变化用过去时。
- [GR] “Looking first in ... in a upward trend” → “Looking first at the growing sectors”；单独使用时应为 “an upward trend”。
- [GR] “remained the lowest number since 1980” → “had the fewest jobs from 1980 onwards”：行业不能本身是一个数字；这里用过去时加明确的历史区间。
- [GR] ⚠ recurring “recording at 2 millions” → “with figures of 2 million ...” 或 “was recorded at 2 million”；笔记已记录 record 的宾语/被动结构。
- [GR] “by the end of period” → “by the end of the period”；“during the former two decades” → “over the first two decades”；“with the start of 15 millions” → “up from 15 million in 1960”。
