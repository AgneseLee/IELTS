## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a plan that you had to change recently**

> **You should say:**
> - When this happened
> - What made you change the plan
> - What the new plan was
> - And how you felt about the change

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / daily-routine / manageable-steps

## 1. Band 7 Answer (用户提供的完整版本)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, my company went through a layoff, so suddenly I was given a much heavier workload. <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">My original plan was to keep following my usual work schedule and handle my tasks as I normally did.</mark> But after the layoff, due to the heavier workload, I had to change my original plan because it was no longer realistic. At that time, I just couldn't fall asleep at night. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my life and my career and worrying about everything that was bothering me. Somehow, I was kind of overwhelmed and burned out. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. So anyway, that's why I decided to make a change and adapt my plan to my current situation. I wanted to figure out a new plan that would help me get through this difficult period. The next day, I honestly talked to my manager and asked him for help. He did, and he helped me reorganize my workload. Then I also pointed out some unrealistic deadlines and stopped replying to those non-urgent messages at night. Within a few weeks, by tackling these issues, I gradually recovered both physically and mentally.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Looking back,<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;"> I felt really relieved about the new plan I came up with</mark>, and it also changed my view of resilience somehow. I used to think strong and capable people should continue working even under huge pressure. But now, after experiencing all this uncertainty, I realize real strength also includes asking for help, accepting support, and, most importantly, changing direction when things don't go as planned. It can help people focus on the things they can handle and realize what matters most at the moment. So I think if I face any career challenges in the future, I'll be more confident dealing with them.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
