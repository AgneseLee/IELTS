## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe an interesting video**

> **You should say:**
> - When and where you watched it
> - What it is about
> - Why you watched it
> - And explain how you feel about it

> Bank: B5 | Modules: psychology-video / sleep-pressure / self-reflection

## 1. Band 7 Answer

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my career and my future. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me.

One night, I couldn't sleep again, so I opened my laptop and randomly picked a video on social media, and an interesting one caught my attention. It was a psychology story with several cute animated characters. One of them was a Toad, and the story was told through short conversations between these characters. The animation was also really adorable, so I found it quite interesting and kept watching.

What they said left a lasting impression on me. I still remember one character saying, **“Slow is fast.”** That made me think a lot because I used to think I had to finish everything immediately, handle every challenge alone, and fix every problem as quickly as possible. I thought doing that would prove that I was capable and strong enough. However, that mindset actually put a lot of pressure on me and made me feel exhausted and nervous. **That was probably one of the reasons why I had trouble sleeping at that time.**

Anyway, I ended up watching that video again and again. After finishing it, I realized that slowing down didn't mean falling behind. Instead, it helped me focus on what I could control and **what really mattered most at that moment**. I also realized that I didn't have to solve every problem immediately.

The video really relieved some of my stress, and my sleep improved a little bit that night. **That's why such a simple video has stayed with me for such a long time.**

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I reconsidered the need to rush and focused on what I could control, which eased my stress.
