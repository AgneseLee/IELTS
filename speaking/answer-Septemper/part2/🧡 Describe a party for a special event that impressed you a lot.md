## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a party for a special event that impressed you a lot**

> **You should say:**
> - When and where it was held
> - What the party was held for
> - How many people attended it
> - And explain why it impressed you a lot

> Bank: B6 | Modules: birthday-dinner / lottery-joke / reconnecting

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
A party that really impressed me was my birthday dinner at my apartment in Beijing last year. There were four of us: Stella, two other close friends and me. I cooked a simple meal to celebrate. We hadn't met as a group for months, so everyone was rather quiet at first. After dinner, Stella mentioned two lottery tickets we'd bought in Malaysia. Neither had won, but she joked that we'd bought back our luck. She sounded so serious that we all burst out laughing, and the conversation became much easier.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
What stayed with me was the warm and relaxed atmosphere. I used to think a memorable party needed expensive food or lots of guests. That evening changed my mind because one small joke helped us reconnect. It also taught me something about communication. Stella noticed that we were a little awkward and found a friendly way to include everyone. Since then, I've tried to pay more attention to quieter people at gatherings. I ask simple questions and give them time to answer. For me, a successful celebration is one where everyone feels comfortable joining in. That's why this small birthday party impressed me so much.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: Stella helped everyone join the conversation, and I learned to include quieter guests.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: My birthday dinner with Stella and two other close friends.
- Background: It was held at my Beijing apartment last year after months apart.
- Main event: Stella joked about losing lottery tickets and helped the four of us reconnect.
- Ability shown: Noticing an awkward mood and making conversation welcoming.
- Reflection: A memorable party depends on people feeling included.
