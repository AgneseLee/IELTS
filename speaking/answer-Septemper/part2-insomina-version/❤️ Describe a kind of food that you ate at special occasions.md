## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a kind of food that you ate at special occasions**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is
> - What occasion it was
> - Why you would like to eat it
> - And explain how you feel about it

> Bank: B6 | Modules: dumplings / family-preparation / shared-memory

> Logic: L1-light · emotional reset: stress → pause → steady action → clearer mind

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Leadership & Collaboration
- Reason: I share the preparation with my relatives, repair broken wrappers with them, and contribute to a meal we complete together.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Homemade pork and vegetable dumplings.
- Background: My family prepares them together during Lunar New Year.
- L1 trigger: Stress was affecting sleep, so the experience became a cue to change.
- Main event: My mother makes the filling while the younger relatives fold the wrappers.
- Ability shown: I cooperate with my relatives and take responsibility for one part of the shared preparation.
- Reflection: Emotional reset: stress → pause → steady action → clearer mind.
