## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a time when you sent a message or an email to someone but received no reply for a long time**

> **You should say:**
> - Who you sent it to
> - What the message/email was about
> - Whether you finally received the reply
> - And explain how you felt about the experience

> Bank: B4 | Modules: project-message / polite-follow-up / delayed-reply

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: I explained the deadline clearly and followed up politely without blaming Stella.
- Secondary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I managed the uncertainty, adjusted my assumptions, and focused on the actions within my control.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: I sent a project message to Stella, my former colleague.
- Background: I needed technical information from her before an approaching deadline.
- Main event: After three days without a reply, I followed up politely and eventually received the information.
- Ability shown: Clear communication, patience, and adaptability under uncertainty.
- Reflection: The delay taught me not to interpret silence too quickly or personally.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I remember sending a message to Stella, my former colleague, while we worked on a software project. I needed technical information to finish my section before a deadline. I sent it on Tuesday but received no reply for three days. I assumed she had overlooked it and became stressed. Instead of sending repeated messages, I waited one day and sent a polite follow-up. I stated the deadline and asked whether everything was all right. She replied on Friday, explained that she had been dealing with a personal issue, and sent the information.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Initially, I felt <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">frustrated</mark> and slightly <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">ignored</mark>, but her explanation changed my perspective. The experience improved my communication because I learned to express urgency without sounding accusatory.

It also made me more adaptable under uncertainty. I could not control Stella's reply, but I could control my wording, timing, and assumptions. If I need time myself, I acknowledge the message instead of disappearing. Looking back, the delay did not damage our relationship. It taught me that silence has many possible causes and good communication requires patience as well as speed. I still value timely replies, but I no longer make quick judgments. This helps me avoid misunderstandings and remain calmer in conversations.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
