## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a time when you received good service from a staff member in a shop**

> **You should say:**
> - Where the shop is
> - When you went to the shop
> - What service you received from the staff
> - And explain how you felt about the service

## 1. Better Version（最新最小改动版）

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future and my work. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. That's why I went to a pharmacy near my apartment one day. I wanted to buy something to help me sleep. A staff member there listened very patiently and asked how long this had been going on. After hearing my explanation and feelings, she suggested that I talk to a doctor at a hospital. She didn't push me to buy any medicine right away. Instead, she gave me time to explain how I was feeling. What impressed me most was that she took my problem seriously. I felt understood and supported after hearing what she told me. Even though it didn't lead to a sale, she was just trying to help me. It touched me a lot, and it also changed what I look for in a shop. **Now I'd rather go back to a place where the staff really listen and give honest advice than one with just discounts or quick hellos.**

## 3. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

| 原始表达 / 问题 | 修改表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| asked me that how | asked me how | 间接疑问句由 how 引导，不加 that。 |
| how long this had been going | how long this had been going on | go on 表示持续。 |
| suggested going to a hospital | suggested that I talk to a doctor at a hospital | 原表达语法可成立；that I talk 明确建议的执行者，不属于必改语法错误。 |
| a sell | a sale | sale 是名词。 |
| help me on my side, from my situation | she was just trying to help me | 原表达不自然；直接表达她想帮我。 |
| I'd happily to go | I'd happily go / I'd rather go | would 后用动词原形；rather 表达偏好。 |
| the staff listen carefully and give honest advice | the staff really listen and give honest advice | 原句主谓一致正确；staff 指员工群体时可搭配复数动词，尤其在英式英语中。 |
