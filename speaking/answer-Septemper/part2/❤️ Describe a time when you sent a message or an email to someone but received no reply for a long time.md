## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a time when you sent a message or an email to someone but received no reply for a long time**

> **You should say:**
> - Who you sent it to
> - What the message/email was about
> - Whether you finally received the reply
> - And explain how you felt about the experience

> Bank: B4 | Modules: sleeplessness / delayed-reply / advice

## 1. Band 7 Answer

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my career and my future. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. So one night, I sent a message to my best friend Stella to ask for help. But she didn't reply to me immediately, which made me even more frustrated and anxious. I felt slightly ignored at that time, and somehow, I worried I would probably stay up all night and wouldn't be able to sleep at all. The next morning, Stella replied to my message and explained why she hadn't replied the night before. She had been dealing with a personal issue and had just missed my message. Afterwards, she called me to comfort me. She said, “Don't push yourself so hard. Slow is fast.” She also said she would always stand by me whenever I needed her.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Hearing that really relieved some of my stress and lifted my mood a little. I used to think I had to finish every task on my own and handle every challenge immediately to prove that I was capable and strong enough. But after hearing what she told me, I realized that slowing down didn't mean falling behind. Instead, it could actually help me focus on what I could control and what mattered most at the moment. That experience really made me calmer. And thanks to her response, I slept better the next night.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I reconsidered the pressure I put on myself and accepted that slowing down could help me focus on what mattered.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: My best friend Stella and a message asking for help.
- Background: Worries about my career and future had kept me awake for several nights.
- Main event: Stella replied the next morning, explained her delay, and called to comfort me.
- Ability shown: Accepting support and adjusting my expectations of myself.
- Reflection: Slowing down did not mean falling behind; her response helped me feel calmer and sleep better.
