## Cue Card

**Describe an event you attended in which you didn't enjoy the music played**

> **You should say:**
> - What it was
> - Who you went with
> - Why you decided to go there
> - And explain why you didn't enjoy it

> Bank: B6 | Modules: outdoor-festival / poor-sound / leave-early

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: I explained my discomfort honestly to Stella instead of pretending to enjoy the event.
- Secondary Ability: Problem-solving Skills
- Reason: I identified the venue and sound balance as the problem and planned practical changes for future events.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: An outdoor music festival in a crowded Beijing park.
- Background: I attended with Stella because she liked one of the bands.
- Main event: Excessive volume and poor sound caused a headache, so I left early.
- Ability shown: Honest communication and analysis of the real problem.
- Reflection: The venue, rather than the musicians, caused the disappointing experience.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The event was an outdoor music festival in a Beijing park, and I went with Stella and several former colleagues. I agreed to go because Stella liked one of the bands, and I expected a relaxed evening on the grass. Instead, the venue was overcrowded, and people constantly pushed past us to film videos. More importantly, the speakers were uncomfortably loud and badly balanced, so I could hardly hear the singer's voice. After about an hour, I developed a headache and could not talk with my friends properly. I explained the problem to Stella and left early.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I felt disappointed but did not blame the musicians. The song sounded clear through headphones later, confirming that the sound system ruined the experience.

The event improved my communication because I gave Stella an honest explanation instead of pretending. She understood, avoiding unnecessary misunderstanding. It also sharpened my problem-solving: I separated the band from the venue and identified changes for next time. I would read venue reviews, stand farther from the speakers, and find a quieter area. Looking back, a shared atmosphere is enjoyable only when people can listen safely. I now strongly prefer smaller indoor performances where the music itself remains the main musical focus.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
