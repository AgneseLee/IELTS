## Final Integrated Version (7.0–7.5)

The diagram illustrates the seven-stage process used to produce sugar from sugar cane.

Overall, this is a linear process that begins with the cultivation of sugar cane and ends with the drying and cooling of the finished sugar crystals. The agricultural phase takes between 12 and 18 months, whereas the remaining stages involve harvesting and mechanically processing the crop.

Initially, sugar cane is grown for 12 to 18 months until it reaches maturity. It is then harvested either by a machine or manually by farmers. After harvesting, the sugar cane is crushed by a machine to extract its juice. This marks the beginning of the industrial production process.

During the next stage, the raw juice is passed through a limestone filter to remove impurities. The purified liquid is subsequently heated in an evaporator, causing water to evaporate and the juice to thicken into syrup. This syrup is then placed in a centrifuge, where rapid spinning separates the sugar crystals from the remaining liquid. Finally, the crystals are dried and cooled, producing the finished sugar.

## Summary of Corrections

### 审题与信息准确性

| 原文问题 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `the proceeding other five steps` | `the remaining stages` | 生长之后还有六个阶段，而不是五个 |
| `transported to industry` | 删除 | 图中没有展示运输或工厂 |
| `Crushing implies... refinement` | `the crusher... extracts their juice` | 破碎阶段的具体作用是榨取汁液 |
| `sugar is extracted after drying and cooling` | `the crystals are dried and cooled` | 糖晶体在离心阶段已被分离，最后一步只是干燥和冷却 |
| Overview 未说明整体流程类型 | `this is a linear process` | Process diagram 的 overview 应概括起点、终点及主要特征 |

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `sugar canes` | `sugar cane` | 表示这种农作物或原材料时通常用不可数形式 |
| `final sugar product` | `finished sugar` | 避免重复，表达更自然 |
| `original material growth` | `cultivation of the raw material` / `agricultural phase` | 原搭配不自然 |
| `proceeding steps` | `subsequent stages` / `remaining stages` | `proceeding` 不表示“之后的” |
| `undergo harvest` | `are harvested` | 正确搭配及被动结构 |
| `by tractors` | `by a harvesting machine` | 图中是收割机，并非普通拖拉机 |
| `transported to industry` | 删除 | `industry` 不是具体地点，且图中无此步骤 |
| `go crushing` | `are crushed` / `are fed into a crusher` | 原表达不符合英语搭配 |
| `crushing material plants` | `crushing the harvested cane` | 原表达语义不清 |
| `main resources for limestone filter` | `raw juice is passed through a limestone filter` | 果汁是被过滤的物质，不是过滤器的“资源” |
| `converts into syrup` | `is converted into syrup` / `thickens into syrup` | 该过程需要被动结构或表示状态变化的动词 |
| `step to centrifuge` | `placed in a centrifuge` | `centrifuge` 在图中是设备名词 |
