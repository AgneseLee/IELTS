## Cue Card

**Describe an interesting building**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - What it looks like
> - What function it has
> - And explain why you think it is interesting

> Bank: B7 | Modules: office-skyscraper / city-view / daily-comfort

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Creativity & Innovation
- Reason: I examined both the skyscraper's impressive design and its practical weaknesses, then considered realistic ways to improve it.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: An office skyscraper near my former workplace in Beijing.
- Background: Stella and I visited it several times for meetings.
- Main event: Its glass exterior, bright atrium, and city view impressed me, while its crowded lifts and sealed windows revealed practical weaknesses.
- Ability shown: I explored ways to improve the building's ventilation, lifts, and outdoor space.
- Reflection: The building taught me that interesting design should create a better everyday experience, not merely a striking image.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
An interesting building I know is an office skyscraper near my former workplace in Beijing. Stella and I visited it several times for meetings. It has a glass exterior, a bright atrium, and upper floors with a wide city view. Its main function is providing offices, but it also contains cafés and shared meeting rooms. I found the contrast especially interesting. The tower uses limited urban land efficiently and provides plenty of natural light, yet its crowded lifts and sealed windows create ordinary problems for the people inside.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
That contrast changed the way I judge creative design. I used to think an unusual shape or dramatic skyline was enough to make architecture successful. Now I pay more attention to the experience it creates over time. The building encouraged me to think creatively and explore practical improvements. Better lift management, accessible outdoor areas, and natural ventilation could make it healthier and easier to use. This is the kind of innovation I value because it improves an existing method instead of chasing novelty. Looking back, the skyscraper is interesting because it is not perfect. It gives me both impressive design details and real problems to analyse. That makes it more memorable than a building that simply looks attractive in a photograph.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
