## Final Integrated Version (7.0–7.5)

The bar chart illustrates how the distribution of annual household income changed in the US in 2007, 2011 and 2015.

Overall, the proportion remained generally steady over the 8-year period. The number of households with an annual income of over $100,000 accounted for the lion's share in both 2007 and 2015, although it was surpassed by the $25,000–$49,999 bracket and fell to second position in 2011.

Looking at the figure for the highest level of annual income, it fluctuated over the course of 8 years, plummeting to roughly 28m in 2011 before peaking at 33m in 2015. In 2007, it stood at approximately 30m, ranking first among all categories. Meanwhile, the lowest two income levels, which were one below $25,000 and one between $25,000 and $49,999, stood in stark contrast to it and recorded peaks of 28m and 30m in 2011, before dropping to approximately 28m and 29m by 2015, respectively. Furthermore, the two middle brackets, $50,000–$74,999 and $75,000–$99,999, remained relatively stable throughout the period.

## Summary of Corrections

### 语法 & 拼写

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `distribution annual` | `distribution **of** annual`（缺介词）|
| `2001` | `2011`（年份笔误）|
| `remained general steady` | `remained **generally** steady`（副词）|
| `figures of` | `figure **for**`（介词）|
| `plummeting **at**` | `plummeting **to**`（介词）|
| `peaking at 33m **by** 2015` | `**in** 2015`（特定数据点用 in）|
| `fall to second place` | `**fell** to second position`（过去式）|
| `which **are**` | `which **were**`（时态一致）|
| 缺逗号：`...and $49,999 stood` | `...and $49,999**, stood**` |
| Comma splice：`stood..., recorded` | `stood... **and** recorded` |

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `over 100,000 dollars on annual income` | `with an annual income of over $100,000` | 固定搭配 |
| `the level of income between $25,000 and $49,999` | `the $25,000–$49,999 bracket` | bracket 更简洁正式 |
| `one less than $25,000` | `one below $25,000` | "one less" 暗示少一个单位（即$24,999）|
| `occupied the largest 2 proportions` | `recorded peaks of 28m and 30m` | 这是绝对数量非比例 |
| `medium income level accounting for the remaining amount` | `the two middle brackets, $50,000–$74,999 and $75,000–$99,999` | 原句结构混乱 |
| `having been surpassed... though` | `although it was surpassed...` | though 放句尾是口语用法 |

### 数据错误

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| $100k+ 在 2011 年：`23m` | `28m` |
| 单位错误：`dropped by 1% to 3%` | `dropped by approximately 1–2 million` |

### `respectively` 修复

`respectively` 须配对两组平行数据：

- ✗ `with a high of 28m and 30m in 2011 and dropped slightly by 1-2 million in 2015, respectively` — 后半段未分别对应两个区间
- ✓ `recorded peaks of 28m and 30m in 2011, before dropping to approximately 28m and 29m by 2015, respectively` — 清晰配对每个区间的峰值与终值
