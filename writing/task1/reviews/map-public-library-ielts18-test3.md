## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] Overview 中 `make way for accessibility for people` 属于图中无法验证的目的推断。只能描述可见事实：`the central tables and chairs have been removed, creating an open central area`。
- [TA] `digital media room` 不是原图标签；旧区域应准确写成 `a room for CDs, videos and computer games`。
- [TA] `a general children story room` 混淆了三个同时存在的元素；现在的西北房间包含 `children's fiction books, sofas and a storytelling area`。
- [TA] 现在东侧由北向南依次为 adult fiction books、information desk 和 self-service machines。原文把后两者的相对方向写错，并使用了 `audit fiction books`。
- [TA] 原文正确识别了 entrance 保持不变，但应在 overview 中将其作为主要稳定特征明确提出。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 使用“Twenty years ago—Today”的时间分组是正确的，可以保留。
- [CC] 旧布局段基本遵循中心—两侧—四角的顺序，但 `across to the hall` 没有明确连接哪两个设施；应直接说明两个房间分别位于西北和西南角。
- [CC] 当前布局段从西北跳到西南，再跳到东南和东北，最后返回中央两侧。最小调整是按西侧和东侧分别描述，使读者能顺时针还原平面图。
- [CC] `of which with the entrance remaining...` 同时叠加两个连接结构，应拆成清楚的独立关系：`The entrance, however, has remained in its original position.`

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] ⚠ recurring `experienced a significant development` → `has been extensively modernised and reorganised`：室内布局变化不用空泛的 `development`。
- [LR] `incorporated with facilities` → `equipped with new facilities` / `has gained new facilities`，前者搭配不自然。
- [LR] `children story room` → `children's fiction section with a storytelling area`，准确区分藏书区与活动区。
- [LR] `flanked either side of` → `were positioned on either side of`；`flank` 若作动词，应写 `A and B flanked the central space`。
- [LR] `demolished` 对可移动的 tables and chairs 过强，使用 `removed` 更准确。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] ⚠ recurring `has been undergone` → `has undergone` / `has been modernised`：`undergo` 不使用这种被动形式。
- [GR] ⚠ recurring `the seating area situated`、`a cafe and a lecture room located` 均缺少 `be`：应写 `was situated`、`are located`。
- [GR] `Before 20 years ago` → `Twenty years ago`：`before` 与 `ago` 不能这样重复标记过去时间。
- [GR] `in where the reading room was` → `where the reading room used to be`：`where` 本身已经表示地点。
- [GR] `the northeastern and southeastern corner` → `the north-eastern and south-eastern corners`：两个角落需要复数。
- [GR] `children book area` → `children's book area`，表示儿童图书需要所有格形式。

## Final Integrated Version(183) (7.0–7.5)

The diagrams compare a public library's layout 20 years ago and today.

Overall, the library has been extensively modernised and reorganised. Several traditional reading and book areas have been replaced by facilities for computers, events and refreshments, while the central tables and chairs have been removed. The entrance is the only feature that has retained its original position.

Twenty years ago, tables and chairs occupied the centre, flanked by adult fiction books to the west and adult non-fiction books to the east. The north-western corner contained CDs, videos and computer games, while children's books were housed in the north-east. A reading room occupied the south-western corner, opposite an enquiry desk in the south-east.

Today, the north-western room contains children's fiction books, sofas and a storytelling area, while all reference books and a computer room occupy the remaining western side. In the east, a lecture room has replaced the children's section, and adult fiction books, an information desk and self-service machines are arranged from north to south. The former enquiry desk has become a cafe, while the entrance remains unchanged at the southern end.
