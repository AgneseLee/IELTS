## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a home that you like to visit but do not want to live in**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - What it is like
> - Why you like to visit it
> - And explain why you would not like to live there

> Bank: B7 | Modules: high-rise home / city view / daily comfort

## 6. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

> 根据本轮原回答和错误汇总整理；“可优化”表示自然度调整，不代表原表达必然有语法错误。

| 原表达 / 问题 | 最小修改 / 推荐表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| high rise residential building | high-rise residential building | 书写时复合形容词 high-rise 加连字符；这不是口语发音错误。 |
| visit there | visit her / visit her apartment | visit 通常直接接人或地点；也可说 go there。 |
| just simply | just | 可精简：两个副词在此重复。 |
| lay on the sofa | lie on the sofa | 描述平时习惯用 lie；过去式才是 lay。 |
| literatures | literature | 这里泛指文学，用不可数形式 literature。 |
| make coffee and drinking them / just chatting | make some coffee, drink it, and just chat | 并列动词形式一致；此处 coffee 用 it 指代。 |
| in the balcony | on the balcony | 固定地点搭配 on the balcony。 |
| the room sometimes feel | the rooms sometimes feel / the room sometimes feels | 检查主谓一致；单数 room 配 feels，复数 rooms 配 feel。 |
| high speed roads | a highway | 此处用 highway 表达高速公路更自然。 |
| interrupt me while I have to study | kept interrupting me while I was studying | kept 后接 doing；假设情境中与过去式条件保持一致。 |
| study or learning English or learning some new skills | studying English or learning new skills | 并列动词保持相同形式。 |
| would probably got crazy | would probably go crazy | 情态动词 would 后接原形 go。 |
| but rather live there | but I'd rather not live there | would rather + 动词原形；此处需要 not 表达不想住在那里。 |
