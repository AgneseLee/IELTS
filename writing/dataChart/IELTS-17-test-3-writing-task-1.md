## Summary of Corrections

### 语法 & 拼写

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `dramatical` | `dramatic` |
| `upward trending` | `upward trend` |
| `account for` | `accounted for`（时态）|
| `drop` / `climb` | `dropped` / `climbed`（时态）|
| `descreased` / `maginally` / `neligibly` | `decreased` / `marginally` / `negligibly` |

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `families' weekly income` | `weekly expenditure` | 图表是支出不是收入 |
| `allocation for food` | `spending on food` | allocation 用于机构预算 |
| `the most decreased one` | `which saw the steepest decline` | awkward |
| `transformed from a mere X to a more significant Y` | `climbed sharply` | 套词 |

### 程度副词

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| food `substantially` (35→17%) | `dramatically` | 降幅 >50% |
| clothing `marginally` (10→5%) | `considerably` | 降幅 50% |
| leisure `considerably` (9→22%) | `more than doubled` | 涨幅 144% |

### 结构 & 逻辑

| 问题 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| Overall `almost to triple` vs body `more than doubled` | 统一为 `more than doubled`，数据前后一致 |
| `in contrast to leisure soared` | `in contrast to leisure, which soared`（contrast to 后接名词短语）|
