## Original Draft(226)
I believe it is an overall positive trend.

First of all, advertisements undoubtedly bring substantial benefits both for individuals and companies. One key strength is its ability to help consumers to make informed decisions by providing as much information as possible through advertising. Individuals can compare prices, qualities, and features of products among multiple choices, allowing them to select and purchase one that is most suitable for them. Moreover, aiming for winning consumers’ favor, such competition encourages companies to produce products that are cheaper yet with better quality, thereby improving basic technology involved and making much profits for people. As a result, advertisements not only help consumers obtain sufficient information but also contribute to sustainable long-term improvement in commercial business. 

However, opponents may argue that advertisements produce harmful effects on consumers by leading to excessive consumption. When people are constantly exposed to repeated advertisements, they may be induced to buy something they don’t truly need. In some cases, they make purchases on account of appealing prices and exaggerating functions products don’t have. Nevertheless, a viable solution is to introduce and strictly enforce advertising regulations, which limit the number of advertisements showing. Furthermore, regulators should supervise whether advertisements present with emotional words or mislead people with fake data. If implemented properly, it can address the problem of misleading context while curbing excessive consumption in the long run.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] `it` 没有在已提交文本中说明指什么。完整题目缺失，不能确认所谓 trend 是广告增多还是其他现象；本次暂改为明确评价广告的作用，实际考试应对准题目中的具体趋势。
- [TA] ⚠ recurring：笔记已提醒避免过度绝对化。`undoubtedly` 本身不是语法错误，但这里用 `can bring` 更符合有条件的收益。`as much information as possible` 强调数量；能支持知情选择的是相关、可靠的信息，而非越多越好。
- [TA] `cheaper yet with better quality → ... profits` 缺少机制。补出“改进生产技术 → 降低成本并吸引更多买家 → 可能提高利润”；降价本身不能证明利润增加。
- [TA] 限制广告数量、检查情绪化用语都不能自动消除过度消费。把前者落实为限制同一广告的重复展示，把后者落实为移除误导性主张、要求可核实证据，并用 `help curb` 限定效果。
- [TA] 对正负评价类题目，监管应服务于“为什么总体仍然有益”的判断。原文最后停在解决问题；修改版明确保留信息收益、减少购买压力，使回应回到立场。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] `such competition` 前文只谈消费者比较，没有明确说明竞争如何形成。加入 `when consumers can compare competing offers`，再写企业争取消费者，衔接更完整。
- [CC] 原文一个长句同时承担竞争、质量、技术和利润四个环节。最小调整版拆成两句，只补足原有论点的机制。
- [CC] `If implemented properly, it...` 的 `it` 指代不清。用 `these measures` 明确指向前面两项监管措施。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `benefits both for individuals and companies` → `benefits to both individuals and companies`：后者是此处更自然的搭配，原表达不妨碍理解。
- [LR] `qualities` → `quality`：这里是产品质量；`qualities` 常表示某人或某物的多种特质。
- [LR] `cheaper yet with better quality` → `cheaper yet of better quality`；`basic technology involved` → `production technology`，明确所指技术。
- [LR] `making much profits for people` → `generating greater profits for businesses`：搭配、数量修饰及受益主体均需明确。
- [LR] `exaggerating functions products don’t have` → `false claims about product features`：不存在的功能属于虚假宣称；已有功能被夸大才用 `exaggerated claims`。
- [LR] `emotional words` → `emotive language` 是表达优化；但情绪化语言不一定构成误导，论证上应针对误导性主张及证据。
- [LR] `misleading context` → `misleading content`：`context` 是语境、背景，`content` 才是广告内容。
- [LR] `on account of`、`appealing prices` 并非必改错误；改成 `because of`、`attractive prices` 是自然度优化。`sustainable long-term improvement in commercial business` 则过于抽象，宜明确为企业表现的长期改善。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `advertisements ... its ability` → `advertisements ... their ability`：代词与复数先行词一致。
- [GR] `aiming for winning consumers’ favor, such competition...` 存在悬垂修饰：争取消费者的是企业。表达版改为 `competition to win consumers’ favour`；最小调整版让 `companies` 明确承担这一行为。
- [GR] ⚠ recurring：笔记已有可数名词数量搭配问题。`much profits` → `greater profits` / `more profit`，不能用 much 直接修饰复数 profits。
- [GR] `advertisements showing` → `advertisements shown`：这里指“被展示的广告”，需要过去分词。
- [GR] `advertisements present with emotional words` → `advertisements use emotive language`，原谓语结构不成立；也可写 `advertisements are presented using emotive language`。
- [GR] `help consumers to make` 正确，`to` 可保留或省略；`don’t` 可在正式作文中改为 `do not`，但并非语法错误。`favor` 是正确美式拼写，`favour` 只是英式统一。

## Final Integrated Version(213) (7.0–7.5)
I believe advertising is broadly beneficial, provided that effective regulation limits its harmful effects.

First of all, advertising can benefit both consumers and businesses by making product information more accessible. Consumers can compare the prices, quality and features of different products and choose those that best meet their needs. When buyers can compare competing offers, companies have a stronger incentive to offer better value. This can encourage firms to improve production technology so that they can deliver higher quality at lower cost. Businesses that attract more customers while controlling costs may then earn greater profits. Advertising can therefore support informed purchasing decisions and improvements in business performance.

However, repeated exposure to advertisements may encourage people to buy products they do not really need. Attractive prices and false claims about product features can make unnecessary purchases seem worthwhile. To reduce this pressure, regulators could limit how often the same advertisements are shown to consumers. They should also require advertisers to remove misleading claims and provide verifiable evidence for statements about product performance. These measures would reduce repeated sales pressure and help consumers judge products more accurately. Although regulation cannot eliminate unnecessary consumption, it can help preserve the informational benefits of advertising while limiting the risk of consumers being pressured or misled into buying things.
