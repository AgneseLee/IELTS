## Summary of Corrections

### 审题错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| Musical performances reached `20` in 2020 | Musical performances reached `about 18` | The final point is below the 20-participant gridline. |
| Film club and martial arts had averages of `64 and 34` | They fluctuated at around `60–66 and 32–38` | The chart does not provide averages; observable ranges describe the data more reliably. |

### 语法 & 拼写

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `participants who enroll` | `participants who enrolled` / `people who took part` |
| `this was an almost opposite pattern occurred` | `an almost opposite pattern occurred` / `table tennis followed an almost opposite pattern` |
| `by end of the period` | `by the end of the period` |

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `participants who enroll in activities` | `participation in activities` | The chart shows participation, not necessarily enrolment. |
| `recorded the highest participants` | `recorded the highest number of participants` | It is the number, rather than the people themselves, that is highest. |
| `the number of amateur dramatics` | `participation in amateur dramatics` | Amateur dramatics is an activity, not a countable group. |
| `climbed up` | `climbed` | `Climb` already expresses upward movement. |
| `maintained steady growth` | `grew steadily` / `maintained a steady upward trend` | The original verb–noun combination is unnatural in this context. |

### 结构改进

The original response places all detailed figures in one long paragraph. The integrated version groups the film club and martial arts together because they remained relatively stable, then discusses amateur dramatics, table tennis and musical performances together because they showed clearer directional changes. This makes the comparisons easier to follow and reduces dependence on contrast markers.
