## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a person you know who would like to choose a career in the medical field (e.g. a doctor, a nurse)**

> **You should say:**
> - When you knew him/her
> - When he/she started to think about that
> - What he/she would like to do
> - And explain why he/she would like to choose this career

> Bank: B8 | Modules: nursing / first-aid / career-motivation

> Logic: L1-light · emotional reset: stress → pause → steady action → clearer mind

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Learning Ability
- Reason: Lin studies clinical skills seriously and learns from direct experience caring for patients and family members.
- Secondary Ability: Communication Skills
- Reason: She explains care calmly and helps frightened people feel safer.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: My cousin Lin, a nursing student who hopes to work in emergency care.
- Background: I have known her since childhood, but she considered nursing during our grandmother's illness.
- L1 trigger: Stress was affecting sleep, so the experience became a cue to change.
- Main event: She later handled my cut hand calmly at a family gathering and explained what to watch for.
- Ability shown: She combines continuous learning with clear, reassuring communication.
- Reflection: Emotional reset: stress → pause → steady action → clearer mind.

She cleaned it, checked it carefully, and explained the warning signs. Her calm manner immediately made me feel <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">safer</mark>. That became the turning point I needed. Instead of pushing harder, I paused and focused on one manageable step at a time. Once I slowed down, I could think more clearly and make fewer mistakes. The improvement was gradual, but my sleep and confidence both recovered. I realised that slowing down does not mean falling behind; steady action can move me forward faster. Since then, when pressure builds, I protect my sleep, reduce unnecessary tasks, and notice ordinary moments. I now feel calmer and enjoy daily life more.
