## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 所有百分比均与图表一致；概述已经识别出最大用户群体和不满意度最高的两项，这些应保留。
- [TA] “the opening hours took the lead” 未明确比较指标。如果指 very satisfied，staff helpfulness 才最高（95% 对 65%）；如果指两档满意合计，两项均为 100%，只能说并列。建议写 “Neither service attracted dissatisfaction”，再比较两项的 very satisfied 比例。
- [TA] “a relatively even distribution” 不适用于 50%、40%、10%：三档差距明显。可以直接报告数据，或比较 books 的不满意率仅为 10%，低于 journals 和 wi-fi 的 20% 和 19%。
- [TA] “education roles / user education status” 不能准确涵盖学生类型及 academic staff；用 “user categories” 或 “the composition of university library users”。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 四段结构合理。本题两张图分别描述用户构成和服务评价，分置两个正文段自然，不必为了混合图而强行交叉合并。
- [CC] 表格段连续写三档数字，读者需要自行寻找比较。将 opening hours 与 staff helpfulness 归为无不满意评价的一组，再比较 journals、wi-fi 和 books。
- [CC] “followed a similar pattern” 只有在明确指两项均无不满意评价时才准确；不能掩盖 very satisfied 的 30 个百分点差距。
- [CC] 原文 226 词并非本身违规，但有压缩空间。优先删掉反复出现的 category、levels 和冗长引入语，把篇幅留给比较。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] ⚠ recurring：笔记已记录占比与排名搭配。“took the largest share throught all categories” 可简化为 “formed the largest group”；“The figure of distance learning” 改为 “Distance learners represented 8%”。
- [LR] ⚠ recurring：静态图笔记已强调 “relatively even / close” 必须有数值支持；50%、40%、10% 不符合该条件。
- [LR] “the top two unstatisfied categories” → “the two services with the highest dissatisfaction rates”。不满意的是用户，服务项目获得不满意评价。
- [LR] “staff service” → “staff helpfulness”；“showing very satisfied” → “very satisfied” 或 “reporting that they were very satisfied”。
- [LR] 拼写应集中修正：throught、unstatisfied、statisfied、stasfied、avaliability、farily、satifaction。重复拼错 satisfied / satisfaction 尤其影响词汇准确性。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] “Users showed overall satisfied” → “Users were generally satisfied”。satisfied 是形容词，不能直接作 showed 的宾语。本题这两项可更精确地写 “All users were either very or fairly satisfied”。
- [GR] “academic staffs” → “academic staff”：这里 staff 指该校学术人员整体，不加 s。
- [GR] “the satisfaction of library's services” → “users’ satisfaction with library services”：使用 satisfaction with；若保留所有格则需 the library’s。
- [GR] “an relatively even distribution” → “a relatively even distribution”：冠词取决于紧随其后的 relatively 的起始音；但修正冠词后，本题仍有上述数据解读问题。
- [GR] “compared with 7% of the academic staff” → “compared with 7% for academic staff”：原句容易变成“学术人员中的 7%”，而图中表示他们占全部用户的 7%。

Overall, full-time undergraduates formed the largest user group, while academic staff and distance learners were the smallest groups. Staff helpfulness received the highest proportion of very satisfied responses, whereas journals and wi-fi attracted the most dissatisfaction.

By contrast, dissatisfaction with journal availability and wi-fi reliability stood at 20% and 19%, respectively, compared with 10% for books. The proportions very satisfied with these three services were 45%, 48% and 50%, respectively, while the corresponding figures for fairly satisfied users were 35%, 33% and 40%.
