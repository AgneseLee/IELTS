## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement
[TA] 总体判断正确：女性在多数年龄组比例更高，15-24 岁是例外。但 overview 需要更直接概括最高点和最低点。可以写 `"Female participation peaked among 45-to-54-year-olds, while the male rate was highest in the youngest group and lowest among 35-to-44-year-olds."` 原文的 `"stood a highest percentage"` 不够准确，因为主语不是 figure/rate。

### Coherence and Cohesion
[CC] 四段结构是合格的，但正文分组的逻辑可以更清晰。相比 `"proportions with great variation"`，更自然的分组是：先写 25-64 岁女性明显更高，再写两端年龄组，其中 15-24 岁反转，65 岁及以上几乎相同。

### Lexical Resource
[LR] ⚠ recurring `"accounted for higher proportions"` 可以理解，但描述某群体比例更高时，`"had higher participation rates"` 更直接。`"gap against male"` 应改为 `"gap for men"` 或 `"compared with men"`。`"physical activities"` 在这里应使用不可数概念 `"physical activity"`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy
[GR] ⚠ recurring 注意拼写和词性：`"noticeble"` -> `"noticeable"`, `"femal"` -> `"females/female"`, `"througout"` -> `"throughout"`。`"male"` 不能直接替代复数人群时的 `"men"`；可写 `"men"` 或作定语的 `"male rates"`。`"who are between 45 and 54"` 可压缩为 `"aged between 45 and 54"`。

## Final Integrated Version(175) (7.0–7.5)
The bar chart compares the proportions of Australian men and women in six age groups who did regular physical activity in 2010.

Overall, women exercised more regularly than men in every age group except those aged 15 to 24. Female participation peaked among 45-to-54-year-olds, while the male rate was highest in the youngest group and lowest among 35-to-44-year-olds.

Among people aged 25 to 64, female rates were consistently above male rates. The widest gap was in the 35-to-44 group, at 52.5% versus 39.5%, followed by the 45-to-54 group, where the figures were 53.3% and 43.1%, respectively. Women aged 55 to 64 also recorded a high rate of 53%, compared with 45.1% for men, and a smaller difference appeared among 25-to-34-year-olds, at 48.9% versus 42.2%.

The pattern differed at the two ends of the age range. In the youngest group, men led women by 52.8% to 47.7%. Among those aged 65 and over, rates were almost identical, at 46.7% for men and 47.1% for women.

Overall, women **had higher participation rates** than men in most age groups, except the **15-to-24 group**. **The highest female figure was recorded among 45-to-54-year-olds, while men reached their peak in the youngest group and their lowest point among 35-to-44-year-olds.**

By contrast, the percentages **were almost even among those aged 65 and over**, with 46.7% for **men** and 47.1% for women. The **15-to-24 group** showed the reverse trend, **with men at 52.8%, whereas the figure for women was 47.7%**.
