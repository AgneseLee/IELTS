## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a person whose health and fitness you have helped to improve**

> **You should say:**
> - Who this person was
> - Why you helped him/her
> - How you helped him/her
> - And explain whether it was easy or difficult

> Bank: B4 | Modules: layoff / advice / manageable-steps

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The person I helped was Stella, my former colleague and one of my best friends. About three months ago, she received a heavier workload after a layoff. She was sleeping badly and had stopped exercising, so I wanted to support her. I suggested short walks after work and joined her whenever we were both free. We started with fifteen minutes rather than an ambitious fitness plan. I also encouraged her to discuss unrealistic deadlines with her manager. Gradually, she became more active and told me she felt less exhausted.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
It wasn't easy at first because she felt too tired to do anything. I had to listen carefully instead of simply telling her what to do. Walking together gave us time to talk, which helped her feel understood and supported. I learned to focus on what I could control: being available and offering encouragement. I couldn't change her workload myself, but I could help her take manageable steps. Looking back, helping someone doesn't mean having all the answers. It means finding something they can actually keep doing. Now, when friends are struggling, I ask what support they want before offering advice.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
