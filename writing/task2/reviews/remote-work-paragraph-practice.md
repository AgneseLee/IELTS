## Original Draft(127)

### 正反论证练习
- 正：远程办公提高满意度、减少通勤、支持工作家庭平衡。
First of all, remote work undoubtedly brings substantial benefits. One key strength is it can reduce commuting time, allowing employees to spend more time on their family responsibilities and maintain better work-life balance. As a result, this practice not only promotes individual job satisfaction but also contributes to higher productivity and competitiveness for companies in the long run.

- 反：远程办公模糊工作生活边界，削弱团队合作。
Nevertheless, it is important to recognize working remote can produce harmful effects when it blurs boundaries between work and life. This practice may lead to working overtime if employees don't excel at planning their schedules and juggling on multiple tasks simultaneously. More importantly, the chronic lacking of face-to-face interactions often generates new problems, such as mitigating team collaboration and ultimately impacting team cohesion, instead of delivering the intended benefits.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 正向段的核心链条 `less commuting → more family time → better work-life balance` 很清楚。
- [TA] 从 `job satisfaction` 直接跳到 `higher productivity and competitiveness` 稍快。应补充机制：满意度和休息改善使员工更专注、更投入，从而提高效率。
- [TA] 负向段把加班主要归因于员工“不擅长安排时间”，弱化了远程办公本身的缺点。更有力的机制是：没有物理上的上下班分界，而且员工下班后仍可通过线上工具被联系。
- [TA] `team collaboration` 和 `team cohesion` 语义接近，最后最好形成递进：沟通减少 → 协调变差 → 凝聚力下降。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 两段都采用“主题句 → 机制 → 结果”的结构，方向正确。
- [CC] `First of all` 通常暗示后面还有同方向的第二点；在单独的正反段落练习中，`On the positive side` 与 `On the negative side` 对称性更强。
- [CC] `More importantly` 使用合理，但末尾 `instead of delivering the intended benefits` 过于模板化，与前面的具体结果衔接不够紧密，可以直接落到员工参与度或团队绩效。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `working remote` 应改为 `working remotely` 或 `remote working`。
- [LR] `juggling on multiple tasks` 应为 `juggling multiple tasks`，`juggle` 后不加 `on`。
- [LR] `mitigating team collaboration` 语义相反：`mitigate` 表示缓解坏事；这里应为 `weakening collaboration`。
- [LR] `boundaries between work and life` 可以升级为更固定的搭配 `work-life boundaries`。
- [LR] `competitiveness for companies` 更自然地写成 `corporate competitiveness` 或 `the competitiveness of companies`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `One key strength is it can...` 缺少表语从句连接词，应为 `One key strength is that it can...`。
- [GR] `maintain better work-life balance` 缺冠词，应为 `maintain a better work-life balance`。
- [GR] `recognize working remote can...` 应为 `recognize that working remotely can...`。
- [GR] `lead to working overtime` 的执行者不清楚，改为 `lead employees to work overtime`。
- [GR] `the chronic lacking of` 中需要名词 `lack`：`the chronic lack of face-to-face interaction`。

## Summary of Corrections

### 语法 & 拼写

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `One key strength is it can` | `One key strength is that it can` |
| `maintain better work-life balance` | `maintain a better work-life balance` |
| `recognize working remote can` | `recognize that working remotely can` |
| `lead to working overtime` | `lead employees to work overtime` |
| `the chronic lacking of` | `the chronic lack of` |

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `juggling on multiple tasks` | `juggling multiple tasks` | `juggle` 是及物动词，不加 `on` |
| `mitigating team collaboration` | `weakening team collaboration` | `mitigate` 通常表示缓解负面问题，方向与原意相反 |
| `competitiveness for companies` | `corporate competitiveness` | 后者更简洁自然 |
| `boundaries between work and life` | `work-life boundaries` | 固定且简洁的书面搭配 |

### 结构改进

正向段需要在满意度与生产率之间补充“更专注、更投入”的机制；负向段应把加班风险归因于远程办公造成的持续在线和物理边界消失，而不是主要归咎于员工时间管理能力。

## Final Integrated Version(109) (7.0–7.5)

On the positive side, remote work can substantially improve employees' working lives. By eliminating daily commuting, it gives workers more time for family responsibilities and makes it easier to maintain a healthy work-life balance. This greater flexibility can improve job satisfaction and help employees remain rested and focused, thereby raising productivity and strengthening corporate competitiveness over time.

On the negative side, working remotely can blur work-life boundaries because employees remain reachable through digital communication outside normal working hours. This constant accessibility may encourage overtime and eventually lead to stress or burnout. Moreover, reduced face-to-face interaction can make spontaneous communication and coordination more difficult, weakening collaboration and, ultimately, team cohesion.
