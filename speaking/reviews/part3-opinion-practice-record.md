## Part 3 观点练习

### 1. Environmental protection 环境保护

**主题链：** `T07 环境与动物` — practical infrastructure and incentives → encourage greener choices → lower pollution and resource waste

**Your original answer**

> Personally, I think both government and individuals should take visible steps to protect the environment. For government, practical infrastructure and financial incentives may encourage greener choices for people. For example, with better public transportation, people would like to use them instead of driving their own private car. That would help lower pollution and resource losses and carbon emissions. As for individuals, people can pick some more greenable choices in their daily life. After all, even small daily choices can make a great difference. For example, people can use a reusable bags instead of single-use plastic bags when shopping. Over time, these habits can reduce environmental damage.

**主要错误 / 不自然**

- `both government and individuals` → `both governments and individuals`
- `For government` → `For governments`
- `greener choices for people` → 直接说 `encourage greener choices`
- `people would like to use them` → `people are more likely to use public transport`
- `resource losses` → `resource waste`
- `greenable choices` → `greener choices`
- `a reusable bags` → `reusable bags` / `a reusable bag`
- `pollution and carbon emissions` 有一点语义重复

**Band 7 version（最小改动）**

> Personally, I think both governments and individuals should take visible steps to protect the environment. For governments, providing better infrastructure and financial incentives can encourage greener choices. For example, with better public transport, people are more likely to commute without using private cars, which reduces emissions and resource waste. As for individuals, even small daily choices can add up and make a big difference. For example, people can use reusable bags instead of single-use plastic bags when shopping. Over time, these habits can help reduce environmental damage.

**复练**

- [ ] 不看稿说出完整逻辑链
- [ ] 正确使用 `governments`、`resource waste` 和 `reusable bags`
- [ ] 用另一个生活例子替换购物袋

### 2. Technology — efficiency and over-reliance 科技提高效率 / 过度依赖

**主题链：** `T06 科技、互联网、信息与影响` — save time and effort → complete tasks more easily → improve efficiency；excessive convenience → put in less active effort → greater dependence

**跨题机制：** `M06 长期能力与短期便利` — excessive help or convenience → dependence → weaker ability

**Your original answer**

> Well, I think technology save people a lot of time and effort. With their automatic assistance, people can complete tasks more easily and efficiently. For example, a software engineer can finish a project in three days, which used to cost fairly a whole month. That would be a great improvement on work efficiency. But on the other hand, people should not overlook the drawbacks technology brings. With the help of technology or AI, they provide too much convenience. So over time people may become over-dependent on them. Because people can put less active effort on their work. Being diligent is demanding and exhausting to some extent. So, over time, less active effort would let people impair their independent thinking and critical thinking ability.

**主要错误 / 不自然**

- `technology save` → `technology saves`
- `automatic assistance` → `automated tools`
- `used to cost a whole month` → 时间用 `used to take a whole month`
- `improvement on work efficiency` → `boost in productivity` / `boost in efficiency`
- `over-dependent on them` → `over-reliant on them` 更自然
- `put less active effort on` → `put in less active effort`
- `let people impair their...` → `may weaken their...`
- `independent thinking and critical thinking ability` → `independent and critical thinking`

**Band 7 version（最小改动）**

> I think technology saves people a lot of time and effort. With automated tools, people can complete tasks more efficiently. For example, a software engineer might finish a new project in three days, which used to take them a whole month. That is a big boost in productivity. But we shouldn't ignore the downsides. Too much convenience can make people over-reliant on technology. When we put in less active effort, over time it may weaken our independent and critical thinking.

**复练**

- [ ] 不看稿说出正向链和反向链
- [ ] 正确使用 `takes time`，不用 `costs time`
- [ ] 将例子替换为学习或日常生活场景

### 3. Technology — internet reshaping habits 互联网重塑习惯

**主题链：** `T06 科技、互联网、信息与影响` — instant access and global connection → faster exchange of information and services → changed habits and wider participation；constant connectivity and algorithm-driven feeds → bias and narrower perspectives → echo chambers and weaker real-world interaction

**Your original answer**

> Well, I think technology offers instant access and global connection. Nowadays, people can exchange their ideas, information and services in real time online, which may lead people to develop new habits or change their original habits. For example, some people who want to clean their house may make an appointment on some housekeeping services online instead of cleaning the house by themselves. That would be a common changes in our daily life. On the other hand, constant connectivity and algorithm exposure online may have some downsides that we should not overlook. When people click on some videos or posts they are interested in, then the platforms tend to push more of the same. So people may become biased toward what they already like. Such a echo chamber may weaken people's real-world interaction over time and narrow their perspectives.

**主要错误 / 不自然**

- `global connection` 可以，但 `global connectivity` 更自然
- `may lead people to develop...` 可简化为 `can change daily habits`
- `make an appointment on some housekeeping services` → `book a cleaning service online`
- `a common changes` → `a common change`
- `algorithm exposure` → `algorithm-driven feeds`
- `a echo chamber` → `an echo chamber`
- `become biased toward what they already like` 自然，可保留
- `narrow their perspectives` 正确、自然

**Band 7 version（最小改动）**

> I think technology offers instant access and global connection. Nowadays, people can exchange ideas, information and services in real time online, which can change their daily habits. For example, some people book a cleaning service online instead of cleaning the house themselves. On the other hand, constant connectivity and algorithm-driven feeds can also shape our habits. When people click on posts or videos they're interested in, platforms tend to push more of the same. This can create echo chambers because people may become biased toward what they already trust or like. In the long run, this may weaken people's real-world interaction and narrow their perspectives.

**复练**

- [ ] 不看稿说出 `algorithm-driven feeds → echo chambers → narrower perspectives`
- [ ] 正确使用 `an echo chamber`
- [ ] 将线上家政例子替换为购物、学习或社交场景

## 本轮高频错误

| Pattern | 你的错误 | 正确表达 |
|---|---|---|
| 单复数 | `a public gardens` / `works` | `public gardens` / `work` |
| 第三人称 -s | `childhood give` / `which help` / `that make` | `gives` / `helps` / `makes` |
| 过去时 | `drawing feels interesting` | `drawing felt interesting` |
| 冠词 | `go to park` / `visiting park` | `go to the park` / `visit a park` |
| 固定搭配 | `appeals me` | `appeals to me` |
| 动词结构 | `help me I’m relaxed` | `help me relax` |
| 自然搭配 | `develop memories` / `various scenery` | `build memories` / `more variety` |
| 容易口误 | `That’s makes me...` | `That makes me...` |
| 主谓一致 | `What matter` / `connection bring` / `culture become` | `What matters` / `connection brings` / `culture becomes` |
| 单复数 | `family` / `different level` / `cost` | `families` / `different levels` / `costs` |
| 非谓语 | `people live in...` | `people living in...` |

本批最需要优先盯住：**主谓一致 → 单复数 → 非谓语**。
