## Original Draft(144)

A significant advantage of technological advancement is that it address the issue of poverty from the ground up. Unlike approaches that manage surface-level issues, the improvements in technology works by generating tremendous output with same resources, such as time and budgets. While AI and automation handle repetitive tasks, employees can focus on higher-value work, thereby producing greater output per hour and ultimately higher business productivity. ## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 正向段实际建立的是 `automation → higher-value work → greater output per hour → higher productivity`，这条链条清楚且有效。
- [TA] 主题句声称科技能从根本上解决贫困，但后文没有说明生产率如何转化为就业、收入增长或减贫，属于核心论点与论据错位。最小修复是把主题句统一为 `improves economic productivity`。
- [TA] `industrial transformation a far more sustainable solution` 没有说明它是什么问题的解决方案。应把结论收束为 `a sustainable driver of industrial growth`，直接回应本段已经证明的生产率优势。
- [TA] 负向段完整覆盖了岗位替代、再培训困难、失业或工资停滞以及收入差距扩大，因果链基本成立。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 正向段从“减贫”转向“生产率”，结尾又转向“产业转型”，中心发生漂移；统一主题后，四句可以形成一条稳定的效率链。
- [CC] `this creates deeper improvements` 和 `this improvement` 指代含糊，而且后者用正面词指代负面后果。分别改为 `these productivity gains` 和 `this development` 更清楚。
- [CC] `Nevertheless`、`A central problem` 和 `As a consequence` 的功能使用正确，能清楚标示转折、解释和结果。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `tremendous output` 过度强调绝对产量；比较同等投入下的效率时，`more output` 更准确。
- [LR] `time and budgets` 并列不自然，企业生产投入可写成 `time and capital`。
- [LR] `higher business productivity` 前缺少能与 `producing` 平行的动作，可改为 `improving business productivity`。
- [LR] `this improvement` 不宜指代可能造成失业的技术变化，宜使用中性的 `this development`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `technological advancement ... address` 应为 `technological advancement ... addresses`。⚠ recurring：第三人称单数主谓一致仍是反复出现的问题。
- [GR] `the improvements ... works` 应为 `improvements ... work`；复数主语使用动词原形。⚠ recurring
- [GR] `that makes` 的先行内容是复数 `improvements`，原结构中应写 `that make`；改写后同样要保持复数主语与谓语一致。⚠ recurring
- [GR] `recognize technological advancement can` 应为 `recognize that technological advancement can`，正式写作中的宾语从句需明确连接。
- [GR] `harmful effect` 应为 `harmful effects`；这里泛指多种就业影响。
- [GR] `this improvement often generate` 应为 `this development often generates`。⚠ recurring
- [GR] `rise in unemployment rate` 应为 `a rise in the unemployment rate`，两个名词都需要正确限定。

## Final Integrated Version(124) (7.0–7.5)

A significant advantage of technological advancement is its ability to raise economic productivity. By using AI and automation to handle repetitive tasks, businesses allow employees to focus on higher-value work. This division of labour enables firms to generate more output from the same amount of time and capital, thereby improving productivity. Over time, these sustained gains can support industrial transformation and make technology a durable driver of economic growth.

Nevertheless, technological advancement can also have harmful effects on employment. Automation replaces many routine jobs, posing serious challenges to low-skilled workers who struggle to retrain. These workers may consequently face unemployment or wage stagnation, both of which can widen income inequality. Technological progress may therefore create serious social costs instead of delivering its intended benefits.
