## Prompt

The maps below show an industrial area in the town of Norbiton, and planned future development of the site.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

## Original Draft(191)

The maps illustrate how Norbiton industrial area is planned to be redeveloped  compared with its present layout.

Overall, the whole area will undergo significant changes. It will be transformed from an industrial area into a residential area with a range of public facilities, such as a school, a playground, shops and a medical center. The road system will be expanded considerably, while the town boundary and most farmland remain unchanged.

Currently, a river runs along the northern edge of the area, separating the factory section from the northern farmland. The town is situated in the western part of the area, while a roundabout stands in the center part of the area. Several factories are located along the road and next to the roundabout. Only one main load running east serves the area.

As planned, all factories will be demolished to make way for housing sections. The road network is planned to be expanded with an additional roundabout and a new bridge, connecting the new houses built across the river. Meanwhile, shops, a medical center, a playground and a school are going to be constructed along the road, near the residential sections.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] “the town is situated in the western part of the area” 仍不够准确：镇区在工业区边界以西，应写 “the town lies to the west of the site”。
- [TA] “Only one main load running east serves the area” 除拼写外还有事实遗漏；中央环岛同时通过南向道路连接南侧主路。
- [TA] 未来住房不只在河对岸，还会分布在中央环岛西北、正北和东侧道路两旁。
- [TA] “shops, a medical center, a playground and a school ... along the road” 过于笼统；商店和医疗中心位于中央环岛西南及东南，游乐场和学校在东侧道路的北部及东端。
- [TA] 概述基本准确，但可补充最显著的变化：“all factories will disappear”。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 现状段可沿“北部河流 → 西侧镇区 → 中央环岛和道路 → 工厂”的顺序推进。
- [CC] 规划段的道路、住房和公共设施应按顺序展开，避免最后一句用 “along the road” 模糊概括所有位置。
- [CC] “Meanwhile” 本身使用正确，但其后的四项设施需要分成两组位置关系，才能形成清晰比较。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] “main load” → “main road” ⚠ recurring：`load` 是负载，`road` 才是道路。
- [LR] “housing sections” → “housing” / “residential buildings”：原搭配不自然。
- [LR] “factory section” → “industrial site”：后者更自然地指整个工业区域。
- [LR] “center part of the area” → “centre of the site”：更简洁、自然。
- [LR] “residential sections” → “residential areas” / “housing”：地图题中更常用。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] “how Norbiton industrial area...” → “how the Norbiton industrial area...”：特指该工业区时需要定冠词。
- [GR] “is planned to be redeveloped compared with...” 语法可理解，但结构偏重；可简化为 “compare the present industrial area with its proposed redevelopment”。
- [GR] “a new bridge, connecting the new houses” 修饰关系不准确：桥本身不是连接住宅的主体，应写 “a road will cross the river via a new bridge to reach the houses”。
- [GR] 删除引言中 `redeveloped` 与 `compared` 之间的双空格及句末多余空格。
