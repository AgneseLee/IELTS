## Cue Card

**Describe a quiet place you like to go**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - How you knew it
> - How often you go there
> - What you do there
> - And explain how you feel about the place

> Bank: B7 | Modules: neighbourhood-park / after-rain / weekly-routine

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I use a simple park routine to adjust my mood and regain focus when work becomes stressful.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: A small park near my apartment in Beijing.
- Background: I discovered it during an evening walk around my neighbourhood.
- Main event: I visit weekly to walk, read, and spend some time away from my phone.
- Ability shown: Adjusting my routine and managing pressure in a practical way.
- Reflection: The park helps me create distance from screens and respond to stress more calmly.
