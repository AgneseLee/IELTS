## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a person you know who has a successful business**

> **You should say:**
> - Who this person is
> - How you got to know him/her
> - Why and how he/she started the business
> - What business he/she does
> - And explain why you think the business is successful

> Bank: B8 | Modules: business / career change

## 1. Band 7 Answer (minimum changes)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The person I'd like to talk about is Stella, one of my former colleagues. About one year ago, our company went through a layoff, and Stella was given a much heavier workload. Gradually, she started having trouble falling asleep. She just couldn't fall asleep naturally, she said. Then she became overwhelmed and kind of burned out. She was worrying about everything that was bothering her, and it put a lot of pressure on her. So that's why she decided to resign from her job. Then she began to figure out what to do for a living. In the end, she decided to start her own business. She became an influencer online and also started an online shop selling handmade products, like postcards, scarves, and personalized journals, things like that.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I think her business is successful, not because she has made any huge achievements, but because it allows her to make a living from something she's really interested in. She also lives a healthier life now and maintains a better work-life balance. She doesn't need to work overtime anymore. Her experience made me realize that success isn't always about money or big achievements. Sometimes, being able to make a living from something you enjoy and have a healthier work-life balance is already a kind of success.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

- Primary Ability: Adaptability

## 6. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

> 根据本轮原回答和错误汇总整理；“可优化”表示自然度调整，不代表原表达必然有语法错误。

| 原表达 / 问题 | 最小修改 / 推荐表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| falling sleep | falling asleep | 固定搭配 fall asleep。 |
| kind of burn out | kind of burned out | 描述精疲力竭的状态用 burned out。 |
| she worrying | she was worrying | 过去进行时需要 was + doing。 |
| decided resign her job and quit | decided to resign from her job | decide 后接 to do；resign from her job 自然常用。resign her job 也可用，并非必然错误；quit 与 resign 在此重复。 |
| how to do for a living | what to do for a living | 这里问做什么谋生，用 what。 |
| hand-making stuffs | handmade products | 形容词用 handmade；stuff 不可数，products 可数。 |
| personalized journal | personalized journals | 列举店内泛指的商品种类，用复数。 |
| not because how big achievement she made | not because she has made any huge achievements | because 后接完整从句；achievement 是可数名词。 |
| make her living with her interest | make a living from something she's really interested in | 用 make a living from 表示靠某事谋生。 |
| She also lived a healthier life now / She didn't need | She also lives a healthier life now / She doesn't need | 描述目前的生活用现在时。 |
