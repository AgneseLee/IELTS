## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a place you have travelled to that you would like to recommend to others**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is
> - Where it is
> - What you saw and did there
> - And explain why you would like to recommend it to others

> Bank: B3 | Modules: Semporna / freediving / sunrise

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I followed safety instructions, controlled my breathing, and adjusted calmly to freediving in an unfamiliar marine environment.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Semporna, a coastal destination in Sabah, Malaysia.
- Background: Stella and I spent the final week of our ten-day Malaysia trip there.
- Main event: We watched the sunrise, tried local food, and went freediving above coral reefs where we saw a sea turtle.
- Ability shown: I adapted to an unfamiliar activity and respected the instructor's safety guidance.
- Reflection: The trip made me more confident outdoors and more protective of the natural world.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I would recommend Semporna, a coastal destination in Sabah, Malaysia. Stella and I took a ten-day Malaysia trip last year and spent the final week there. We tried local dishes, watched the sunrise, and took a boat to several small islands. The highlight was freediving for the first time. Under the crystal-clear water, I saw colourful coral, schools of fish, and a sea turtle moving slowly below us. I followed the instructor's safety guidance and kept a respectful distance from the wildlife. I recommend it because the experience offers more than beautiful photographs.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Freediving made me step outside my comfort zone and improve my adaptability. I had to control my breathing, remain flexible, and respond calmly in an unfamiliar environment. That success made me more confident about trying outdoor activities. The trip also broadened my horizons by making marine protection feel personal rather than abstract. Seeing a turtle in its natural habitat reminded me that visitors should enjoy nature without disturbing it. Looking back, Semporna left a lasting impression because it combined challenge, beauty, and responsibility. I would tell other travellers to choose a responsible guide, listen carefully to safety advice, and avoid touching the coral. With that approach, the place can be exciting while still being treated with respect.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
