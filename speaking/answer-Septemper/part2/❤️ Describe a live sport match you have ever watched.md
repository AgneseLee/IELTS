## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a live sport match you have ever watched**

> **You should say:**
> - What it was
> - When you watched
> - What it was like
> - And explain how you felt about it

> Bank: B6 | Modules: badminton-final / comeback / adapting

## 1. Band 7 Answer

About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. I wanted to make a change and take a break from my daily routine. So that's why I talked to Stella, my best friend, and asked her to watch a badminton final with me, which was held in Beijing last May. I still remember that the arena was crowded and full of energy. In the first game, Sun lost. Then he fell behind by several points in the second game. But he didn't panic. Instead, he played more patiently, made his opponent run around, and waited for a good chance to attack. He won the second game and completed a dramatic comeback in the third. When the final shot landed, the crowd stood up and cheered together. I was really excited, and I'd never felt that level of excitement before. What impressed me most was how calm and adaptable he was. He didn't stick to a strategy that had failed. Instead, he adjusted. It reminded me that success isn't just about talent; it's also about adapting. The match left a lasting impression on me and encouraged me to use feedback more carefully in my life and at work.

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I reconsidered my response to difficulties after watching a player change his approach during a match.
