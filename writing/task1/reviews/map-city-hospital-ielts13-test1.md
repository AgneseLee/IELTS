## Prompt

The maps below show the changes in road access to a city hospital between 2007 and 2010.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

## Original Draft(209)

The two maps illustrate how roads accessing to a city hospital had changed between 2007 and 2010.

Overall, the road network has undergone significantly extensive upgrade, with two more roundabouts, a new bus station center and two separating car parks available for staffs and public. One feature that has remained unchanged is the Ring Road and the City Road.

Looking at the map of 2007, the City Hospital was encircled by the Ring Road, with the Hospital Road connected southward. Six bus stops flanked the Hospital Road with three on each side, while a car park catering to both staff and public stood immediately east to them. The City Road ran along the southern edge of the area and across the Hospital Road.

In 2010, the road system has been reorganized and modernized. Two new roundabouts has been constructed at the southern and northern end of the Hospital Road respectively, while the southern one connected with the City Road. Previous bus stops has been demolished and integrated into a large bus station situated west to the Hospital Road. The old car park remained its original position but has been transformed into one only available for staff, whereas an additional public car park has been added east to the hospital.

## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 概述已抓住环岛、公交站和停车场分离这三个主要变化，这是文章的主要优点。
- [TA] “two more roundabouts” 容易暗示 2007 年已有环岛；应写成 “two new roundabouts”。
- [TA] “the old car park remained its original position” 大致正确，但更精确的比较是原共用停车场改为员工停车场，另在医院东侧新建公众停车场。
- [TA] “integrated into a large bus station” 把六个站点写成了“合并”；图中只能确认站点被移除并以新公交站取代。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] 以 2007 和 2010 为两个主体段是合理分组，建议每段保持“医院环路 → Hospital Road → City Road → 交通设施”的顺序。
- [CC] “east to them” 中 `them` 可能指六个站点或 Hospital Road；改为 “east of Hospital Road” 可消除歧义。
- [CC] 环岛不只是新增设施，还重新组织了 Hospital Road 与 Ring Road、City Road 的连接；正文应明确南侧环岛位于 City Road 交叉口。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] “roads accessing to a city hospital” → “the road system providing access to City Hospital”；`access` 作动词时不接 `to`。
- [LR] “significantly extensive upgrade” → “an extensive upgrade” 或 “was substantially reorganised”；`significantly extensive` 副词与形容词搭配生硬。
- [LR] “a new bus station center” → “a new bus station”；`station` 已能表达设施，`center` 多余。
- [LR] “two separating car parks” → “separate car parks”；`separating` 表示“正在分开其他事物”，不表示“彼此独立”。
- [LR] “staffs” → “staff”；表示员工群体时 `staff` 通常作集体名词。
- [LR] “east to the Hospital Road” → “east of Hospital Road” ⚠ recurring：方位使用 `east/west of`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] “had changed between 2007 and 2010” → “changed between 2007 and 2010”：无另一过去时间点作参照，不需要过去完成时。
- [GR] “the road system has been reorganized” → “the road system was reorganized”：地图结束于 2010 年，应使用过去时。
- [GR] “Two new roundabouts has been constructed” → “Two new roundabouts were constructed” ⚠ recurring：复数主语需要 `were/have`。
- [GR] “Previous bus stops has been demolished” → “The former bus stops were removed” ⚠ recurring：同样存在主谓不一致，且应与历史时间框架保持过去时。
- [GR] “at the southern and northern end” → “at the southern and northern ends”：两个端点需要复数名词。
- [GR] “One feature ... is the Ring Road and the City Road” → “The features ... were Ring Road and City Road”：名词数量和谓语必须一致。

## Final Integrated Version(181) (7.0–7.5)

The maps compare changes to the road system providing access to City Hospital between 2007 and 2010.

Overall, access was reorganised through the construction of two roundabouts and the replacement of six roadside bus stops with a bus station. Parking was separated into distinct areas for staff and the public, while Ring Road and City Road remained largely unchanged.

In 2007, the hospital was encircled by Ring Road and linked southwards to City Road by Hospital Road. Three bus stops stood on each side of Hospital Road. Immediately to the east was a single car park shared by staff and the public, and Hospital Road met City Road at a simple junction.

By 2010, roundabouts had been built at both ends of Hospital Road, with the southern one forming its junction with City Road. The roadside stops had been removed, and a bus station had been constructed to the west, with access from both roundabouts. The former shared car park was reserved for staff, while a larger public car park was created east of the hospital and connected to Ring Road.
