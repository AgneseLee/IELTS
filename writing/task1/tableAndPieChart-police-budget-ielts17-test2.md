## Final Integrated Version (7.0–7.5)

The table and pie charts show the sources of funding for the police budget in one area of Britain and how this money was spent in 2017 and 2018.

Overall, the total police budget increased slightly over the period. The national government remained the main source of income, while salaries comprised the largest share of expenditure in both years, despite a fall in their proportion.

In terms of income, funding from the national government rose marginally from £175.5m in 2017 to £177.8m in 2018. Local taxes recorded a more noticeable increase, climbing from £91.2m to £102.3m, whereas other sources, such as grants, rose only slightly from £38m to £38.5m.

Regarding expenditure, salaries for officers and staff still accounted for the lion's share, although their proportion fell from 75% to 69%. By contrast, the allocation for technology grew from 8% to 14%, while spending on buildings and transport remained stable at 17%.

## Summary of Corrections

### 语法 & 拼写

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `slightly changes occur` | `the total police budget increased slightly` |
| `which gain more 14.1m` | `increased by £13.9m` / `rose from £304.7m to £318.6m` |
| `the local taxes second` | `local taxes ranked second` / `local taxes recorded...` |
| `Salaries ... still rank first` | `salaries ... still accounted for the lion's share` |
| `maintained its original share in 17%` | `remained stable at 17%` |

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `transformation of police budget income and expense` | `sources of funding ... and how this money was spent` | `transformation` 不适合描述预算来源和支出分布 |
| `income and expense` | `income and expenditure` | IELTS 图表题更正式 |
| `this part` | `income` / `funding` | 指代模糊 |
| `Other sources like grants took the leftover` | `other sources, such as grants, rose only slightly` | `leftover` 口语且不精确 |
| `consumption aspects` | `expenditure` | 预算支出不用 consumption |
| `transformed from a mere 8% to a more significant 14%` | `grew from 8% to 14%` | 避免套词和夸张评价 |

### 结构改进

原稿把 introduction 和 overview 写在同一段，信息拥挤。修订版拆成四段：介绍、总览、收入来源、支出分配，更符合 Task 1 图表作文结构。
