## Revision Advice

### Task Achievement

- [TA] 第一段的主线 `welfare support → poverty reduction → social stability` 切题且完整。
- [TA] 第二段的资源配置逻辑写反了：`the government allocates more funding to other essentials` 表示教育和科技获得更多资金，本身不能证明福利导致 `resource misallocation`。应改为“过度福利支出挤占教育和科技预算”。
- [TA] `this advantage is short-lived`、`supposed benefits appear superficial` 和 `promote greater social inequality` 都过于绝对。福利设计不当可能削弱其收益，但这不等于正面作用必然短暂或虚假。
- [TA] `low labour participation` 与福利之间缺少关键解释：只有当福利设计削弱就业激励时，劳动参与率才可能下降。

### Coherence and Cohesion

- [CC] `once ... when ...` 在同一句中叠加两个时间/条件连接结构，使因果关系难以识别。应拆成“机会成本”和“就业激励”两条清楚的逻辑链。
- [CC] `That makes...` 指代范围模糊；应明确写成 `This can make the initial gains difficult to sustain.`。
- [CC] 结尾从生产率下降直接跳到社会不平等，缺少中间推理。按原有论点收束到 `pressure on public finances` 更连贯。

### Lexical Resource

- [LR] `make provision for sufficient funding for basic living` 搭配生硬，宜改为 `provide sufficient financial support to meet basic needs`。
- [LR] `throughout the society` 应写为 `across society`；泛指整个社会时通常不加定冠词。
- [LR] `long-term outcomes` 含义空泛，可直接写成 `long-term social stability`。
- [LR] `undesirable national productivity` 不自然，应写为 `reduced national productivity`。
- [LR] `promote greater social inequality` 中 `promote` 带有积极推动的色彩；表达负面加剧可用 `exacerbate social inequality`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- [GR] `One key strength is that its ability` 混用了 `is that...` 与 `is its ability...` 两种结构，应改为 `One key strength is its ability...`。
- [GR] `which make provision` 存在主谓不一致且先行词不清。可改为 `welfare provision, which provides...`。
- [GR] `issues ... offsets` 的主语是复数，谓语应为 `offset`。⚠ recurring：主谓一致仍是需要重点检查的问题。
- [GR] `they not only offsets ... but also ... promote` 的并列谓语既不一致，也没有保持正确形式，应写为 `they not only offset ... but also exacerbate...`。

## Summary of Corrections

### 审题错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| 教育和科技获得更多资金导致资源错配 | 过度福利支出挤占教育和科技预算 | 只有后者能说明福利支出的机会成本 |

## Final Integrated Version(142) (7.0–7.5)

Social welfare systems can bring substantial benefits by providing vulnerable groups with enough financial support to meet their basic needs. This protection not only reduces poverty across society but can also enhance social stability by preventing severe hardship and exclusion.

However, these gains may be undermined if welfare programmes are poorly designed or absorb an excessive share of public spending. Heavy welfare expenditure can divert funds away from other essential areas, such as education and technology, while overly generous unconditional benefits may weaken some recipients’ incentive to work. Lower labour force participation can then reduce national productivity and place greater pressure on public finances. Over time, these effects may offset the initial reduction in poverty and make the welfare system harder to sustain. Therefore, social welfare is most beneficial when it offers adequate protection without crowding out essential investment or discouraging employment.
