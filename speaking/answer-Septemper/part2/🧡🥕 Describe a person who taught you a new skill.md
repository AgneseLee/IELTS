## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a person who taught you a new skill**

> **You should say:**
> - Who this person was
> - What the skill was
> - How this person taught you
> - And explain how you felt about learning this skill

> Bank: B4 | Modules: mother / breathing-method / daily-practice

> Logic: sleep anxiety → mother’s breathing technique → five-minute trial → relief → less self-pressure

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally, you know. I would lie in bed overthinking my future and my career. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. Anyway, that's why I talked to my mother about what I was going through. She taught me <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">a simple breathing technique</mark>: breathe in for three, hold for five, and breathe out for three. At first, I was a bit skeptical about whether it would work. But she asked me to just try it for five minutes without judging the result. I did, and it turned out to be quite helpful. Somehow, I just calmed down and felt some relief.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
I was really grateful because she taught me patiently and didn't present it as an instant cure. Although it didn't fix everything right away, after practicing it a few times, I stopped feeling so anxious and frustrated. I realized I didn't need to push myself so hard and could just focus on one small thing at a time. Now, whenever I'm stressed out, I use this technique wherever I am. **It works like a little reset, almost like meditation, and it helps me ease up at least a bit.**
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
