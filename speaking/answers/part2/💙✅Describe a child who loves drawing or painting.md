## Cue Card

**Describe a child who loves drawing/painting**

> **You should say:**
> - Who he/she is
> - How/when you knew him/her
> - How often he/she draws/paints
> - And explain why you think he/she loves drawing/painting

> Bank: B1 | Modules: Mia / park-painting / creative-freedom

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Creativity & Innovation
- Reason: Mia changes familiar scenes through colour and imagined details to express how they feel to her.
- Secondary Ability: Learning Ability
- Reason: She draws several times a week, notices details, and calmly identifies what she wants to improve.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Mia, my eight-year-old niece, whom I have known since she was born.
- Background: She began drawing seriously after receiving a small watercolour set for her birthday.
- Main event: She paints several times a week and created an imaginative version of a park we visited.
- Ability shown: Creative experimentation supported by observation and regular practice.
- Reflection: She loves the freedom to choose colours, change reality, and tell a story without adult control.
