## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a place you have travelled to that you would like to recommend to others**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is
> - Where it is
> - What you saw and did there
> - And explain why you would like to recommend it to others

> Bank: B3 | Modules: Semporna / freediving

## 1. Band 7 Answer (minimum changes)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I'd like to recommend Semporna, a coastal area in Malaysia. About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future, my career, and whether AI might replace our jobs. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. So I asked my best friend Stella to go on a 10-day trip to Semporna with me. I wanted to take a break from my daily routine and relax a bit.

During the trip, we saw colorful coral reefs and schools of fish because we tried freediving for the first time. It was really fascinating. The underwater world seemed magical. The water was so clear that we could clearly see everything. We even saw a sea turtle moving slowly in front of us. We were really excited, but we kept a respectful distance because we didn't want to disturb the wildlife.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Seeing such beautiful natural scenery helped me forget about all the things that had been bothering me. I could actually feel some of my stress fading away. It was a completely different experience from my daily urban life, where I'm usually surrounded by buildings, traffic, and work.

Freediving also helped me step outside my <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">comfort zone</mark> without feeling overwhelmed. Since it was my first time, I had to learn how to control my breathing, follow safety instructions from our guide, and adjust to the underwater environment. At first, I was a little nervous, but each small success made me more <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">confident</mark>.

After the trip, my mental state was much better. Somehow, I also felt more confident dealing with difficulties at work and in life. So, I'd definitely recommend Semporna to others, especially people who feel stressed or tired of city life. It's a great place to enjoy nature, try something new, and just take a break from your daily routine.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 5. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

> 根据本轮提供的原回答节选及 Main mistakes 栏整理；节选未展示的表达仅来自错误汇总，不补写为完整原始回答。“可优化 / 可精简”不表示原表达必然有语法错误。

| 原表达 / 问题 | 最小修改 / 推荐表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| if AI gonna replace our jobs | whether AI might replace our jobs | gonna 前缺少 be；这里表达“是否会”，用 whether might 更贴合担忧的不确定性。 |
| schools of fishes | schools of fish | 这里 fish 表示多条鱼，通常单复数同形；fishes 可指不同种类的鱼。 |
| we can clearly saw | we could clearly see | 情态动词后用原形 see；回忆旅行经历用 could。 |
| clear + transparent | The water was so clear that we could clearly see everything. | 可精简：clear 与 transparent 在此意思重复，不是语法错误。 |
| some of my relief had been stress had been relieved | I could actually feel some of my stress fading away. | 口述自我修正导致结构混乱；也可简化为 some of my stress was relieved。 |
| adjusted my approach to adapt to | adjusted to the underwater environment | 可精简：原结构可以成立，但这里直接用 adjust to 就能表达适应环境。 |
