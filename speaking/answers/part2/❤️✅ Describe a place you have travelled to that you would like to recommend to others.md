## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a place you have travelled to that you would like to recommend to others**

> **You should say:**
> - What it is
> - Where it is
> - What you saw and did there
> - And explain why you would like to recommend it to others

> Bank: B3 | Modules: Semporna / freediving / sunrise

## 1. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I followed safety instructions, controlled my breathing, and adjusted calmly to freediving in an unfamiliar marine environment.

## 2. Story Bank

- Who/What: Semporna, a coastal destination in Sabah, Malaysia.
- Background: Stella and I spent the final week of our ten-day Malaysia trip there.
- Main event: We watched the sunrise, tried local food, and went freediving above coral reefs where we saw a sea turtle.
- Ability shown: I adapted to an unfamiliar activity and respected the instructor's safety guidance.
- Reflection: The trip made me more confident outdoors and more protective of the natural world.

## 3. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
I'd like to recommend Semporna, a coastal area in Sabah, Malaysia. Stella and I visited it last year during a ten-day trip and stayed for about a week. We tried local dishes, watched the sunrise, and took a boat to several small islands. The <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">highlight</mark> was freediving for the first time. Under the clear water, I saw colourful coral, schools of fish, and a sea turtle moving below us. I followed the instructor's safety guidance and kept a respectful distance from the wildlife. The scenery was beautiful, but the underwater experience made the place <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">unforgettable</mark>.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
At first, free diving felt <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">uncomfortable</mark> because I was not used to controlling my breathing underwater. I stayed close to the instructor, moved slowly, and stopped trying to go deeper. Once I <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">adjusted</mark> my approach, I relaxed and enjoyed the view.

The experience made me more <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">confident</mark> about unfamiliar outdoor activities. Seeing a sea turtle in its natural environment also made plastic waste feel like <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">a real threat</mark>, not an abstract issue.

I recommend Semporna because it <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">combines</mark> natural beauty with a chance to step outside your comfort zone. However, visitors should choose responsible guides and never touch coral or wildlife. For me, respecting the place turns an <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">exciting</mark> trip into a <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">meaningful</mark> one.
