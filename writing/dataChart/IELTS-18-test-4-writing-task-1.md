## Final Version (7.0–7.5)

The line graph illustrates the average monthly percentage change in the price of three metals throughout 2014.

Overall, there was a general downward trend in the rates of change across all three categories. While Nickel began with the highest growth rate in January, it declined dramatically, plummeting to the lowest point in June, before recovering to approximately 1% by December — a level comparable to Zinc and Copper. Similarly, the rates of change of Copper and Zinc also decelerated compared to their January levels.

Regarding Copper, its rate of change remained relatively stable at roughly 1% throughout most of the year, except for May and June when it experienced a slight drop.

In stark contrast, Nickel peaked at 6% in January but plummeted to -3% in June. Its rate of change then stabilised at around -1% between July and October before rebounding to approximately 1% by year end. Zinc's rate of change followed a broadly similar pattern, having peaked at 3% in February before declining to around -1% in the second half of the year and recovering to approximately 2% by December.

## Summary of Corrections

### 审题错误

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `prices changed` | `percentage change in the price` | y 轴是 % change vs 上月，非价格本身 |
| `price of Copper rose gradually` | `rate of change remained relatively stable` | 图表显示 Copper 全年约 1%，并非上升 |
| `continued to dip` (Nickel Jul–Oct) | `stabilised at around -1%` | 图表显示平稳，非继续下跌 |

### 语法 & 拼写

| 错误 | 修正 |
|---|---|
| `throught` | `throughout` |
| `Januaray` | `January` |
| `onced soar` | restructured → `having peaked at` |
| `became slower than` | `decelerated compared to`（`slowed than` 语法错误）|
| `throughout the most of` | `throughout most of` |
| `comaprable` | `comparable` |
| `January level` | `January levels` |
| `saw a dramatic decline and plummeted` | `declined dramatically, plummeting`（冗余）|

### 词汇误用

| 错误 | 修正 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `the rise rate` | `rate of change` | 非自然搭配 |
