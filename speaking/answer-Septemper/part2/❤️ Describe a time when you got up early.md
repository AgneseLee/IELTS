## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a time when you got up early**

> **You should say:**
> - When it was
> - What you did
> - Why you got up early
> - And how you felt about it

> Bank: B4 | Modules: sleeplessness / park-sunrise / remembered-advice

## 1. Band 7 Answer

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
Several months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my future and my career. I was worried about everything that was bothering me. One night, at around 2 or 3 a.m., I was still awake, and I felt like I probably wouldn't be able to sleep at all. So, at about 5 in the morning, I decided to get up. I brushed my teeth, washed my face, and went to a nearby park. At first, I felt really exhausted, both physically and mentally, because I hadn't slept. It was hard to concentrate, and my head was kind of messy. Then I simply sat on a bench and waited for the sunrise. The sky slowly and gradually got brighter while I was waiting. I could also hear birds singing around me.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
And surprisingly, some of my stress had been relieved somehow, and my mood had lifted a little. When the sun finally came up, I ended up remembering something my best friend Stella had told me before: “Slow is fast. Don't push yourself so hard.” I used to think I had to finish everything immediately and handle every challenge alone to prove that I was capable and strong enough. But that mindset really put a lot of pressure on me and made me even more anxious sometimes. But while I was watching the sunrise and listening to the sounds of nature, I felt a sense of peace. Although my problems didn't disappear, somehow they didn't seem as serious as before. Although I got up early because of sleeping badly, it turned out to be quite a peaceful experience. I was still tired, of course, but mentally, I felt relieved, calmer and more relaxed afterwards.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->

## 2. Core Ability Mapping

- Primary Ability: Adaptability
- Reason: I changed my routine after a sleepless night and reconsidered my expectations while watching the sunrise.

## 3. Story Bank

- Who/What: An early morning visit to a nearby park.
- Background: I could not sleep because I was worrying about my future and career.
- Main event: I got up at about 5 a.m., sat on a bench, and watched the sunrise.
- Ability shown: Adjusting my routine and my expectations of myself.
- Reflection: The quiet surroundings and Stella’s advice helped me feel calmer even though my problems remained.
