## Cue Card

**🧡 Describe a building you enjoy visiting (e.g. a library, a shopping mall, etc.)**

> **You should say:**
> - Where it is
> - How you knew it
> - What it looks like
> - And explain why you enjoy visiting it

> Bank: B7 | Modules: office-skyscraper / city-view / daily-comfort

## 1. Band 7 Answer (1:40–2:00)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
A building I enjoy visiting is an office skyscraper near my former workplace in Beijing. I first went there with Stella for a meeting. It has a glass exterior, a bright lobby and large windows overlooking the city. Inside, there are offices, cafés and shared meeting rooms. I especially enjoy the natural light and the view from the upper floors. After meetings, I'd sometimes stop for a coffee before heading back. Sitting there for a few minutes helped me clear my head and take a break from my busy daily routine.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
What I appreciate now is the practical comfort, although the building isn't perfect. The lifts get crowded, and some rooms feel stuffy because the windows don't open. Those details made me think about how the design could work better. I'd add better ventilation and somewhere outdoors where people could sit. Thinking about these changes helped me look beyond an impressive appearance and find practical solutions to ordinary problems. I still enjoy visiting because the light and view give me a welcome pause. But I've learned that a building should feel good to use, as well as look good from the street.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
