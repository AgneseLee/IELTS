## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a person you know who would like to choose a career in the medical field (e.g. a doctor, a nurse)**

> **You should say:**
> - When you knew him/her
> - When he/she started to think about that
> - What he/she would like to do
> - And explain why he/she would like to choose this career

> Bank: B8 | Modules: career motivation / counseling studies

## 1. Band 7 Answer (minimum changes)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
The person I'd like to talk about is Stella, one of my former colleagues. About one year ago, our company went through a layoff, and Stella suddenly received a much heavier workload. Then she began sleeping badly at night. She said she was worried about things like her future, her career, and whether AI might replace people's jobs. Things like that kept bothering her and really put a lot of pressure on her. So that's why she decided to resign from her job. Because of her experience with such a heavy mental burden and pressure, she realized how important people's mental health was. So she ended up taking some formal lessons in psychology and counseling. She decided that she would like to become a counselor, so that she could help people reduce their stress and live happier lives. Now she's taking some courses and learning some basic knowledge about counseling and psychology. She doesn't need to work overtime anymore, so she has more time to prepare for her new career.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
Her experience also reminded me that a career in the medical field isn't always about being a doctor or a nurse. It can also be about mental health. Helping people deal with things like insomnia and stress can also be really helpful. Her experience also made me wonder whether I had been putting too much pressure on myself. I used to think I had to finish everything immediately and handle every challenge on my own to prove that I was capable and strong enough. But that also put a lot of pressure on me and sometimes made me even more anxious. So Stella's career choice made me rethink that. Maybe sometimes I should leave more room and time for my own well-being instead of only focusing on my job.

## 6. Mistakes / Unnatural Expressions

> 根据本轮原回答和错误汇总整理；“可优化”表示自然度调整，不代表原表达必然有语法错误。

| 原表达 / 问题 | 最小修改 / 推荐表达 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| resign her job and quit | resign from her job | resign from 更常用；原来的及物用法也可成立。quit 在此重复。 |
| people's mental states was | people's mental health was | states 是复数，若保留应配 were；这里 mental health 更符合心理健康的含义。 |
| lessons about psychology | lessons in psychology | 可优化：in 常用来表示所学科目，about 并非语法错误。 |
| become a counseling | become a counselor | counselor 指从业者；counseling 指咨询活动。 |
| make them live happy | help them live happier lives | live happier lives 更自然；若用 happy 修饰 live，需改为副词 happily。 |
| taking and learning some basic knowledge | taking some courses and learning some basic knowledge | take 搭配 courses，不能与 learn 共用 knowledge 作宾语。 |
| that kind of things | those kinds of things / counseling and psychology | 可用 that kind of thing 或 those kinds of things；直接点明学科更清楚。 |
| remind me that whether | made me wonder whether | 表达“让我思考是否”用 make me wonder whether。 |
| choice on a career | career choice | 名词搭配 career choice 更自然。 |
| rethink of it | rethink that | rethink 直接接宾语，不加 of。 |
| leave more space and leave more room | leave more room and time | 可精简：space 与 room 在这里意思重复。 |
| that also puts a lot of pressure on me and make me | that also put a lot of pressure on me and sometimes made me | 此处回顾过去用 put / made；若描述现在，应为 puts / makes。 |
