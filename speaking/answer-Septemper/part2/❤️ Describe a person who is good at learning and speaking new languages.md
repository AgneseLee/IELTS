## Cue Card

**❤️ Describe a person who is good at learning and speaking new languages**

> **You should say:**
> - How you got to know him/her
> - How he/she learns a new language
> - What languages he/she can speak
> - And explain how you feel about him/her

> Bank: B1 | Modules: daily-conversations / language-exchange / recording-and-review

## 1. Band 7 Answer (用户提供的完整版本)

<!-- NARRATIVE_START -->
About three months ago, I had trouble falling asleep. I just couldn't fall asleep naturally. I would lie in bed but couldn't stop thinking about my life, my future, and my career. I was basically worried about everything that was bothering me. After several nights of sleeping badly, I felt like I couldn't live like that anymore. So that's why I talked to my best friend Stella, who is really good at learning and speaking new languages. She's good at English, and she can also hold conversations in Japanese. She comforted me by telling me how she learned those two languages and how she overcame difficulties during the learning process. She keeps practicing Japanese in daily conversations, and sometimes she<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;"> does language exchanges online</mark> with foreigners. Besides that, she also records short dialogues and conversations using new expressions. Then she replays those recordings until she fixes the mistakes she made. That's how she learned those two languages.
<!-- NARRATIVE_END -->

<!-- REFLECTION_START -->
She taught me that learning is a slow process, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">and the same applies to life and work. </mark>Slowing down helps us focus on the things we can control and realize what matters most at the moment, so that we can focus our efforts without distractions. What she taught me left a lasting impression on me. That night, I even slept better thanks to what she said. Some of my stress was relieved, and I felt a bit more relaxed. So I really admire not only her ability to learn languages, but also her<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;"> patient attitude</mark> towards learning.
<!-- REFLECTION_END -->
